[Update] Event "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament" Now Open!

[Updated on 2025/04/28]

We have made the following adjustments to the "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament" event.

These will be applied starting from "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament ~Flame Festival~" which begins on 2025/04/28.

●Battle Adjustments

○The resistance to elements other than the effective element of the event has been significantly increased, making it harder to deal damage with attacks using elements other than the effective element

Example: In "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament ~Flame Festival~", it will be harder to deal damage with non-Fire element attacks

○Adjustments have been made so that enemies deal increased damage to characters with elements other than the effective element as their preferred element

Example: In "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament ~Flame Festival~", characters with preferred elements other than Fire will take more damage from enemy attacks than before

○The enemies appearing in battle have been changed (Along with this change, some enemy stats such as defense have been adjusted to increase.)

○The order of effect panels at the start of battle has been changed

●Ranking Reset

○Along with the battle adjustments, the rankings for each element of "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament" held in the past have been reset (With this change, emblem progress will be reset, but acquisition status will be maintained.)

The ranking-based permanent event "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament" has been partially renewed!

Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament is a special battle where Total Damage becomes your score!

Scores are announced in rankings, and based on your rank, you can GET various rewards such as Lodestar Gems, Sacred Orbs, and Grand Martial Arts Tournament Coins!

Grand Martial Arts Tournament Coins can be exchanged for SSR Memories and Memory Fragments in the event exchange shop!

Additionally, by reaching certain Total Damage thresholds, you can also obtain Emblems!

Take on the Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament and GET ranking rewards, exchange shop rewards, and emblems!

What is the Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament

A martial arts tournament hosted by the adventurer Roman.

Adventurers and strong warriors from across the country challenge themselves to defeat monsters and compete in martial prowess.

The tournament draws enormous attention, and its champion is widely celebrated.

How to Play Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament

Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament is a special battle where the amount of HP you deplete from enemies becomes your score!

Your acquired score is displayed in ranking format, and you can obtain various rewards based on your rank.

There are 3 difficulty levels, and at the highest difficulty, enemies with infinite HP will appear, allowing you to attack more within the specified turns and increase your score!

Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament will be regularly updated, and rankings will be reset!

Also, the effective element for battles will change with each update.

※There may be cases where a Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament with the same effective element as a previously held tournament is held again. In such cases, your past maximum score will be carried over.

※Exchange status in the exchange shop and the number of Grand Martial Arts Tournament Coins you possess will be carried over even after renewal or updates.

About Ranking Rewards

You can obtain various rewards based on your ranking in the Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" after logging in following the end of the event period for each effective element of the Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will be placed in the same rank.

Example: If 3 users have the 10th place score, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.
