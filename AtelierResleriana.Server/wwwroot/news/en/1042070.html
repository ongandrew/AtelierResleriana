Letter from the Development Team vol.14

Thank you very much for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

This is Hosoi, Producer of "Atelier Resleriana".

On 2024/09/27, we announced "Crimson Alchemist and White Guardian ~Atelier Resleriana~" from the "Atelier" series. This title is set on the same Lanterna continent as "Resleriana," and will expand the world of "Resleriana" further.

A limited-time LOGIN BONUS commemorating the announced release of "Crimson Alchemist and White Guardian" is scheduled to begin after the maintenance on 2024/11/08, so please look forward to it.

With the ongoing "1st Anniversary Star Festival LEGEND FES Irreplaceable Things," we have now delivered all the content planned for the 1st anniversary. We hope you enjoy everything we've prepared for "Resleriana's" 1st anniversary.

The Main Story Part 2, which began in 2024/05, is now approaching its core revelations.

After the maintenance on 2024/11/08, Main Story Chapter 19 Part 2 will begin, so please look forward to it.

Additionally, a new format story event "Flowers Blooming in Thirst" will begin after the maintenance on 2024/11/08. This event focuses on "Resleriana" original characters and features a new approach where your choices during the story will change subsequent story developments. Please give it a try.

We would also like to announce updates that will make the game more accessible.

※This announcement includes content that was shared during the livestream on 2024/11/07.

●Adjustments to the Roman Grand Fighting Tournament

●Other Updates

▼Adjustments to the Roman Grand Fighting Tournament

〇Addition of Event Missions

We will add event mission content and rewards. With these adjustments, you will be able to obtain up to an additional 50 Bottled Mana and 300 Grand Fighting Tournament Coins.

○Addition of Rewards to Event Exchange

We will add a new SSR Memoria "Warrior Guided by Starlight" to the event exchange. This Memoria can only be obtained from the "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament," so please aim to acquire it.

●SSR "Warrior Guided by Starlight"

○Battle Adjustments

We will adjust some battle content in the "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament" event. We will lower the resistance values for effective attributes and adjust some enemy actions to make battles easier.

○Start of Wish Ticket Sales and End of Paid-Only Wishes

For the "◯◯ Festival Wish LEGEND FES" that runs alongside each attribute's Roman Grand Fighting Tournament, we will begin selling Wish ticket sets that can be purchased 2 times only for 1,500 Star Quartz. For details, please check the in-game announcement when the Wish begins.

With this adjustment, we will end the "◯◯ Festival Wish LEGEND FES [Paid-Only]" and will only run the "◯◯ Festival Wish LEGEND FES" in the future.

These adjustments will be implemented from the "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament ~Fire Festival~" scheduled to begin on 2024/11/11 12:00. For details, please check the in-game announcements that will be posted on that day.

▼Other Updates

○"Batch Synthesis" Feature

We will add a feature that allows you to perform multiple syntheses at once for a selected number of times in normal synthesis, where you arrange characters and synthesis materials. Many of you have requested this feature. Like "Quick Synthesis," you will be able to perform up to 100 syntheses at once.

○"Level Cap Release" Feature

We will add a "Level Cap Release" feature that allows you to raise the level cap by using character-specific items. If you have unlocked Growboard XVI and have a level cap of 90, you will be able to raise the level cap to 100.

To unlock the cap, you will need "Particles of Hope" or "Fruits of Hope". These items can be obtained from event rewards and other sources. "Particles of Hope" can be obtained from event mission rewards and the event exchange in the "Flowers Blooming in Thirst" event scheduled to begin after the maintenance on 2024/11/08, so please aim to acquire them.

"Fruits of Hope" will be available to obtain at a later date.

Unlike the previous level cap increases through Growboards, the levels unlocked and items used with this newly added "Level Cap Release" feature will not be reset when performing a reset from Home > Character > Training > Reset, so please use them on your favorite characters.

To commemorate the addition of this feature, we will present all players with 2 Particles of Hope.

Details such as the distribution period will be announced in a future in-game notice, so please check there.

○Addition of Floors to "Tower of Four Elements"

We will add floors 41-45 to the Secret Quest "Tower of Four Elements". By clearing floor 45, you can also obtain the aforementioned "Particles of Hope," so those confident in their abilities should definitely give it a try.

○Permanent Addition of "All Colors" Tab to Light Orb Quests

The All Colors Light Orb Quest, where all light orbs drop, which was available for a limited time from after the 2024/09/19 maintenance to 2024/10/01 11:59, will become permanent. You can obtain all colors of light orbs evenly.

This concludes the content we are announcing this time. In addition to the above, we are making improvements and considerations based on user feedback to make "Resleriana" more enjoyable.

We will continue to strive to grow this game into a better one together with our users, so please continue to support "Atelier Resleriana."
