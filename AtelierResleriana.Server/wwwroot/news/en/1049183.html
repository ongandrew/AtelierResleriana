▼Break System Adjustments

Based on the current battle environment, we have made adjustments to the Break system in battles.

When an enemy was in a Break state, their turn order was delayed, and they received increased damage until their Break gauge recovered. Considering the current battle environment, we discussed within the development team whether the benefits of Break were sufficient, and have adjusted it so that enemies receive even more increased damage.

We will continue to consider adjustments in the future to deliver a more exciting battle experience.

▼January Bond Story Additions
