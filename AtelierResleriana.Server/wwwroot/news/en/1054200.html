▼Event "Dreaming Special Moments with You" now live

The event "Dreaming Special Moments with You" has been live since the end of maintenance on 2025/02/14.

This event introduces a new feature called "Presents." With "Presents," you can give synthesized items to Plachta as gifts. Please check the in-game announcement for details.

Additionally, in the simultaneously running "VALENTINE 2025 Plachta LEGEND FES," ★3 Plachta【Curious Theorist】 from "Atelier Sophie 2: The Alchemist of the Mysterious Dream" has been newly added, so please welcome her.

▼"★3 Character Select Guaranteed Wish [Magic]" and "SSR Memoria Select Guaranteed Wish [Magic]" now available

Similarly, since the end of maintenance on 2025/02/14, we have launched the "★3 Character Select Guaranteed Wish [Magic]" and "SSR Memoria Select Guaranteed Wish [Magic]."

These are step-up Wishes from STEP 1 to 4, and at STEP 4, you can make a free "single Wish guaranteed to obtain your selected character or memoria."

This time, during the event period, the STEP will reset at the daily login reset time, and you can wish up to 3 full cycles during the event period. Please see the in-game announcement for details.

▼Limited-time addition of "Memory Radiance [1.5 Anniversary]"

Similarly, since the end of maintenance on 2025/02/14, we have added "Memory Radiance [1.5 Anniversary]" to the shop for a limited time to celebrate the 1.5 Anniversary Eve Campaign.

In this shop, you can welcome only original "Atelier Resleriana" characters that appeared in Wishes up until the 1st anniversary.

The Memory Radiance [1.5 Anniversary] consumed when using this shop has been sent to all users via mail, so please welcome your favorite characters.

▼"1.5 Anniversary Special Wish" coming soon

The "1.5 Anniversary Special Wish" will be available from 2025/02/21 12:00.

This Wish can be made up to 200 times for free. It can only be made using special Wish tickets, and you can obtain up to 200 Wish tickets through login bonuses and mission rewards during the period. Please check the in-game announcement that will be posted on that day for details.

▼Event schedule until the 1.5 anniversary

In addition to the "1.5 Anniversary Special Wish," we plan to hold events and Wishes as shown above.

In Main Story Chapter 23, Resna and friends will begin a new journey to the Continent of Ends. Also, special enemies with new abilities will appear, so please try to progress through the Main Story up to Chapter 22.

Additionally, we will begin the 1.5 Anniversary event with the ver2.0.0 update scheduled for 2025/03/06. We will be implementing various campaigns and new features to commemorate the 1.5 Anniversary, so please look forward to further announcements.

Also, with the ver2.0.0 update, we will update the following lineup. We have posted advance notice about this after the maintenance today, 2025/02/14, so please check the in-game announcement for details.
