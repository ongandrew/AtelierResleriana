The "Super Growth! All Quest Drop Amount +100% Campaign" is now live!

The item drop amount in all material quests will increase by 100%!

Please make good use of this for your synthesis and growth needs!

※ Content that does not consume stamina and first-time clear rewards are not included in this campaign.

※ Cole obtained from material quests is not included in this campaign.

※ For Score Battles, synthesis materials obtained from drop rewards when clearing with rank C to SS will be increased by 100%.
