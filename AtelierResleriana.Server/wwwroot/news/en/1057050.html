[Update 2025/03/06 23:00]

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

After the maintenance on 2025/03/06, we have confirmed that PC (Steam®) version users are experiencing an issue where settings in Home > Menu > Options and new notification icons are being reset to default values on their first launch.

This only occurs on the first launch, so we kindly ask that you reconfigure your options settings when this happens.

We are working to prevent this from occurring in future updates.

We sincerely apologize to all PC (Steam®) version users for any inconvenience caused.

Fixed Issues

●[Update]Issue with PC (Steam®) version's user agreement

○Fixed an issue where part of the PC (Steam®) version's user agreement was incorrectly showing an outdated version

●Display issue when purchasing certain sales packs

○Fixed an issue where the dialog box displayed when purchasing the "1.5 Anniversary Bonus Star Guidance Stone Sale" incorrectly showed "New Year's Pack" as the sale name (※This was only a display issue and purchases were processed correctly. The fix may take some time to apply depending on your environment.)

●Issue with battle hints in certain Score Battles

○Fixed an issue where battle hints for the enemy "Malicious Decoy" were not displaying in Score Battle "Imprisoned Vengeful Spirits - 2" in Home > Quest > Material Quest > Score Battle > Chapter 23

●Progress counting issue with certain achievements and emblems

○Fixed an issue where the following achievements and emblems were incorrectly counting Legend Challenge clears towards their progress (※As previously announced, we will not reset progress that was incorrectly added from Legend Challenges completed before this fix.)

■Achievement "Obtain ○ SS Score Ranks"

■Emblem "Score Battle Master"

●Issue with SSR Memoria acquisition animation under specific conditions

○Fixed an issue where when obtaining 2 or more SSR Memoria in a 10x Wish, the acquisition animation for the second and subsequent SSR Memoria would appear blurry

●Display issue with bonus effect screen

○Fixed an issue where during events with Memoria bonuses, viewing the bonus effect screen in battle party formation for battles with special effects could sometimes result in unintended displays

●Issue with Light Orb Quest difficulty selection

○Fixed an issue in Light Orb Quests where after clearing "Difficulty: I" in the "All Colors" tab, switching to other color tabs would maintain the "Difficulty: I" selection instead of showing the highest cleared difficulty

●Display issue with Geron [Beastly Fencer]'s bond story

○Fixed an issue where dialogue was not displaying correctly in Episode 2 of Geron [Beastly Fencer]'s bond story

Issues Under Investigation

●[Update]Issue with "Rerun Polar Night Battle - Time of Thirst LEGEND FES"

○"Rerun Polar Night Battle - Time of Thirst LEGEND FES" does not start when obtaining the Rerun Polar Night Battle - Time of Thirst Wish Ticket from guide missions (※In a future update, this will be fixed to start when clearing Story Chapter 21-53 "The Reward for the Request". Currently, "Rerun Polar Night Battle - Time of Thirst LEGEND FES" starts when clearing Story Chapter 21-60 "Never Forget". Rerun Polar Night Battle - Time of Thirst Wish Tickets received from guide missions before this point can be used during the applicable Wish period.)

●Issue with Grow Board unlock conditions not being removed

○After the maintenance on 2025/03/06, Grow Board unlock conditions are not properly removed and boards up to XVI are not unlocked from the start (※Currently, boards up to XVI can be unlocked by meeting the unlock conditions shown when tapping the locked unlock button in the Grow Board screen. A temporary fix for this issue is planned for the data update on 03/17.)

●Display issue when synthesizing with rental characters

○When synthesizing using characters from Popular Formations, three gift colors are shown when selecting the final synthesis material (※This is only a display issue. Only synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the second character in the formation are valid.)

●Issue with "Conquer! Consecutive Battle β version" event

○The rental party help text is displaying in the "Conquer! Consecutive Battle β version" event (※There are no quests that allow the use of rental parties in the "Conquer! Consecutive Battle β version" event.)

●Issue with "Challenge Battle Quvarelga" event

○The rental party help text is displaying in the "Challenge Battle Quvarelga" event (※There are no quests that allow the use of rental parties in the "Challenge Battle Quvarelga" event.)

●Display issue with Memories of Brilliance [1.5 Anniversary] shop screen

○The text "Until update" appears in the bottom right of the Home > Shop > Limited Time > Memories of Brilliance [1.5 Anniversary] screen (※There will be no lineup updates during the event period. The displayed time shows the remaining time until the shop ends.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○The app may force close when using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using high-speed skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○The link for post-paid billing is not displayed for the Daily Pass on Android devices

●Issue with movie playback on certain Android devices

○The following issues may occur when playing movies on certain Android devices:

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering

■Audio desync

■App may force close when replaying movies repeatedly

●Display issue with mail collection status

○The collected status in mail does not update after collecting characters, Memoria, or character pieces (※This is only a display issue. Rewards are properly received.)

●Behavior issue in certain battles

○The app may force close at certain timings in Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request - 3" (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Issue with video ad viewing on certain devices

○The app may force close when viewing video ads with certain environment settings on specific devices (※Please be assured that view counts are not reduced. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.
