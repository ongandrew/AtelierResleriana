Item Battle Event "Citrine's Challenge: The Jack of All Trades" is now live!

The Item Battle Event is a special Atelier-style battle event where you can challenge battles with massively increased item gauge recovery speed and unlimited item uses.

Clear Event Score Battles to obtain event-limited synthesis materials and recipes, and clear Event Boss Challenges to earn event-limited emblems!

Additionally, Battle Coins obtained from Event Score Battles can be exchanged in the shop for items such as Star Guidance Stones and UR weapons!

※The "massively increased item gauge recovery speed" in this event increases the item gauge recovery that occurs when allies take actions. Item gauge recovery from ally skills is not increased.
