Wish Details

The "Season 2 Launch! Bepp [Mourning Blades] LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now available!

The 3★ character Bepp and Memoria "Blade of Mourning" make their debut!

This is a step-up Wish with STEP 1-3, where STEP 3 allows you to do a free "10x Wish"!

STEP 1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Star Quartz

STEP 2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Star Quartz

STEP 3

Free 10x Wish

※You can complete STEP 1-3 up to 5 times.

※STEP 3's "10x Wish" does not award LEGEND Medals.

※After reaching STEP 3, you will return to STEP 1.

※During the event period, obtaining a duplicate featured character will award 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Brilliants.

Feature Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select which featured character or memoria to boost from Wish > Select Feature.

Don't miss this chance to awaken your newly obtained Bepp [Mourning Blades]!

※For mission details, please check Home > Missions > Events > Bepp [Mourning Blades] Release Celebration Mission.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You will receive LEGEND Medals when using Star Quartz to Wish.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for 3★ characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters/Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters released after 2025/03/06 can only be exchanged for with LEGEND Medals during their Wish period. Characters released before that date can be exchanged for at any time.

※Exchangeable SSR memoria include the new featured SSR memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memoria with memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※LEGEND Medal rewards are as follows:

●STEP 1 and STEP 2 10x Wish: 8 medals

※The free 10x Wish from STEP 3 does not award LEGEND Medals.
