Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or memoria to increase its appearance rate. The selected target will have a 1% appearance rate, while unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% appearance rate.

You can select the featured character or memoria you want to increase the appearance rate for in Wish > Featured Selection.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Begins! Holy Warrior Mother Janne LEGEND FES exclusive) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab! Each set can be purchased twice with either Lodestar Gems×1,500 or LEGEND Medals×25.

Each Wish Ticket can be used for 1 Wish.

※LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained when using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Begins! Holy Warrior Mother Janne LEGEND FES exclusive) cannot be used for any Wish other than "Season 2 Begins! Holy Warrior Mother Janne LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets will expire after the usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals by making Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. They can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets in the Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debut after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during their featured Wish period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged permanently.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria include the newly featured SSR Memoria in the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
