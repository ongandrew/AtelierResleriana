LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when you use Lodestar Gems to make a Wish.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character/Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be exchanged with LEGEND Medals only during the Wish event period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include the newly added featured SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are part of the memoria bonus.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●STEP 1, STEP 2 10x Wish: 8 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish in STEP 3.
