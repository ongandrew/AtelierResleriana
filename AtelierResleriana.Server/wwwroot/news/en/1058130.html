Wish Overview

"Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES'" is now available!

A new ★3 character, Resna in a new outfit after one year of growth and the Memoria "Single-minded Devotion" have been added!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES' exclusive) is now available in the shop!

※During the event period, if you welcome a featured character that you already have, you will receive 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Crystals.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can choose one character or Memoria from the featured units to increase their appearance rate. The chosen target will have a 1% appearance rate, while the unchosen targets will equally share a 0.5% appearance rate.

You can select your preferred featured character and featured Memoria from Wish > Target Selection.

Resna【Rise Above Fate】Release Celebration Missions

To celebrate the release of Resna【Rise Above Fate】, limited-time "Resna【Rise Above Fate】Release Celebration Missions" are now available!

Based on the number of times you Wish on "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES'" or "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES' [Paid Only]", you can earn up to 445 Resna【Rise Above Fate】Character Pieces! Other items are also available as rewards.

This is your chance to immediately awaken your newly welcomed Resna【Rise Above Fate】! Don't miss it!

※Missions will end at 2025/03/28 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the end date for "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES'" and "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES' [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home > Missions > Events > Resna【Rise Above Fate】Release Celebration Missions.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES' exclusive) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab, purchasable up to 2 times each with 1,500 Lodestar Gems or 25 LEGEND Medals!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

Additionally, "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna Wish Ticket'" has been temporarily added to Home > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket tab.

※When using Wish Tickets, you will not receive LEGEND Medals.

※Wish Tickets from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES' exclusive) cannot be used for any Wish other than "Season 2 Begins! 'Those Who Defy Fate - Resna LEGEND FES'".

※Wish Tickets will expire after the usage period.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When making a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you will receive LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters/Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab. They can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets from Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters released after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memorias include newly featured SSR Memorias from ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias that provide Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 Medal

●10 Wishes: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
