Wish Details

"Season 2 Begins! One Who Stands Against Fate Resna LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now available!

★3 character Resna, who has grown and wears new attire after a year, and Memoria "Single-Minded" are now available!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 3, and at STEP 3, you can make a "10x Wish" for free!

STEP1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP3

Free 10x Wish

※You can complete STEP 1-3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when making a "10x Wish" at STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, you will return to STEP 1 again.

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character that you already have, you will receive 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Crystals.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one of the featured characters or Memoria to increase its appearance rate. The selected target will have a 1% appearance rate, and non-selected targets will equally share a 0.5% appearance rate.

You can select the featured character and Memoria you want to increase the appearance rate for in Wish > Featured Selection.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters released after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. ★3 characters released before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria include the newly released featured SSR Memoria from the ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are part of the Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●STEP 1 and STEP 2 10x Wish: 8 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish at STEP 3.
