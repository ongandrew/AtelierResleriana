Letter from the Development Team vol.20

Thank you very much for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

This is Hosoi, the producer of "Atelier Resleriana".

"Resleri" has officially reached its 1.5-year anniversary today, 2025/03/23. I would like to express my sincere gratitude once again.

To provide you with even more enjoyment, I would like to inform you about the update content implemented today, 2025/03/23, as well as content scheduled to be added from 2025/03/26.


▼Events currently taking place from 2025/03/23

○ "1.5-Year Anniversary Day Commemoration LOGIN BONUS" ongoing

We are holding the "1.5-Year Anniversary Day Commemoration LOGIN BONUS" where you can receive 3,000 free Lodestar Gems. By logging in for 1 day during the event period, you can obtain 3,000 free Lodestar Gems, so please be sure to log in and claim them.

○ "Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES" ongoing

We are holding the "Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES".

In this Wish, the SSR Memoria What Awaits Beyond the Sea has made its debut. When this Memoria is unlocked to Rank V, its ability effect becomes "When self is an Attacker, Critical Damage +100% and Critical Rate +10%". Furthermore, it has higher physical attack than magical attack stats, making it a Memoria that works well when using physical Attackers.


▼Content scheduled to be added from 2025/03/26

○ Main Story Chapter 26 addition

We will be adding Main Story Chapter 26. Along with this, mission content and rewards for the ongoing "Main Story Support Mission" will also be added. This will be the final mission addition during this period, so please be sure to take on the challenge during the event.

In Chapter 26, Resna, Valeria, and Alvina will finally meet. Please look forward to seeing where the story woven by these three distinctive protagonists will lead.

○ Addition of bond story for Alvina [Dream of Hero]

We will be adding a bond story to Alvina [Dream of Hero].

In this story, you can enjoy a "bond" with Alvina as a grown Resna. For Alvina, who has served as the nation's guide in accordance with her destiny, what thoughts will meeting Resna, who has traveled the outside world, stir in her? The story depicts Alvina's own feelings from this perspective.

With the update on 2025/03/06, we have made improvements so that even if you haven't welcomed the character, you can unlock the first episode of the bond story by consuming Friendship Keys, which can be obtained from Weekly Missions and other sources. Please enjoy!


That concludes the information we have to share with you this time.

Once again, I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude that "Resleri" has reached its 1.5-year anniversary.

We will continue to make improvements and considerations to ensure that you can enjoy "Resleri" even more as we aim for the 2-year anniversary on 2025/09/23. In particular, we are planning to implement updates related to events, such as new battle event formats, so we hope you look forward to them.

We appreciate your continued support for "Atelier Resleriana".


Producer

Junzo Hosoi (KOEI TECMO GAMES CO., LTD.)
