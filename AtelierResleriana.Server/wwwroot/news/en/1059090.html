Wish Overview

"Season 2 Launch! Memory Seeker Valeria LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now live!

★3 character Valeria, who has grown over the past year and wears a new outfit and Memoria "With My Own Hands" have been added!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP1 to STEP3, and at STEP3, you can do a "10x Wish" for free!

STEP1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP3

Free 10x Wish

※You can complete STEP1-3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when performing a "10x Wish" at STEP3.

※After reaching STEP3, you will return to STEP1 again.

※During the event period, if you welcome a pickup character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Shards ×50.

Pickup Selection

In this Wish, you can select one of the pickup characters or memorias to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a drop rate of 1%, and the unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

The pickup character and pickup memoria that you want to increase the drop rate for can be selected from Wish > Target Pickup.

Valeria [Get Over Limit] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate the debut of Valeria [Get Over Limit], we are holding the limited-time Valeria [Get Over Limit] Debut Celebration Mission!

Based on the number of times you Wish on "Season 2 Launch! Memory Seeker Valeria LEGEND FES" or "Season 2 Launch! Memory Seeker Valeria LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Valeria [Get Over Limit] pieces! Other items can also be obtained.

This is your chance to immediately awaken Valeria [Get Over Limit] after welcoming her! Don't miss it!

※Missions will end on 2025/04/01 11:59.

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Missions > Events > Valeria [Get Over Limit] Debut Celebration Mission.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can earn LEGEND Medals when you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. Characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added pickup SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can earn is as follows:

●STEP1, STEP2 10x Wish: 8 Medals

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish at STEP3.
