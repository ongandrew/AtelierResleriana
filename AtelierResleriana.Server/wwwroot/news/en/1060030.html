List of Fixed Issues

●Tutorial issue with orders in Season 2

○We have fixed the following issues with the order tutorial that occurs after completing Story Chapter 26-10 "Welcome!":

■Tutorial sometimes not progressing properly

■Tutorial pie could sometimes be received one extra time

●Issue where equipment was not properly reflected during party formation under specific conditions

○We have fixed an issue where, when changing characters in the party screen, if you filtered out the selected character, then replaced them with another character, and then returned to the original character and confirmed the formation, the equipment individually set in the character details screen would not be reflected

●Display issue when unlocking abilities

○We have fixed a display issue that occurred when evolving all skills of a character awakened to ★6 and unlocking their third ability

●Photo mode issue with Valeria [Get Over Limit]

○We have fixed an issue where expression 11 in photo mode for Valeria [Get Over Limit] displayed an unintended expression

○When performing synthesis by renting a character from popular formations, the gift color displayed when selecting the final synthesis material shows 3 colors (※This is only a display issue; only the synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the character in the second position are effective.)

○The app may force close when using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices:

■Audio desynchronization

■The app may force close when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with receipt status in mail

○Even after receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail does not change to "received" (※This is only a display issue; rewards are properly received.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3," the app may force close at specific timings (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Video ad viewing issue on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may force close when viewing video ads (※Please be assured that viewable counts will not decrease. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)
