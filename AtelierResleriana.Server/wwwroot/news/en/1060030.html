List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Update] An issue where progression becomes impossible in certain event stories

○There are cases where playing the event story "I Must Stop It!" in the "Reminiscent Alchemist and Blue Conspiracy" event may cause progression to become impossible (※This issue can be avoided by continuously tapping the screen immediately after the story begins to skip through the initial scene of Yumia and Valeria running, and the next scene where Yumia appears in close-up.)

●[Updated] Display issues with certain synthesis materials

○For the following synthesis materials, the display of obtainable locations is not appropriate when there are locked battles

■Forest Moody

■Wind Mill

■Punk Cracker

■Goldfinch Chirp

■Breeze Stone

■Rock Fish

■Mountain Spring Water

■What Is This

■Crystal Sea Urchin

■[04/16 Addition] Lottery Ore

■[04/16 Addition] Cutlass

■[04/16 Addition] Hunter's Claw

●Display issues with character details on specific screens

○When accessing the character details screen by tapping a character icon in Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates, characters are supposed to display with their maximum enhancement status, but for characters that can be awakened to ★6, skills are displayed with pre-evolution performance, and details of their third ability cannot be viewed

●Display issues when synthesizing with rental characters

○When synthesizing with characters rented from popular formations, three gift colors are displayed during the process of selecting the final synthesis material (※This is only a display issue; only the synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the second character in the formation will be effective.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to forcibly close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the post-payment link is not displayed in the Daily Pass

●Issues with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur when playing movies on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■Forcible app closure when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issues with receive status in mail

○When receiving characters, memorias, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail doesn't change to "received" (※This is only a display issue; rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issues in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3," the app may forcibly close at specific timings (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may help improve this issue.)

●Issues when watching video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, watching video ads may cause the app to forcibly close (※Please rest assured that the viewable count will not decrease. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may help improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.
