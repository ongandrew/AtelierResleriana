[2025/04/14 Update] Regarding Currently Known Issues

[2025/04/14 Update]

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

We have added the following issue to our list of known bugs:

●Display issue in the event exchange shop for "Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 2"

List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Update]Display issue in the event exchange shop for "Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 2"

○When accessing the event exchange shop for "Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 2", the logo displayed at the top of the screen shows "Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 1" (This issue is scheduled to be fixed in the data update on 2025/04/16.)

●Display issue with certain synthesis materials

○For the following synthesis materials, when there are locked battles in the obtainable locations, the display is not showing correctly

■Forest Moody

■Wind Mill

■Puncracka

■Canary's Chirp

■Breeze Stone

■Rock Fish

■Mountain Spring Water

■What's This

■Crystal Sea Urchin

●Display issue with certain character details in specific screens

○When accessing character details by tapping character icons in Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates, while character stats are shown at maximum enhancement, for characters that can be awakened to ★6, skills are displayed with pre-evolution performance and the third ability details cannot be viewed

●Display issue during synthesis with rental characters

○When synthesizing with a character rented from popular formations, three gift colors are displayed when selecting the final synthesis material (※This is only a display issue; only synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the second character in the formation will be effective.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○Using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using high-speed skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the link for post-payment options is not displayed in the Daily Pass

●Movie playback issues on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur when playing movies on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Frame rate drops

■Audio desynchronization

■App force closes when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with mail collection status

○When collecting characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display does not change to "collected" in the mail (※This is only a display issue; rewards are properly received.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3", the app may force close at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Ad viewing issues on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may force close when watching video ads (※Please note that viewing attempts are not counted. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused.
