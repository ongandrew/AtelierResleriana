Wish Overview

"Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES" is now available!

Yumia from "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~" makes her debut!

Additionally, the new Memoria "To Know the Truth" is also available!

Furthermore, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES) is now available in the shop!

※During the event period, when you recruit a featured character you already own, you will receive Character Pieces×100 and Wish Crystals×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The drop rate for your selected target will be 1%, while unselected targets will equally share a total drop rate of 0.5%.

You can select which featured character and featured Memoria you want to have an increased drop rate from Wish > Select Featured.

Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Release Celebration Missions

To celebrate the release of Yumia [Seeker of Memories], we're holding the limited-time "Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Release Celebration Missions"!

Based on the number of times you make a Wish on "Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES" or "Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can earn up to Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Pieces×445 as well as other items!

Don't miss this chance to immediately awaken your recruited Yumia [Seeker of Memories]!

※The missions will end on 2025/04/07 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the end date of "Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES" and "Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home screen > Missions > Event > Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Release Celebration Missions.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab, purchasable up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems×1,500 and LEGEND Medals×25!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes made with Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES) can only be used for "Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets will disappear after their expiration date.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you'll receive LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. They can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets in the Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters released after 2025/03/06 can be recruited through LEGEND Medal exchange only during their featured Wish period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly added featured SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and the SSR Memoria eligible for Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals received is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 Medal

●10x Wish: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes made with Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Yumia [Seeker of Memories] to ★5, you can gain inspiration for an SSR equipment recipe that is only effective when equipped by Yumia [Seeker of Memories].

Aim to awaken her to ★5 by challenging the limited-time "Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Release Celebration Missions" and the Piece Quests!

※For details on the SSR equipment recipe that can be inspired, please check Home screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for gaining recipe inspiration.
