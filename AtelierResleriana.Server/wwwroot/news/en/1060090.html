▼Current Event Details

○Major Event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" Now Live

We are currently hosting the major event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy."

This event begins with Alvina and her companions being asked to investigate a small falling star on the outskirts of the Nova Holy Kingdom. They meet Yumia and her companions, alchemists from a foreign land, at the investigation site. The fallen star emits blue particles, and the story follows their joint investigation of the particle-filled area, which Yumia and her companions seem to know something about.


The event structure is similar to last year's "Atelier Summer 2024: Swimsuits at the Resort!" with a special event home and the ability to perform "Facility Development."

In "Facility Development," you can construct various structures at the "Research Base," one of the settings for this event. For details, please check the in-game announcements.


The event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3, with quests and stories being added gradually. As of the 2025/03/31 maintenance, only Part 1 is available, and by reading all of the Part 1 event stories, you can recruit ★1 Viktor [Noble of Unwavering Justice].

Additionally, since this event features characters from the latest title in the series, you can exchange for Pieces of characters that will debut in April's Wishes in the event exchange shop. New Pieces will be added to the exchange shop when the Wishes are available, so please be sure to check.

As for Viktor's Pieces, they can be obtained by playing through the event. More acquisition methods will be added over time, so we hope you enjoy the event until the end.


○"Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES" Now Live

"Yumia Festival: Alchemist of Memories Yumia LEGEND FES" is now available.

This Wish introduces ★3 Yumia [Seeker of Memories] and Memoria "To Know the Truth".


In the battle system of "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~," there's a concept of attack distance called "Range," where you switch between two types of attack distances: "In-Range" and "Out-Range." In "Atelier Resleriana," Yumia's Skill 1 and Skill 2 also have In-Range and Out-Range versions.

This doesn't mean that the concept of distance is being introduced to "Atelier Resleriana" battles, but rather that the effects of skills change between In-Range and Out-Range, providing a different battle experience from before. Please welcome her and take her into battle.


Additionally, Viktor, whom you can recruit from the aforementioned event, and other characters from "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~" who will appear in April, will all be able to use skills that change between In-Range and Out-Range. Please look forward to the other characters that will appear in April.


▼Updates Related to Improved Playability

○Shop Renovation

The following improvements have been made to the Shop > Daily > Shiny Coin tab:

●Unified the display probability of character Pieces regardless of their initial rarity

●Changed to display Pieces of characters not yet recruited

●Changed to display three character Pieces

●Changed to display two Mana items

Until now, the display probability for Pieces in the Shiny Coin tab varied based on the character's initial rarity, with ★1 characters having a higher chance and ★3 characters having a lower chance. With this update, we've adjusted the system so that Pieces are displayed with the same probability regardless of initial rarity.


We've also made changes so that Pieces of characters you haven't yet recruited can now be displayed. This allows you to collect Pieces for characters before recruiting them. Previously, there was no way to collect Pieces until after recruiting a character, which created a time imbalance before you could awaken them enough to take them into battle. We implemented this improvement to provide a way to gradually collect Pieces in advance.

After this update, there's no change to the fact that Pieces won't be displayed in the Shiny Coin tab for characters who already have enough Pieces to awaken to ★6.


In addition to these changes, due to the increasing demand for Pieces and Mana, previously one character Piece and one Mana item were displayed with each refresh, but after this update, three character Pieces and two Mana items will be displayed, increasing the number of Pieces and Mana that can be acquired from the shop.
