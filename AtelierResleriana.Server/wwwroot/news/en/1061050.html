How to Play

"The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy Middle Chapter LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in a score attack format.

There is only one battle available to challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battles.

Challenge repeatedly to aim for higher scores!

Your earned scores will be displayed in a ranking format, and higher ranks will earn more rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy Middle Chapter LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can earn various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will share the same rank.

Example: If three users have the score for 10th place, all three will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be checked from Event > The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy Middle Chapter LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
