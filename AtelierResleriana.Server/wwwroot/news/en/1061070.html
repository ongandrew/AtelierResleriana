Wish Details

"Yumia Festival: Fashionable, Energetic, Young Lady! Isla LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now live!

Isla from "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~" makes her debut!

Also introducing the new Memoria "Unwavering Feelings"!

This is a step-up Wish consisting of STEP 1-3, where you can make a free "10x Wish" at STEP 3!

STEP 1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 3

Free 10x Wish

※You can complete STEP 1-3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when making a "10x Wish" at STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, you will return to STEP 1.

※During the event period, when you obtain a featured character you already have, you will receive 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Crystals.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can choose one from the featured characters and memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while the unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select which featured character or memoria to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Select Featured.

Isla [Friendly Fashionista] Debut Mission

To celebrate the debut of Isla [Friendly Fashionista], we're holding a limited-time "Isla [Friendly Fashionista] Debut Mission"!

Based on the number of times you make a wish in "Yumia Festival: Fashionable, Energetic, Young Lady! Isla LEGEND FES" or "Yumia Festival: Fashionable, Energetic, Young Lady! Isla LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Isla [Friendly Fashionista] Pieces and other items!

Don't miss this chance to awaken your newly obtained Isla [Friendly Fashionista]!

※Missions end at 2025/04/16 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the "Yumia Festival: Fashionable, Energetic, Young Lady! Isla LEGEND FES" and "Yumia Festival: Fashionable, Energetic, Young Lady! Isla LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" periods.

※For mission details, please check Home > Mission > Event > Isla [Friendly Fashionista] Debut Mission.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during their Wish period. Characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memoria include the newly featured SSR memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※LEGEND Medals are obtained as follows:

●10x Wish at STEP 1 and STEP 2: 8 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish at STEP 3.
