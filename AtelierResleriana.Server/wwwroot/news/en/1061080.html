How to Play Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 2

'Yumia Festival Special Cup Part 2' is an event where you play special battles where your score is determined by how much HP you deplete from enemies.

Keep challenging to aim for higher scores!

Your scores will be displayed in a ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards. There are no score rewards or clear rewards.

※This event's exchange shop is the same as the 'Roman Great Martial Arts Tournament' exchange shop.
