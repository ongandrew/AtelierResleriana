Second Half Added to the Event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy"!

The second half has been added to the event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy"!

Enjoy this major event featuring Yumia and her companions from the latest series title "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasmal Creation~"!

In the second half of the event, new "Score Battles" and "Boss Challenges" have been added.

In the second half's Score Battles, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Second Half] to further raise your facility levels, as well as a small chance to acquire Rare Coins [Yumia Festival Second Half]. Rare Coins can be exchanged for Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Pieces at the exchange shop. Additionally, by clearing specific Score Battles, you can conceive event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, by clearing the second half's Boss Challenge, you can obtain an event-exclusive emblem. This emblem is different from those in the first and middle parts, so aim to GET it by taking on the challenge!

※This notice only contains information about the second half. For information about the first half of the "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" event, please see here.

※For information about the middle part of the "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" event, please see here.

Facility Development

In "Facility Development," you can build various structures at the "Research Base," which is one of the settings for this event. From the second half, it is possible to raise the facility level to 21 or higher, and the "Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Second Half]" needed for this can be obtained in the newly added second half event Score Battles. There are cases where you must select one option from multiple types of structures, and based on your selection, you can read event stories or create "Research Records."

Progress with "Facility Development" to create your own Research Base!

Additionally, from the second half, you can reset your facility level after reaching facility level 30.

When you reset, your facility level and development status will be reset, allowing you to choose different options that you didn't select before as you progress through development again, and you can obtain different facility level rewards depending on the number of times you've cycled through.

Aim to develop all facilities and collect all Research Records!

※"Facility Coins [Yumia Festival First Half]" needed to raise the facility level from 1 to 10 can be obtained in the first half's event Score Battles.

※"Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Middle Part]" needed to raise the facility level from 11 to 20 can be obtained in the middle part's event Score Battles.

Research Records

"Research Records" are event-exclusive short stories that are unlocked by meeting certain conditions, such as progressing with facility development.

These provide glimpses into Yumia and her companions during this event that aren't covered in the main event story.

New "Research Records" have been added with the second half! Play the event and collect many records!

Rental Party

You can challenge the event Boss Challenges using a "Rental Party" consisting of specific characters and Memoria.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party, and challenge the Boss Challenges!

※You can still obtain clear rewards even when clearing with a Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the characters and Memoria in the lineup differ depending on the event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess Memoria that are bonus targets, the drop amount of synthesis materials and coins will increase!

Target Memoria and bonus amounts can be confirmed from Event > The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※The Memoria that are bonus targets differ for each tab (first half, middle part, second half) within the Score Battles.

※To commemorate the Yumia Festival, for this event only, Memoria bonuses can be stacked up to 400%. The bonus amount increases based on the unlocked status of the target Memoria.

※Bonuses apply just by owning the target Memoria. You don't need to have them in your party.

※If you own multiple of the same Memoria, the unlock ranks are added together to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you own two target Memoria at unlock rank I, they will be calculated as one Memoria at unlock rank II.
