Wish Overview

"Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES" is now live!

Rutger from "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasmal Creation~" makes his debut!

Additionally, the new Memoria "Forging My Own Path" also debuts!

Furthermore, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES) is now available in the Shop!

※During the event period, if you welcome a featured character that you already have, you will receive 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Shards.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one from the featured characters or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while unselected targets will equally share a total of 0.5% drop rate.

You can select which featured character or Memoria to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Select Featured.

Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Debut Commemoration Mission

To commemorate the debut of Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path], we are holding the limited-time "Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Debut Commemoration Mission"!

Based on the number of times you wish on "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES" or "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Pieces! Other items are also available.

This is your chance to immediately awaken the Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] you've welcomed! Don't miss out!

※Missions will end at 2025/04/23 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the end of "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES" and "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Debut Commemoration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, purchasable up to 2 times each for 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals respectively!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

Additionally, "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity Wish Ticket" has been added for a limited time to Home > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket tab.

※When using Wish Tickets, you cannot obtain LEGEND Medals.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES) cannot be used for Wishes other than "Yumia Festival Rutger the Quick-Witted Who Laughs in the Face of Adversity LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets will expire after their usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When making Wishes using Lodestar Gems, you will receive LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. They can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange only during their Wish period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria include newly debuted featured SSR Memoria from the ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※LEGEND Medals are obtained as follows:

●1 Wish: 1 Medal

●10 Wishes: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Conception

When you awaken Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] to ★5, you can conceive a recipe for an SSR equipment item that is effective only when equipped by Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path].

Aim for ★5 awakening by challenging the limited-time "Rutger [The One Who Forges His Own Path] Debut Commemoration Mission" and Piece Quests!

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be conceived, please check Home Screen > Recipe Conception > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for conceiving the recipe.
