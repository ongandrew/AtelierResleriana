Wish Overview

'Atelier Yumia 300,000 Copies Milestone Celebration ★3 Character & SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wish' is now available!

We are pleased to announce that the highly popular "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Envisioned Land~" has reached a milestone of 300,000 cumulative copies shipped, the fastest in the series, and we are holding a special Wish to celebrate!

This Wish is a special single Wish that can be made using 1,500 paid Lodestar Gems, and guarantees either a ★3 character or SSR Memoria that newly appeared in the Yumia Festival!

Additionally, when you receive a character that you already have through this Wish, the amount of character pieces you obtain will be increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Shards ×50.

※This Wish can be performed only once.

※Characters or Memoria other than the ★3 characters or SSR Memoria that newly appeared in the Yumia Festival will not appear in this Wish.

※Please check the drop rates from the Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates menu.

LEGEND Medals and LEGEND FES Exchange

You will receive LEGEND Medals when you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Exchange > LEGEND Medal tab.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the new featured SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and the SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 8 medals

Notes

●This Wish is exclusive to paid Lodestar Gems and cannot be made using free Lodestar Gems or Wish Tickets.

●Please check the drop rates from the Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates menu.

●After making this Wish once, it will no longer be displayed in the Wish list.

●If you receive a character that is already in your roster, it will automatically be converted to Character Pieces and Wish Shards.

●The event period and content are subject to change without prior notice. Thank you for your understanding.
