List of Issues Under Investigation

●Display issue with Trait Activator item details

○In the event exchange for "Group Practice ~Material Collection~" event, item details for various Trait Activators may not display correctly (this is a display-only issue, and exchanged Trait Activators can be used normally.)

●Issue with bonus effects in specific rerun events

○In rerun events with Score Battle format, when viewing the bonus effect screen from the event screen or the party formation screen before battle, bonus details may not display correctly (this is a display-only issue, and bonus effects that are displayed correctly in other events and cultivation quests are also applied to Score Battles in rerun events.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using fast-forward in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast-forward.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the link for post-payment options is not displayed in the Daily Pass

●Issue with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur when playing movies on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Video stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■Force closing when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with mail reception status

○When receiving characters, Memorias, or character pieces from mail, the mail may not show as "received" (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without any problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timing (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may potentially improve this issue.)

●Issue with video ad viewing on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may force close when viewing video ads (※Please rest assured that this does not reduce the number of viewable ads. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may potentially improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
