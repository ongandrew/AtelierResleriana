About This Wish

"Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish [Paid Only]" is now available!

The ★3 character Ryza [Blooming Energy] with newly unlocked ★6 awakening is featured!

This is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 3, and at STEP 3, you can perform a "10-Wish" for free!

STEP 1

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 2

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 3

10-Wish for free

※You can complete STEP 1 to 3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when performing a "10-Wish" at STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, you will start again from STEP 1.

Increased Piece Acquisition for Ryza [Blooming Energy]!

In this Wish, the amount of pieces you receive for already owned Ryza [Blooming Energy] is increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Crystals ×50.

※The increase only applies to pieces for already owned Ryza [Blooming Energy]. Pieces for other characters and Wish Crystals are not increased.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly released after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged permanently.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly featured SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are part of Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●10-Wish at STEP 1 and STEP 2: 8 medals

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals from the free 10-Wish at STEP 3.
