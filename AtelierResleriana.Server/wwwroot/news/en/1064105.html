About This Wish

"Resleri Academy Select Guaranteed Wish Vol. 1" is now available!

To celebrate the "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" event, we're holding a special Wish.

This is a special single Wish that costs Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500, and you can choose and obtain one guaranteed character from among Resna [Loved Rookie], Ryza [Blooming Energy], and Eren [Noble Wolf]!

※This Wish can be performed only once.

※If you obtain a character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Crystals ×50.

※Please check the drop rates from Wish > Rates/Wish Details > Rates.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Exchange > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly featured SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are part of Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 8 medals
