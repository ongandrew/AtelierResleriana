Letter from the Development Team vol.22

Thank you very much for playing "Atelier Resleriana."

This is Hosoi, producer of "Atelier Resleriana."


How did you enjoy the "Yumia Festival" in April?

Some of you may have been surprised to see characters from the latest title appear in "Resleriana" so soon after its release. It's been a while since we've had a month-long major event, but we challenged ourselves in various ways to fully convey Yumia and her friends' charm, such as adding full voice acting to the event story and preparing multiple patterns for facility development. We hope many of you enjoyed it.


On 2025/04/25, we added the long-awaited rerun of "Resleri Academy" events parts 1-3. We apologize for the wait, but we wanted to rerun them at just the right timing, so we decided to add them now as we release the sequel event.


And starting today, after the 2025/04/30 maintenance, we're holding the major event "Resleri Academy 2nd Term," which is a sequel to "Resleri Academy." In this letter, I'd like to introduce the content of this event and the updates implemented with this maintenance.

First, let me introduce the current events.


▼Current Event Details

○Major Event "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" Now Underway

We are currently holding the major event "Resleri Academy 2nd Term."

This event features a story that continues from the "Resleri Academy" event held in April 2024. As I mentioned earlier, we've added reruns of "Resleri Academy" parts 1-3 ahead of this event, so please try playing those as well.


Also, like the "Alchemist of Memories and Blue Conspiracy" event held in April, the "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" event is divided into first, middle, and final parts, with quests and stories being added over time. Additionally, you can exchange for character pieces of new characters that will appear in May's Wishes in the event exchange shop. New pieces will be added to the exchange shop when the Wishes become available, so please check it out.


〇"Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students" Now Available


We are currently holding the "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students" Wish.

In this Wish, ★3 Klaudia [Blooming Creation] and Memoria "Are You Excited~?" make their debut.

Klaudia in her school uniform, who appears in the "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" event, is now available in this Wish.

Please check out the event story in May to see how she interacts with the school characters who have already appeared.


○New Event Format "Conquest Total War β Version" Now Underway

We are currently holding the new event format "Conquest Total War β Version."

In this event, you'll form multiple parties and aim to clear all battles placed on 9 squares. There are beginner and advanced difficulty levels, so even those who have just started playing can enjoy the beginner level.

Unlike previous events, in this event, characters who have cleared any battle cannot be deployed in other battles within the event. Therefore, you'll need to form different parties for each of the 9 battles. Please try challenging the battles with various character compositions.

Also, this event is being held as a β version. We are particularly looking to improve the party formation aspect of the event.


▼Updates for Improved Playability

○Shop Improvements

We made the following improvements to the Shop > Daily > Lodestar Gems tab:

●Changed the number of character pieces that can be purchased with paid Lodestar Gems from 1 slot to 2 slots

●Changed the number of character pieces that can be purchased with free Lodestar Gems from 2 slots to 1 slot

After reviewing how players have been exchanging items in the Lodestar Gems tab over the past month and a half, we've made changes so that character pieces that can be purchased with paid Lodestar Gems now appear in 2 slots. Also, character pieces that can be purchased with free Lodestar Gems now appear in 1 slot.


While the character pieces that can be purchased with paid Lodestar Gems in the Lodestar Gems tab are more affordable than the Awakening Packs that were previously sold for new characters, the limited number of display slots made it difficult to purchase pieces for specific characters you wanted.


Therefore, we've implemented this improvement. While the number of slots for pieces that can be purchased with free Lodestar Gems has been reduced, we hope for your understanding.


▼Other Updates

○Ryza [Blooming Energy] Can Now Be Awakened to ★6!

After the 2025/04/30 maintenance, Ryza [Blooming Energy] can now be awakened to ★6.

The new event format "Conquest Total War β Version" mentioned earlier allows you to obtain Star Robe Light needed for ★6 awakening and Secret Technique Scripture needed for skill evolution, so please check it out again.

Also, to celebrate Ryza [Blooming Energy]'s ability to awaken to ★6, we're holding the "Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish." A paid-only step-up Wish will also be available.


Additionally, in this Wish, the number of character pieces you receive when obtaining Ryza [Blooming Energy] again has been increased. Please check the in-game announcement for details.

In addition, to celebrate the start of pre-orders for the "Ryza Resleri Academy Ver." figure, we're offering up to 100 free Wishes through login bonuses.

This is a great opportunity to obtain Ryza [Blooming Energy] if you don't have her yet, or to get a large number of pieces if you already have her, so please be sure to collect them during the period.


That concludes the information I wanted to share with you this time.


The updates in May will mainly focus on the "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" event. We have prepared new Wishes, event dungeons with slightly different elements from previous ones, and events where you can collect powerful synthesis materials, so we hope you look forward to them.

We appreciate your continued support for "Atelier Resleriana."


Producer
