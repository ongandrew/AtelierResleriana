Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We are excited to announce the "Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign" where you can earn up to Lodestar Gems ×3,000 based on your equipment synthesis results!

Synthesize equipment items during the campaign period, and if your synthesis is successful, you'll receive Lodestar Gems according to your synthesis results!

Additionally, if you perform over 300 syntheses during the campaign period, you'll receive the "Synth-GO! Prize" of Lodestar Gems ×3,000.
You can check your current synthesis count from Home > Missions > Event.

Synthesize your desired equipment items and join this campaign!
