This event features a "Score Battle" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you have bonus Memoria in your possession, the amount of synthesis materials dropped will increase!

You can check the target Memoria and bonus amounts from Event > Resleriana School 2nd Semester > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases depending on the unbind status of the target Memoria.

※The bonus applies just by owning the target Memoria. You don't need to include them in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same Memoria, the unbind ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you own two target Memoria with unbind rank I, they will be calculated as one Memoria with unbind rank II.
