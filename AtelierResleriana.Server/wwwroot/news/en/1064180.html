Wish Overview

"Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner" is now live!

The ★3 character with new outfit Plachta and Memoria "After School Idol" are making their debut!

Additionally, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner) is now available in the shop!

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character that you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Fragments ×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while the unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select your preferred featured character and featured Memoria from Wish > Select Target.

Plachta [Mighty Manager] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate Plachta [Mighty Manager]'s debut, we're holding a limited-time "Plachta [Mighty Manager] Debut Celebration Mission"!

Based on the number of times you Wish on "Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner" or "Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner [Paid Only]", you can earn up to Plachta [Mighty Manager] Pieces ×445 and other items!

This is your chance to immediately awaken Plachta [Mighty Manager] after obtaining her! Don't miss out!

※The mission will end on 2025/05/14 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the "Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner" and "Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner [Paid Only]" period.

※For mission details, please check Home > Mission > Event > Plachta [Mighty Manager] Debut Celebration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab for up to 2 purchases each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

Additionally, "Resleriana School My Best Partner Wish Tickets" have been temporarily added to Home > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket tab.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner) cannot be used for Wishes other than "Resleriana School LEGEND FES My Best Partner".

※Wish Tickets will expire after the usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you will obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters/Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted featured SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and the SSR Memoria that provide Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
