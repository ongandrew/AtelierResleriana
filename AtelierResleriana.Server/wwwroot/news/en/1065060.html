Middle Chapter Added to "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" Event!

The middle chapter has been added to the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event!

Enjoy this event set in Resleriana Academy featuring characters in school uniforms!

The middle chapter adds new "Score Battles" and "Boss Challenges".

In the middle chapter's Score Battles, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Battle Coins. Additionally, clearing specific Score Battles allows you to discover event-exclusive recipes.

Battle Coins can be exchanged for items such as Lodestar Gems and Hope Particles at the exchange shop.

Furthermore, clearing the middle chapter's Boss Challenge will earn you an event-exclusive Emblem. This is different from the first chapter's Emblem, so challenge yourself to get it!

※This event is divided into first, middle, and final chapters. The final chapter will be added at a later date.

※Additional "Score Battles" are also planned when the final chapter is added.

※The "Boss Challenge" and "Emblem" for the final chapter will be added at a later date.

※This notice only covers the middle chapter content. For information about the first chapter of the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event, please check here.

Rental Party

You can challenge the event Boss Challenge using "Rental Parties" with specific characters and Memorias already set up.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the specialty attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party, and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※You can still obtain clear rewards even when clearing with a Rental Party.

※The characters and Memorias in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the characters and Memorias included vary depending on the event.

Memoria Bonus

When you clear quests, if you possess Memorias that are eligible for bonuses, the drop amount of synthesis materials will increase!

You can check the eligible Memorias and bonus amounts from Event > Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※To commemorate the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event, Memoria bonuses can be accumulated up to a maximum of 400% exclusively for this event. The bonus amount increases based on the awakening status of eligible Memorias.

※The bonus is applied simply by possessing the eligible Memorias. You don't need to include them in your party.

※If you possess multiple copies of the same Memoria, the awakening ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you have two rank I eligible Memorias, they will be calculated as one rank II Memoria.
