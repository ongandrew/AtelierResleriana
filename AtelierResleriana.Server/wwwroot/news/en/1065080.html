Wish Overview

"Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!" is now live!

The new costume ★3 character Heidi and Memoria "The Mechanic's Creed" make their debut!

Additionally, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!) is now available in the Shop!

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces×100 and Wish Shards×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while non-selected targets will have a 0.5% drop rate divided equally among them.

You can select which featured character and featured Memoria to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Select Featured.

Heidi [Dream Future] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate Heidi [Dream Future]'s debut, we're holding a limited-time Heidi [Dream Future] Debut Celebration Mission!

Based on the number of times you wish on "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!" or "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival! [Paid Only]", you can earn up to 445 Heidi [Dream Future] Pieces! Other items are also available as rewards.

This is your chance to awaken Heidi [Dream Future] right away! Don't miss it!

※The mission will end on 2025/05/19 11:59. Please note that this is shorter than the "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!" and "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival! [Paid Only]" period.

※For mission details, please check Home screen > Missions > Event > Heidi [Dream Future] Debut Celebration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, purchasable twice each with Lodestar Gems×1,500 and LEGEND Medals×25!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to perform one Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!) cannot be used for any Wish other than "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Science Festival!"

※Wish Tickets will expire after their usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When you perform a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you will receive LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged permanently.

※The SSR Memorias available for exchange are the newly-debuted featured SSR Memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias that provide Memoria Bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
