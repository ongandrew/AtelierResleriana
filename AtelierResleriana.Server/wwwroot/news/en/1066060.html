Wish Details

"Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?'" is now live!

★3 character with a new costume Flocke and Memoria "After the Rain" make their debut!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?') is now available in the shop!

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character that you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Desire Crystals ×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, and non-selected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select which featured character and Memoria to increase the drop rate for in Wish > Select Featured.

Flocke [Heartful Future] Debut Missions

To celebrate Flocke [Heartful Future]'s debut, we're holding the limited-time "Flocke [Heartful Future] Debut Missions"!

Based on the number of times you Wish on "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?'" or "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?' [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Flocke [Heartful Future] Pieces and other items!

This is your chance to immediately awaken Flocke [Heartful Future] after obtaining her! Don't miss out!

※Missions will end on 2025/05/23 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?'" and "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?' [Paid Only]" period.

※For mission details, please check Home > Missions > Event > Flocke [Heartful Future] Debut Missions.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?') is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab for 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals, with a limit of 2 purchases each.

Each Wish Ticket allows you to perform 1 Wish.

※Wishes performed using Wish Tickets will not award LEGEND Medals.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?') cannot be used for other Wishes besides "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES 'Shall We Dance?'".

※Wish Tickets that have expired will be lost.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When performing a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you will receive LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets from Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria includes newly debuted featured SSR Memoria from ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are part of the Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
