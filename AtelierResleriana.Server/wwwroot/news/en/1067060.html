Voting

In "Voting," you can select and vote for the character you want to support.

Voting requires the synthesis item Voting Ticket. You can obtain the recipe for the Voting Ticket from the Score Battles in the second half of the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event.

There are 12 characters to choose from!

Vote for your favorite character and nominate them for student council president!

※For information about the second half of the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event where you can obtain the Voting Ticket recipe, please check here.

※This event does not include event stories based on the number of votes.

※The quality of the Voting Ticket does not affect the number of votes; each ticket increases your vote count by 1.

※Voting Tickets used for "Voting" will be consumed.
