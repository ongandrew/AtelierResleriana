Wish Overview

"Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish" is now open!

The ★3 character Sophie [Mighty Boss] who has had her ★6 awakening unlocked is featured!

Additionally, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish) is now available in the Shop!

Increased Sophie [Mighty Boss] Piece Acquisition!

In this Wish, the amount of pieces you receive for already owned Sophie [Mighty Boss] has been increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Shards ×50.

※Only the pieces for Sophie [Mighty Boss] that you already own will be increased. Character pieces for other characters and Wish Shards are not increased.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals!

You can perform 1 Wish per Wish Ticket.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes using Wish Tickets.

※The Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish) can only be used for the "Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish" and cannot be used for other Wishes.

※Wish Tickets will expire after the usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the limited period when their Wish is available. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added featured SSR memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes using Wish Tickets.

Awakening Packs

When you obtain ★3 Sophie [Mighty Boss], "Awakening Packs" containing character pieces and Plain Cookies will appear in Shop > Limited Time! You can purchase these packs for 72 hours from the moment the product banner is displayed on the Home screen.

You can purchase the "★4.5 Awakening Pack" for 4,500 Paid Lodestar Gems, the "★4 Awakening Pack" for 2,000 Paid Lodestar Gems, and the "★3.5 Awakening Pack" for 1,000 Paid Lodestar Gems.
