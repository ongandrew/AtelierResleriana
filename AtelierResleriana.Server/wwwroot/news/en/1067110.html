Wish Overview

"Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Stand By You [Paid Only]" is now open!

★3 character with a new outfit Juna and memoria "The One and Only Glory" make their debut!

This is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 3, and in STEP 3, you can perform a "10x Wish" for free!

STEP 1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 3

10x Wish for free

※You can go through STEP 1 to 3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when performing the "10x Wish" in STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, it will start again from STEP 1.

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character you already own, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Shards ×50.

Feature Selection

In this Wish, you can increase the drop rate of one of the featured characters or memorias of your choice. The drop rate for your selected target will be 1%, while non-selected targets will equally share a drop rate of 0.5%.

You can select which featured character or memoria you want to have an increased drop rate from Wish > Select Feature.

Juna [With Ideals] Release Commemoration Mission

To commemorate the release of Juna [With Ideals], we are holding a limited-time Juna [With Ideals] Release Commemoration Mission!

Based on the number of times you wish on "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Stand By You" or "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Stand By You [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Juna [With Ideals] Pieces! Other items are also available.

This is your chance to immediately awaken the Juna [With Ideals] you obtain! Don't miss out!

※Missions will end at 2025/05/28 11:59. Please note that this is shorter than the period for "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Stand By You" and "Resleriana Academy LEGEND FES Stand By You [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home screen > Missions > Events > Juna [With Ideals] Release Commemoration Mission.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the limited period when their Wish is available. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added featured SSR memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●10x Wish in STEP 1 and STEP 2: 8 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish in STEP 3.
