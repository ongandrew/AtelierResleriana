Wish Overview

"Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester ★3 Character or SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wish" is now open!

We are holding this special Wish to commemorate the story conclusion of the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event.

This is a special single Wish that can be performed with 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems, which guarantees you'll obtain a ★3 character or SSR memoria that was newly introduced in Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester!

Additionally, if you obtain a character you already own, the amount of character pieces you receive will be increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Shards ×50.

※This Wish can be performed only once.

※This Wish only includes ★3 characters or SSR memorias newly introduced in Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester; no other characters or memorias will appear.

※You can check the drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the limited period when their Wish is available. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added featured SSR memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 8 medals

Notes

●This Wish is exclusive to Paid Lodestar Gems, so you cannot use Free Lodestar Gems or Wish Tickets.
