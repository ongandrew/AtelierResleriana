Wish Overview

The "Costume Collection Wish" is now available!

This is a special wish where you can wish 10 times for 1,500 Lodestar Gems, and features pick-up rates for the costume versions of Heidi, Flocke, and Juna that are permanently obtainable as of this wish's launch!

Furthermore, when you obtain costume versions of Heidi, Flocke, or Juna that you already have through this wish, you'll receive increased piece rewards! You'll get 200 character pieces and 50 Wish Shards.

※This wish can be executed up to 15 times.

※Please check the drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

Pick-up Selection

In this wish, you can select any character from the pick-up characters to increase their drop rate. The selected character's drop rate becomes 1%, while unselected characters have their 0.5% rate divided equally among them.

You can select which pick-up character you want to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Target Pick-up.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pick-up SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●10-wish: 6 medals
