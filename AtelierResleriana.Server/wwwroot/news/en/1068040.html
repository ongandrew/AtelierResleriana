We have now added the new feature "Star Prayer Stone Exchange".

The "Star Prayer Stone Exchange" can be accessed from Home > Shop > Character Memoria > Star Prayer Stone Tab.

[Updated] Star Prayer Stone Exchange

With Star Prayer Stone Exchange, you can obtain characters and Memoria in exchange for items. There are 3 types of Star Prayer Stone Exchange: "Star Prayer Stone Bud", "Star Prayer Stone Crown", and "Star Prayer Stone Crown (Memoria)", and you can exchange for characters and Memoria according to their respective drop rates.

The Star Prayer Stone Bud has a combined drop rate of 30% for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria, while the Star Prayer Stone Crown has a combined drop rate of 60% for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria. Additionally, the Star Prayer Stone Crown (Memoria) is a Star Prayer Stone Exchange that provides Memoria only, with a drop rate of 60% for SSR Memoria.

The Star Prayer Stone Bud, Star Prayer Stone Crown, and Star Prayer Stone Crown (Memoria) each use different items, and you can exchange for characters and Memoria by consuming a set number of Star Prayer Stone Seeds, Star Prayer Stone Petals, or Star Prayer Stone Petals (Memoria).
