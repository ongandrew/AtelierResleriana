New Feature "Clear Party" Added!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have now added the new feature "Clear Party"!

Clear Party

"Clear Party" is a feature that allows you to check party information of players who have already cleared that quest. Additionally, for Score Battles and Boss Challenges, you can also check their score ranks.

Clear Party can be accessed from the party screen just before deploying to each quest.

Within Clear Party, there are two types of tabs: "Recommended" and "Latest".

▼About Clear Party Tabs

Tab Name

Tab Content

Recommended

Clear parties featuring characters suited for quest clearing or characters you have joined are displayed.

Latest

The latest clear parties are displayed.

Use other users' clear parties as reference to aim for clearing various battles!

※Clear Party information will not be displayed for certain quests such as Roman's Grand Tournament and Legend Challenge.
