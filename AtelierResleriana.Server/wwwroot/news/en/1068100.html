Event "Hammer of Tested Resolve" Now Open!

Event "Hammer of Tested Resolve" is now open!

Enjoy the event story featuring Izana!

This event features "Score Battle" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, and challenges the high-difficulty battle "Boss Challenge".

This "Score Battle" can be challenged using stamina instead of challenge tickets. When you clear Score Battle, you can obtain Battle Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Battle Coins can be exchanged for Hope Grains and Lodestar Gems at the exchange shop.

Furthermore, clearing the event Boss Challenge will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Additionally, Event Mission Pass will be available for purchase in this event! By clearing event missions and collecting Event Mission Pass Points, you can obtain additional rewards! Unlocking Premium with Paid Lodestar Gems allows you to obtain even more luxurious rewards!

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using "Rental Party" which has specific characters and Memoria pre-organized.

You can freely organize equipment items and battle items created through synthesis into the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the elemental affinities and roles of the characters organized in the Rental Party, and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria organized in Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the organized characters and Memoria vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When you clear quests, having bonus-eligible Memoria will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials!

Target Memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Hammer of Tested Resolve > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonus is added up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amount increases based on the unlock status of target Memoria.

※Bonus applies just by possessing target Memoria. It does not need to be organized in your party.

※When possessing multiple copies of the same Memoria, unlock ranks are combined to increase bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: When possessing 2 target Memoria at unlock rank I, it is calculated as 1 Memoria at unlock rank II.
