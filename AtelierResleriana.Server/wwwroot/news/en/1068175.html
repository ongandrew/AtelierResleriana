[Updated] Regarding the Bug Where Certain Operations May Cause the Home Screen to Become Unresponsive

【June 3, 2025 Update】

We have fixed the bug that was described in this notice.

Ver2.3.1 update is required to reflect the bug fix. For details about the update, please see this notice.

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We would like to inform you about a currently occurring bug where certain operations may cause the home screen to become unresponsive.

Bug Details

●During the purchase process for Lodestar Gems and other items, if you move screens or close the app, when transitioning to the home screen afterwards, buttons and icons may not display, causing the game to become unresponsive

How to Resolve the Bug

If you have already linked your account and know the information needed for account transfer, this bug can be resolved using the following method.

※Account transfer requires prior account linking. If you have not completed account linking, please contact us individually through Title Screen > Menu > Contact Us.

(You can check the account linking method here)

○Reinstall the app and perform account transfer

1.Delete and reinstall the "Atelier Resleriana" app

2.From Title > Menu > Account Transfer, perform a transfer to the same account where the bug occurred

(You can also check the account transfer method here)

Response

We plan to fix this issue in an update scheduled for the near future.

We deeply apologize for the inconvenience caused to everyone.
