Issue Where Certain Operations May Cause Home Screen to Become Unresponsive

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We would like to inform you about a currently occurring issue where certain operations may cause the home screen to become unresponsive.

Issue Details

●When purchasing Lodestar Gems or similar items, if you navigate to another screen or close the app during the purchase process, the home screen may become unresponsive afterward with buttons and icons not displaying, making it impossible to proceed

How to Resolve the Issue

If you have already linked your account and know the information required for account transfer, this issue can be resolved using the following method.

※Account transfer requires prior account linking. If you have not completed account linking, please contact us individually through Title Screen > Menu > Contact Us.

(You can check how to link your account here)

○Reinstall the app and perform account transfer

1.Delete the "Atelier Resleriana" app and reinstall it

2.From Title > Menu > Account Transfer, perform a transfer to the same account where the issue occurred

(You can also check how to transfer your account here)

Response

We plan to fix this issue in an upcoming update scheduled in the near future.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
