Synthesize! Synth-GO! Campaign Now On!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We are now holding the "Synthesize! Synth-GO! Campaign" where you can earn up to Lodestar Gems ×3,000 based on equipment item synthesis results!

Synthesize equipment items during the campaign period, and if your item synthesis is successful, you'll receive Lodestar Gems based on the synthesis results!

Additionally, if you perform synthesis 300 times during the period, you'll receive Lodestar Gems ×3,000 as the "Synth-GO! Prize."
You can check your current synthesis count from Home > Missions > Event.

Synthesize your desired equipment items and be sure to participate in this campaign!
