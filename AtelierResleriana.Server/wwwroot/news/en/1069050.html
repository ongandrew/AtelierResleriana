Event "Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercises~" Begins!

This event features "Score Battles" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, and high-difficulty "Boss Challenges".

This "Score Battle" can be challenged using stamina instead of challenge tickets. Clearing Score Battles will reward you with synthesis materials as well as Battle Coins. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the shop for Wishing Granules and Lodestar Gems.

Additionally, clearing Event Boss Challenges will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Furthermore, this event introduces the "Event BOX Wish"!

During the event period, clear quests to obtain Event BOX Wish Tickets [Knight Practical Exam] and use them in the Event BOX Wish to obtain rewards such as Star Prayer Petals (Memoria)!

Also, when you have Izana [Knight's Beginning] and Johanna [Phenom's Reborn] in your party, Event BOX Wish Tickets [Knight Practical Exam] will drop an additional 5 tickets per character for every 10 stamina consumed!

※In content that does not consume stamina, such as the Main Story, there will be no additional drops of "Event BOX Wish Tickets [Knight Practical Exam]".

Event BOX Wish

The Event BOX Wish can be used with "Event BOX Wish Tickets" obtained during the event period.

When you use the Event BOX Wish, you can randomly obtain items from within the BOX.

Each time you use the Event BOX Wish, the contents of the BOX decrease, and when you obtain all items within the BOX, you move on to the next BOX.

You can check the items in each BOX from Event > Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercises~ > Event BOX Wish > BOX List.

※The method for obtaining "Event BOX Wish Tickets" may differ depending on the event.

※The BOX contents from BOX 1 onwards are all identical.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenges can be attempted using "Rental Parties" composed of specific characters and memorias.

Rental Parties can be freely equipped with equipment items and battle items created through synthesis.

Synthesize items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and take on the Boss Challenge!

※You can still obtain clear rewards even when clearing with a Rental Party.

※The characters and memorias in Rental Parties cannot be changed. Additionally, the characters and memorias vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, possessing bonus-eligible memorias will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials!

Target memorias and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercises~ > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases based on the unlock status of target memorias.

※Simply possessing target memorias applies the bonus. They do not need to be equipped in your party.

※When possessing multiple copies of the same memoria, unlock ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V).

Example: If you possess 2 copies of a target memoria at unlock rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at unlock rank II.
