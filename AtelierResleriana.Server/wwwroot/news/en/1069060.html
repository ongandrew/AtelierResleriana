How to Play

"Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercise~ LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your achieved score will be displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercise~ LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" by logging in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they will have the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

You can check reward details from Event > Knight Practical Exam ~Additional Exercise~ LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
