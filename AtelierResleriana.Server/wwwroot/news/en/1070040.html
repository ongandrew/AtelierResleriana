Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and memoria pre-arranged.

You can freely arrange equipment items and battle items created through synthesis in the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the elemental affinities and roles of the characters in the Rental Party to take on the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and memoria arranged in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the characters and memoria arranged vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess bonus-eligible memoria, the drop amount of synthesis materials will increase!

You can check the eligible memoria and bonus amounts from Event > Spirits of the Eastern Realm: Loyal Retainer > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases based on the release status of eligible memoria.

※Bonuses apply simply by possessing eligible memoria. They do not need to be arranged in your party.

※If you possess multiple copies of the same memoria, the release ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 copies of eligible memoria at release rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at release rank II.
