Wish Overview

"Journey to the Elegant Land: Lila LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now underway!

★3 Character Lila [Elegant Warrior] and Memoria "Unseen Scenery" make their debut & are featured in the pickup!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1-5, with 2x pickup rates at STEP 3, and even 3x pickup rates at STEP 5!

Additionally, after completing wishes from STEP 1-5, an additional "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has 2x pickup rates!

Furthermore, when you perform 10 wishes, you can obtain Lila [Elegant Warrior] Piece ×50 as a bonus!

●STEP 1-5 Step-up Wish

STEP 1

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 2

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 3

2x Pickup Rates!

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 4

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 5

3x Pickup Rates!

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000

●Bonus Wish

2x Pickup Rates!

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×3,000

Includes Lila [Elegant Warrior] Piece ×50 bonus!

※The Step-up Wish from STEP 1-5 can be performed up to 1 complete cycle.

※The Bonus Wish can be performed up to 5 times.

※During the event period, if you obtain a pickup character you already have, you will receive Character Piece ×100 and Wish Gem ×50.

Lila [Elegant Warrior] Debut Commemoration Mission

To commemorate the debut of Lila [Elegant Warrior], we are holding the limited-time "Lila [Elegant Warrior] Debut Commemoration Mission"!

※Please note that this has a shorter duration than "Journey to the Elegant Land: Lila LEGEND FES" and "Journey to the Elegant Land: Lila LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Lila [Elegant Warrior] Debut Commemoration Mission.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When using Lodestar Gems to perform wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 03/06/2025 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish period. ★3 Characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When Lila [Elegant Warrior] is awakened to ★5, you can gain inspiration for an SSR equipment item recipe that only takes effect when equipped by Lila [Elegant Warrior].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
