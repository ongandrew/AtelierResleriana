Revival Event "Explosive!! Marie's Trial!" Added!

Item Gauge Recovery Speed Ultra UP & Unlimited Usage special boss challenges and high-difficulty battles "EX Challenge" are now available!

Additionally, by clearing specific boss challenges, you can acquire event-exclusive recipes, and by clearing EX Challenges you can obtain event-exclusive emblems!

※This event's "Item Gauge Recovery Speed Ultra UP" increases the item gauge recovery that accumulates when allies take action. Item gauge recovery from ally skills is not increased.

※For details about revival events, please check this announcement.

Event Period

2025/06/18 12:00〜Indefinite

How to Play

Unlock the event with "Memory Keys" and take on special boss challenges with Item Gauge Recovery Speed Ultra UP & Unlimited Usage and "EX Challenges"!

Additionally, by clearing specific boss challenges, you can acquire event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, clearing all boss challenges will unlock "EX Challenges"!

By clearing specific EX Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

EX Challenges are high-difficulty battles that do not consume stamina.

Please try challenging them with your trained party!

※In revival events, recipe acquisition progress from past events is also carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards in past events, you cannot obtain them again in revival events.

※Emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For EX Challenges, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

※Note that the previously held "1st Anniversary LOGIN BONUS Round 3" and "Event Exchange Shop" will not be revived in this event.

Important Notes

●The content and duration of this event may be changed without notice.
