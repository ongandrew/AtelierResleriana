How to Play

"Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation items are the same as in score battles.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your achieved score will be displayed in ranking format, and the higher your ranking, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards, and there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE", you can obtain various rewards according to your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" by logging in after the event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will have the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with the 10th place score, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be confirmed from Event＞Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE＞Ranking＞Ranking Reward List.
