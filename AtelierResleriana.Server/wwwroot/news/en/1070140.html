LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop＞Character Memoria＞LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop＞Packs＞Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memoria are the newly debuted pickup SSR memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-Wish: 11 medals

※LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from Wish Tickets.
