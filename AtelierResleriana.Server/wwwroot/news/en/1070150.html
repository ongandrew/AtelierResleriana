Wish Overview

"Arland Festival - Proud Noble Lady Mimi LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now underway!

★3 character Mimi [Proud Voyager] and memoria "Your Color" make their debut & are featured in pickup!

This wish is a step-up wish with STEP 1-5, where STEP 3 has 2x PU rate, and STEP 5 has 3x PU rate!

Additionally, when you complete wishes from STEP 1-5, an additional "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has 2x PU rate!

Furthermore, when you wish 10 times, you can obtain Mimi [Proud Voyager] Pieces×50 as a bonus!

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop＞Character Memoria＞LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memoria are the newly debuted pickup SSR memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When Mimi [Proud Voyager] is awakened to ★5, you can be inspired with the recipe for an SSR equipment item that only takes effect when equipped by Mimi [Proud Voyager].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be inspired, please check Home Screen＞Recipe Inspiration＞Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
