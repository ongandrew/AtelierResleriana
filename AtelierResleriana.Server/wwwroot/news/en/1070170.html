Atelier Week Starts!

From 2025/06/21 to 2025/06/29, to commemorate multiple past "Atelier" series reaching their anniversaries, we will be holding the Atelier Week Celebration LOGIN BONUS and Atelier Week Missions.

In the Atelier Week Celebration LOGIN BONUS, you can obtain Lodestar Gems!

Additionally, in the Atelier Week Missions, you can obtain Atelier Week Coins by clearing missions. Atelier Week Coins can be exchanged at the Exchange Shop for the event-limited SSR Memoria "Higher and Higher" and more!

※Additional missions will be added to Atelier Week Missions during the event period.
