About Battles with Multiple WAVES

Battles with multiple WAVES are cleared by defeating all enemies that appear in all WAVES.

The current WAVE is displayed in the upper left of the screen.

When switching between WAVES, current HP and status effects are maintained.

If you become unable to battle before reaching the final WAVE, it becomes difficult to clear, so refer to battle information and try forming an effective party!
