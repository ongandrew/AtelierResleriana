Wish Overview

"Arland Festival Sea Breeze Guide Totori LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now open!

★3 character Totori [Endless Voyager] and Memoria "Perfect Day for Gathering!" make their debut & are featured!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1-5, with PU rate doubled at STEP 3, and PU rate tripled! at STEP 5!

Additionally, after wishing through STEP 1-5, a "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has PU rate doubled!

Furthermore, when you wish 10 times, you can obtain Totori [Endless Voyager] Piece ×50 as a bonus!

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to make wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be acquired through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-Up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-Up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Totori [Endless Voyager] to ★5, you can be inspired with recipes for SSR equipment items that only take effect when equipped by Totori [Endless Voyager].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
