Part 2 Added to Event "Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~"!

Part 2 added to the event "Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~"!

Enjoy the event featuring Rorona, Totori, Mimi and others!

In event Part 2, new "Score Battle", "Boss Challenge", and "SP Challenge" for Part 2 will be added.

In Part 2's Score Battle, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Battle Coins. Additionally, by clearing specific Score Battles, you can develop event-exclusive recipes.

Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange for Wishing Stone Seeds and SSR equipment items with Trait Level V/V.

Furthermore, clearing Part 2's Boss Challenge will reward you with an event-exclusive emblem. This is a different emblem from Part 1 and Part 1.5, so aim to GET it by taking on the challenge!

Clearing SP Challenge will reward you with Wishing Stone Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Totori [Endless Voyager] and Rorona [Wonderful Princess], as well as characters with the Arland tag are powered up!

※This event is divided into Part 1, Part 1.5, and Part 2.

※For details on character power-ups, please check Event > Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Part 2 > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※This notice only describes Part 2 content. For Part 1 of the event "Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~", please see here.

※For Part 1.5 of the event "Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~", please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained according to the score rank when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging this satisfying battle!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" that has specific characters and memoria equipped.

You can freely equip synthesis-created equipment items and combat items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the elemental affinity and role of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and memoria equipped in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the equipped characters and memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, possessing bonus-eligible memoria will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials!

The eligible memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Part 2 > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonus can be added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases depending on the release status of eligible memoria.

※Simply possessing eligible memoria applies the bonus. They do not need to be equipped in the party.

※When possessing multiple copies of the same memoria, the release ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 copies of eligible memoria at release rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at release rank II.
