How to Play

"Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Part 2 LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your achieved score will be displayed in ranking format, and higher rankings will earn more rewards.

※This event only offers ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Part 2 LEGEND CHALLENGE", various rewards can be obtained based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" by logging in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they will receive the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be checked from Event > Karoston Island of Learning ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Part 2 LEGEND CHALLENGE > Rankings > Ranking Reward List.
