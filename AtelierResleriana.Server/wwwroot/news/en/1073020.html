[2025/07/11 Addition] Regarding Currently Confirmed Bugs

[2025/07/11 Addition]

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have fixed the following bugs.

●Bug where battles with multiple WAVEs become unprogressable under certain conditions

●Bug where activating Izana [Knight's Beginning]'s ability under certain conditions causes the battle to become unprogressable

●Bug where automatic scrolling doesn't work in certain situations

●Bug where synthesis trait effects are not reflected in Mieke [Fashionable Kitty]'s ability

●Bug where some panels are not affected by panel nullification

●Display bug when unlocking SSR Memoria "Blooming Talent"

●Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

●Display bug in prologue battle tutorial

Additionally, we have added the following bug descriptions.

●Display bug when obtaining characters with different skins

●Bug where Grand Recipes are displayed incorrectly on the Recipe Discovery reward screen

List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug where battles with multiple WAVEs become unprogressable under certain conditions

○Fixed a bug where battles with multiple WAVEs could become unprogressable when using the AUTO function

●Bug where activating Izana [Knight's Beginning]'s ability under certain conditions causes the battle to become unprogressable

○Fixed a bug where battle becomes unprogressable when Izana [Knight's Beginning] equipped with the "High Peak Mirror" equipment item activates the ability "Shining Knight's Hammer"

●Bug where automatic scrolling doesn't work in certain situations

○Fixed a bug where scrolling doesn't work on some screens for skill effects and battle item effect descriptions, making it impossible to read the complete text

■Skill detail screen in the i button displayed at the top right of commands when selecting any skill during battle

■Item detail screen in the i button displayed at the top right of commands on the item action screen when selecting the item button during battle

●Bug where synthesis trait effects are not reflected in Mieke [Fashionable Kitty]'s ability

○Fixed a bug where synthesis trait effects such as "Damage Buff Enhancement [A]" were not reflected in the "When attack target has paralysis, sleep, stun status or 'Royal Majesty' effect, damage +50%" effect of Mieke [Fashionable Kitty]'s ability "Big Chance!"

●Bug where some panels are not affected by panel nullification

○Fixed a bug where the following panels would activate without being affected by the status effects "Panel Nullification" and "Minus Panel Nullification"

■Deadly Medicine Panel

■Wisdom Liberation Panel

■Martelarto Panel

●Display bug when unlocking SSR Memoria "Blooming Talent"

○Fixed a bug where when unlocking SSR Memoria "Blooming Talent", the effect values enhanced by rank increases did not turn blue, making it difficult to understand the enhancement details

●Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○Fixed a bug where when selecting locked quests in any rerun event, unlock conditions were not displayed and a dialog stating that the event period had ended would appear, transitioning to the home screen

●Display bug in the prologue "A Sudden Beginning" tutorial

○Fixed a bug where highlights displayed in the tutorial explaining turn order and skills during battle in the prologue "A Sudden Beginning" were not displaying as intended

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[07/11 Addition] Display bug when obtaining characters with different skins

○When obtaining a character with a different skin from one already owned, an unnecessary white icon appears in the acquisition dialog

●[07/11 Addition] Bug where Grand Recipes are displayed incorrectly on the Recipe Discovery reward screen

○Grand Recipes added after the 2025/07/11 maintenance are displayed as "Extra" instead of "Grand" on the Home Screen > Recipe Discovery > Recipe Discovery Count screen

●Bug where the app forcibly terminates on some Android devices

○On some Android devices, the app forcibly terminates when entering specific quests or stories (※This issue may be resolved by setting Shadow Quality and Background Effects to "Low" in Home Screen > Menu > Options > Display Tab > Custom Settings.)

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○The app may forcibly terminate when using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using high-speed skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include links for postpaid payment

●Bug when playing movies on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following bugs may occur when playing movies

■Poor image quality

■Choppy playback

■Audio desynchronization

■Forced termination when playing movies repeatedly

●Display bug for mail receipt status

○Even after receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces via mail, the display in the mail does not switch to "received" (※This is a display-only bug, and rewards can be received without issue.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3", the app may forcibly terminate at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, the app may forcibly terminate when viewing video ads under certain environment settings (※The number of viewable times will not decrease, so please rest assured. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
