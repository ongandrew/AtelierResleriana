Exploration Renewal!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have renewed the Exploration feature to make it more enjoyable for all users!

We would like to introduce the renewal contents.

Changes to Exploration Time Limit and Destinations

The rewards obtainable from a single exploration are now capped at 24 hours. Additionally, all existing destinations have been removed and 7 new types of destinations have been added.

Drop Item Changes

You can now obtain Valuables and Character Pieces, Wrapped Cookies, and Bottled Mana from each destination. Additionally, synthesis material drops have been removed, and the drop amount of common items has been reduced.

●Valuables

○There is only one type per exploration site, and they can be obtained with low probability. After obtaining them, they are registered in the Archive and you can receive Archive rewards. Valuables cannot be used as items (※From the second time onwards, they are converted to 300 Lodestar Gems.)

●Character Pieces

○You can obtain character pieces of characters whose favored attribute matches the destination name attribute.

●Common Items

○The following items can be obtained commonly from all exploration sites.

■Wrapped Mini Cookies

■Bottled Mana

■Enhancement Drop II

■Experience Candy II

■Great Gems, Gems

■Light Orbs I-III of each attribute

■Cole

Recommended characters for each destination will now be updated at regular intervals.

※Recommended characters for each destination can be viewed from Home Screen > Exploration > Destinations > Each Destination.
