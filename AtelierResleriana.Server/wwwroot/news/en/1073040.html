New Feature "Archive" Added!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have added a new feature called "Archive"!

Archive

The "Archive" is a feature that records all the Memoria and Valuables obtained so far, and allows you to receive Archive rewards based on the total number of each type recorded.

Archive rewards allow you to obtain the following items based on the recorded subjects.

Collect Memoria and Valuables to enrich your Archive!
