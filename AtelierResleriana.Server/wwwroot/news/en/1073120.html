Wish Overview

"Valeria [Radiant Summer] ★6 Awakening Commemorative Wish" now open!

The ★3 character Valeria [Radiant Summer], whose ★6 awakening has been unlocked, is featured!

In this wish, you can obtain Valeria [Radiant Summer] already awakened to ★5!

Furthermore, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Valeria [Radiant Summer] ★6 Awakening Commemorative Wish Exclusive) appears in the shop!

Awakening Stage and Character Piece Amount When Obtaining Pickup Character

If you obtain Valeria [Radiant Summer] for the first time from this wish when Valeria [Radiant Summer] has not yet joined, you can obtain her already awakened to ★5!

Additionally, if you obtain Valeria [Radiant Summer] for the first time from this wish when Valeria [Radiant Summer] has already joined, the piece acquisition amount is increased! You can obtain 1,130 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Crystals.

※The piece acquisition increase is only once, and from the second time onward when obtaining the pickup character, you can obtain 100 Character Pieces and 50 Wish Crystals.

Wish Tickets

Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Valeria [Radiant Summer] ★6 Awakening Commemorative Wish Exclusive) that can be purchased with 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals respectively, limited to 2 times each, appears in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab!

You can execute one wish per wish ticket.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from wishes using wish tickets.

※Wish tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Valeria [Radiant Summer] ★6 Awakening Commemorative Wish Exclusive) cannot be used for wishes other than "Valeria [Radiant Summer] ★6 Awakening Commemorative Wish".

※Wish tickets that exceed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when executing wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase wish ticket sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that newly appeared from 2025/03/06 onward can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are newly appeared pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-Pull Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from wish tickets.

Awakening Packs

When you obtain ★3 Valeria [Radiant Summer], "Awakening Packs" containing character pieces and Plain Cookies appear in Shop > Packs > Limited Time! You can purchase them for 72 hours from the moment the product banner appears on the home screen.

You can purchase "★4.5 Awakening Pack" for 4,500 Paid Lodestar Gems, "★4 Awakening Pack" for 2,000 Paid Lodestar Gems, and "★3.5 Awakening Pack" for 1,000 Paid Lodestar Gems.
