LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to wish, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character/Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Antje [Marine Puppeteer] to ★5, you can gain inspiration for SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Antje [Marine Puppeteer].

※For details about the SSR equipment item recipes you can gain inspiration for, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
