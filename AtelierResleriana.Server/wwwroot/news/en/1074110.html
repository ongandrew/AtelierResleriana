Wish Overview

"Atelier Summer 2025 Aiming for the Horizon LEGEND FES" is now live!

SSR Memoria "Aiming for the Horizon" makes its debut & is featured!

Wish Ticket

Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Atelier Summer 2025 Aiming for the Horizon LEGEND FES Exclusive) appears in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket ×1 allows you to execute one Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Atelier Summer 2025 Aiming for the Horizon LEGEND FES Exclusive) cannot be used in Wishes other than "Atelier Summer 2025 Aiming for the Horizon LEGEND FES."

※Wish Tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you execute a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted from 2025/03/06 onward can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias are the newly appeared featured SSR memorias from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●1 Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.
