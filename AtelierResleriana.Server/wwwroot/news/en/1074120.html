Wish Overview

"Atelier Summer 2025 Aiming for the Horizon LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now live!

SSR Memoria "Aiming for the Horizon" makes its debut & is featured!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 5, with STEP 3 having 2x Featured Rate and STEP 5 having 3x Featured Rate!

Additionally, after wishing through STEP 1 to 5, an additional Wish appears!

The additional Wish has 2x Featured Rate!

●STEP 1-5 Step-up Wish

STEP 1

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 2

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 3

2x Featured Rate!

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 4

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 5

3x Featured Rate!

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000

●Additional Wish

2x Featured Rate!

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems ×3,000

※The step-up Wish can be executed up to 1 complete cycle from STEP 1 to 5.

※The additional Wish can be executed up to 5 times.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you execute a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted from 2025/03/06 onward can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias are the newly appeared featured SSR memorias from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Additional Wish: 16 medals

Important Notes

●This Wish is limited to paid Lodestar Gems only, so it cannot be used with free Lodestar Gems or Wish Tickets.

●Please check the drop rates at Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

●After completing one full cycle of the step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 5, it will be hidden from the Wish list, and the additional Wish will appear where the step-up Wish was displayed.

●After executing all 5 additional Wishes, it will be hidden from the Wish list.

●When you obtain a character you already have, it will automatically be converted to character pieces and Wish Crystals.

●The event period and content may be changed without notice. Please understand this in advance.

●Featured memorias may appear in future Wishes.
