Regarding Information Errors in the "Lodestar Gem Gift Campaign"

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana."

Regarding the currently running "Lodestar Gem Gift Campaign," there were errors in the information provided, so we would like to inform you about the nature of the errors and our response.

About the Errors

Regarding the "Lodestar Gem Gift Campaign," there were errors in the purchase amount requirements for lottery participation in the images used in notices and the official livestream.

Error:

From those who purchase a total of ¥10,000 or more, 5 people will be selected by lottery to receive Lodestar Gems ×10,000

Correct:

From those who purchase a total of ¥5,000 or more, 5 people will be selected by lottery to receive Lodestar Gems ×10,000

Our Response

In response to this matter, we will take the following actions.

■Action ①

As an apology for this matter, we have sent Lodestar Gems ×100 to all users.

Please claim them from "Mail" on the Home screen within the period below.

※Mail (including attached items) will be deleted after 90 days from receipt. Please be sure to open and claim them within the period.

Apology Distribution Period

2025/07/17 14:00 ~ 2025/07/31 11:59

■Action ②

We are changing the lottery participation conditions for the second target period as follows, and relaxing the conditions for those who purchased more than ¥5,000 in total during the first target period to be eligible for the lottery in the second target period.

Before Change:

Those who purchased a total of ¥5,000 or more

during the period 2025/07/22 12:00 ~ 2025/07/31 11:59

After Change:

Lottery from those who meet either of the following conditions

① Those who purchased a total of ¥5,000 or more
during the period 2025/07/22 12:00 ~ 2025/07/31 11:59

② Those who purchased a total of ¥10,000 or more during the period from after 2025/07/11 maintenance completion ~ 2025/07/31 11:59

※Those who purchased a total of ¥10,000 or more during the first period, or those who purchased across two periods such as ¥7,000 in the first period and ¥3,000 in the second period for a total of ¥10,000 or more, will also be eligible for the second lottery.

※Meeting both conditions will not increase your chances of winning.

■Action ③

We have added a note to the description section of the official livestream broadcast page from 2025/07/11 stating that there was an error in the displayed image, and have added corrected information.


We will work to prevent similar issues in the future, anticipating the possibility of holding similar campaigns.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to our users.
