How to Play Roman Grand Fighting Tournament Part 1

"Roman Grand Fighting Tournament Part 1" is an event where you play with special battles in which the amount of enemy HP you reduce becomes your score.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your earned score is displayed in ranking format, and the higher your ranking, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event's rewards are ranking rewards only, with no score rewards or clear rewards.

※This event's exchange shop is the "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament" exchange shop.

※SSR Memoria "Let's Dress Up!" can be exchanged indefinitely at the "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament" exchange shop even after this event ends.
