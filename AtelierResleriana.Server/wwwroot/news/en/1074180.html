How to Play

The "Chaotic SPLASH Race!" event is where you assemble synthesis items to boost effective parameters for each character and aim for high scores.

All synthesis items have three parameters: "Strength," "Power," and "Arm Strength", and the values of these parameters vary based on the synthesis item type, trait type, and trait level.

There are restrictions on the types of items you can assemble, and your score is determined by the total parameters of the synthesis items you've assembled. Based on the score achieved by each character, obtain Lodestar Gems, Astral Prayer Petals (Memoria), ATELIER FES Wish Tickets, and more!

In "Chaotic SPLASH Race!", "Valeria," "Johanna," and "Antje" will take on the race! The importance of each parameter differs by character, so be mindful of parameters when assembling items!

※The synthesis items that can be assembled in this event are limited to those available in Grand Recipes and those whose recipes can be conceived in the "Atelier Summer 2025" Part 1 event.

※The types of items you can assemble can be checked from Home Screen > Event > Chaotic SPLASH Race! > Challenge > Available Items.

※Assembled synthesis items will not be lost.

※Rewards obtainable based on scores can only be obtained the first time you achieve the corresponding score.
