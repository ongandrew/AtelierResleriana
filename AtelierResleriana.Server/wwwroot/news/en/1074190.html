Part 2 Added to Atelier Summer 2025 Event!

Part 2 has been added to the "Atelier Summer 2025" event!

Enjoy the event featuring characters in swimwear outfits!

In Part 2, new missions where you can obtain Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] with tan skin, "Score Battle," Part 2 "Boss Challenge," and "SP Challenge" have been added.

By clearing the added event missions, you can obtain Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] with tan skin!

Even if you don't have the regular skin Klaudia [Moonlit Beach], by obtaining the tan skin Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] through this mission, you can take her into battle, so please be sure to obtain her!

▼Check out Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] with tan skin here!

In Score Battle, you can obtain event-limited synthesis materials and Battle Coins.

Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Lodestar Gems, Grains of Hope, Starweave Beacon, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge will earn you event-limited emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will earn you Astral Prayer Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenge, Lara [Seaside Queen] and Antje [Marine Puppeteer], as well as characters with Seasonal Attire tags are powered up!

Furthermore, when challenging SP Challenge, Lara [Seaside Queen] and Antje [Marine Puppeteer], as well as characters with Seasonal Attire tags are powered up!

※This event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Part 3 will be added at a later date.

※"SP Challenge" will also be added when Part 3 is added.

※Part 3's "Boss Challenge" and "Emblem" will be added at a later date.

※Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] has two types of skins: regular skin and tan skin. If you obtain only one, you cannot change to the skin you haven't obtained. If you obtain both, you can change skins from the character list. Additionally, you cannot deploy regular skin and tan skin simultaneously in formation.

※For details about skins, please check Home Screen > Menu > Help > Characters.

※For details about character power-ups in Boss Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Boss Challenge > Battle Info.

※For details about character power-ups in SP Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 BATTLE > SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※This notice only describes Part 2 content. For information about Part 1 of the "Atelier Summer 2025" event, please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank when clearing quests.

Please try taking on this challenging battle!

※You cannot use "Rental Party" in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using "Rental Party" with specific characters and Memoria pre-assembled.

You can freely assemble synthesis-created equipment items and battle items into Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the attribute specialties and roles of the characters assembled in Rental Party, then take on Boss Challenge!

※You can obtain clear rewards even when clearing using Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria assembled in Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the assembled characters and Memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess bonus-eligible Memoria, synthesis material drop amounts increase!

Eligible Memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses stack up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amounts increase based on the release status of eligible Memoria.

※Bonuses apply just by possessing eligible Memoria. They don't need to be assembled in your party.

※If you possess multiple copies of the same Memoria, release ranks are combined to increase bonus amounts (maximum V).

Example: If you possess 2 copies of eligible Memoria at Release Rank I, it's calculated as 1 copy of Memoria at Release Rank II.
