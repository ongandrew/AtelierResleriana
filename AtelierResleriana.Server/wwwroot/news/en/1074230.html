Revival Event 'Flowers that Bloom in Thirst' Added!

Enjoy this special format story event focusing on two girls who admire Lara: Mieke and Diona!

Also, challenge the Boss Challenge and high-difficulty battle "EX Challenge"!

Additionally, by clearing specific Boss Challenges, you can develop event-exclusive recipes, and by clearing EX Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems!

※Unlike the previous event, we have removed the event point acquisition requirement from the event story unlock conditions. We have also made adjustments to reduce some enemy attribute resistances.

※For details about revival events, please check this notice.

Event Period

2025/07/25 12:00 ~ Indefinite

How to Play

Unlock the event with "Memory Keys" and challenge Boss Challenges and "EX Challenges"!

In this event, choices will appear during the event story, and the subsequent story will change depending on your selection. Depending on your choices, the story may branch into IF routes, so enjoy reading through the event story while considering which choices to make!

Also, by clearing specific Boss Challenges, you can develop event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, once you clear all Boss Challenges, "EX Challenge" will be unlocked!

By clearing EX Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

EX Challenges are high-difficulty battles that do not consume stamina.

Please try challenging them with your trained party!

※Only some stories have choices.

※Even if you choose a choice that leads to an IF route, you can replay the story and select different choices to read the next story.

※In revival events, past event recipe development status is also carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards in past events, you cannot obtain them again in revival events.

※Emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For EX Challenges, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

※Note that the previously held "Flowers that Bloom in Thirst Commemoration LOGIN BONUS" and "Event Exchange Shop" will not be revived in this event.

Important Notes

●The content and duration of this event may be changed without prior notice.
