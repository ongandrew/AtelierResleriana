[2025/08/22 Update] Currently Confirmed Issues

[2025/08/22 Update]

Thank you for always enjoying 'Atelier Resleriana'.

We have fixed the following issues.

●Battle information issues in event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation" and event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE"

List of Fixed Issues

●Battle information issues in event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation" and event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE"

○Fixed an issue where the "Wyvern" hint in the boss challenge of event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation" and in the battle information of event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE" contained effects that do not activate

List of Issues Under Investigation

●Display issue with the gift screen in event "Even When Scattered, the Bond of Friends Blooms"

○In the event "Even When Scattered, the Bond of Friends Blooms", the gift items for this event cannot be obtained through synthesis, but the following displays appear on the screen

■A "Synthesize Gift" button appears in the lower left of the screen, but pressing the button does not respond

■In the help dialog screen displayed from the ? button at the top of the screen, it states that giftable items can be confirmed from the "Synthesize Gift" button

■When you don't possess giftable items, the screen displays 'Please check synthesizable items from "Synthesize Gift"'

●Issue where rerun event "Encounter of Chaos and Order" may not become complete

○In rerun event "Encounter of Chaos and Order", there are cases where the complete display does not appear even after clearing all event stories and battles

●Issue where tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] from login bonus cannot be batch received under specific conditions

○When you already have the normal skin Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] sent to mail from the login bonus cannot be batch received (※It is possible to receive it individually from the receive button in the lower right of the mail.)

●Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" issue

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for postpaid billing

●Movie playback issues on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback

■Video quality deteriorates

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio sync issues occur

■Repeatedly playing movies may cause the app to force close

●Display issue with received status in mail

○Even after receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display within the mail does not switch to "received" (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issues in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request - 3", the app may force close at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Video advertisement viewing issues on specific devices

○On specific devices, when certain environment settings are configured, the app may force close when viewing video advertisements (※Please rest assured that the number of viewable times will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
