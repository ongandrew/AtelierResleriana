List of Issues Under Investigation

●[08/15 Update]Gift screen display issue in the event "Scattered Yet Blooming, Bonds of Friendship"

○In the event "Scattered Yet Blooming, Bonds of Friendship", while gift items cannot be obtained through synthesis for this event, the following displays appear on screen:

■A "Synthesize Gifts" button appears at the bottom left of the screen, but pressing it has no effect

■In the help dialog displayed from the ? button at the top of the screen, it states that giftable items can be checked from the "Synthesize Gifts" button

■When not possessing giftable items, the screen displays 'Please check the items to synthesize from "Synthesize Gifts"'

●[08/15 Update]Issue where rerun event "Encounter of Chaos and Order" may not show as complete

○In the rerun event "Encounter of Chaos and Order", the event may not show as complete even after clearing all event stories and battles

●Issue where tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] from login bonus cannot be received in bulk under specific conditions

○When you already own the regular skin Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] sent to mail as a login bonus cannot be received in bulk (※It can be received individually using the receive button at the bottom right of each mail.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Difference"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Difference" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, postpaid payment links are not displayed in Daily Pass

●Movie playback issue on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices:

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■App force closes when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with receipt status in mail

○Even after receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail does not switch to received status (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timing (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Issue when watching video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may force close when watching video ads (※Please be assured that the number of available views will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to our users.
