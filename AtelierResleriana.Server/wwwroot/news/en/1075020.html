List of Issues Under Investigation

●[08/08 Update] Display issue with partial clear status on Player Information screen

○In Home screen ＞ Menu ＞ Player Information, the clear status of the Four Elements Tower is not displaying correctly (We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●[08/08 Update] Issue with exploration collection limit notifications

○Exploration collection limit notifications may be sent at unintended times (We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●[08/08 Update] Display issue when Johanna [Brilliant Diver]'s Active Skill activates

○When Johanna [Brilliant Diver]'s Active Skill "Remove Signal" activates, the effect visuals are displayed on Johanna herself rather than the selected target (We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●Issue where Resna [Pretty Summer] tanned skin from login bonus cannot be collected in bulk under specific conditions

○If you already have the regular skin Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] sent to mail as a login bonus cannot be collected in bulk (※You can still collect it individually using the collection button in the bottom right of the mail.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "The Power Gap"

○Using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "The Power Gap" may cause the app to force quit (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using high-speed skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for deferred payment options

●Movie playback issues on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■App force quits after repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with collection status in Mail

○After collecting characters, memorias, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail does not switch to "collected" status (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be collected without problems.)

●Behavioral issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3", the app may force quit at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Video ad viewing issue on specific devices

○On specific devices, viewing video ads under certain environment settings may cause the app to force quit (※Please be assured that your available viewing count will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to our users.
