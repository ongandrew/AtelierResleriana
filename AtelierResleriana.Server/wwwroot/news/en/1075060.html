How to Play

"Atelier Summer 2025 Part 3 LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your obtained scores are displayed in ranking format, and higher rankings earn more rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Atelier Summer 2025 Part 3 LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards are distributed via "Mail" when you login after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they will have the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Detailed rewards can be checked from Event > Atelier Summer 2025 Part 3 LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
