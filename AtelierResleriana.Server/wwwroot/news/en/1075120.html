In commemoration of Atelier Summer 2025, the limited-time ranking event "Roman's Grand Tournament Part 2" is now active!

Achieve the highest score possible to get ranking rewards and exchange shop rewards!

Additionally, we're simultaneously holding event missions with 600 more Lodestar Gems than each elemental "Roman's Grand Tournament", so please aim to clear them!

How to Play Roman's Grand Tournament Part 2

"Roman's Grand Tournament Part 2" is an event featuring special battles where your score is determined by the amount of enemy HP you deplete.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your earned score will be displayed in ranking format, and higher rankings earn greater rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards - there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

※This event's exchange shop is the "Roman's Grand Tournament" exchange shop.
