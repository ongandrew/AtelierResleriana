LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to make wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop and Shop ＞ Characters/Memorias ＞ LEGEND Medals tab, as well as used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop ＞ Packs ＞ Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memorias available for exchange are newly debuted pickup SSR Memorias from the active LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.
