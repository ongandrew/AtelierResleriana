Wish Overview

The "Atelier Summer 2025 Spirited Johanna LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now active!

★3 character Johanna [Brilliant Diver] and SSR Memoria "Beach Arm Wrestling" make their debut & are featured as pickups!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1 to 5, with 2x PU rates at STEP 3, and even 3x PU rates! at STEP 5!

Additionally, after completing wishes from STEP 1 to 5, a "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has 2x PU rates!

Furthermore, when you wish 10 times, you can obtain 50 Johanna [Brilliant Diver] Pieces as a bonus!

STEP 4

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to make wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop and Shop ＞ Characters/Memorias ＞ LEGEND Medals tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memorias available for exchange are newly debuted pickup SSR Memorias from the active LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-Up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-Up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Johanna [Brilliant Diver] to ★5, you can be inspired with recipes for SSR equipment items that only take effect when equipped by Johanna [Brilliant Diver].

※For details on SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen ＞ Recipe Inspiration ＞ Awakening.

※Recipe inspiration has no time limit.
