"Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation" Event First Part Now Live!

The "Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation" event first part is now live!

This event is divided into first part and final part. The final part will be added at a later date.

Additionally, you can challenge "Score Battle" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as high-difficulty battles "Boss Challenge" and "SP Challenge".

This "Score Battle" can be challenged using stamina instead of challenge tickets. Clearing Score Battle will reward you with synthesis materials as well as Battle Coins. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange for Grains of Hope, Lodestar Gems, Stellar Garment Flames, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will reward you with Starwish Stone Seeds.

Additionally, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Kimon [Devoted Loyal Ninja], Johanna [Brilliant Diver], and characters with the Earnest tag are powered up!

Furthermore, an Event Mission Pass will be sold for this event! By clearing event missions and collecting Event Mission PT, you can obtain additional rewards! Unlocking Premium with paid Lodestar Gems allows you to obtain even more luxurious rewards!

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, please check Event > Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※The final part's "Score Battle", "SP Challenge", "Boss Challenge", and "Emblem" will be added at a later date.

※For details about the Event Mission Pass, please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenge.

Rewards are obtained based on the score rank when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" composed of specific characters and memoria.

You can freely organize equipment items and battle items created through synthesis into the rental party.

Try synthesizing items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the rental party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※You can obtain clear rewards even when clearing using a rental party.

※The characters and memoria organized in the rental party cannot be changed. Also, the organized characters and memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess target memoria, the drop amount of synthesis materials increases!

Target memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases based on the liberation status of target memoria.

※Just possessing the target memoria applies the bonus. They do not need to be organized in your party.

※When possessing multiple of the same memoria, the liberation ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 target memoria at liberation rank I, they are calculated as 1 memoria at liberation rank II.
