[08/22 Update] Gifts

In "Gifts", you can give items to specific characters. The characters to whom you can give gifts are determined for each event, and in this event, you can give gifts to Kimon [Devoted Loyalty Ninja] and Eternity [Smooth-Talking Familiar User].

When you give items as gifts, Friendship Points accumulate, and when you obtain a certain amount of Friendship Points, Intimacy increases. By raising intimacy, you will be able to progress through the event story.

The only item you can give to Kimon and Eternity in this event is Supreme Dedication Sake, which can be obtained by clearing event missions in the event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation". When intimacy reaches a certain level, you will be able to read special stories of Kimon and Eternity.

※Items given to Kimon and Eternity through "Gifts" will be consumed.

※Supreme Dedication Sake can only be obtained through event missions in the event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation". It cannot be synthesized, so please be careful.

※If you convert Supreme Dedication Sake, you will not be able to raise the intimacy of Kimon or Eternity for a certain period, and you will not be able to meet the unlock conditions for event stories, so please be careful (it will become obtainable through means other than event missions at a later date).

※Event missions can be checked from Home Screen > Mission > Event > Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation.
