Event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation" Part 2 Added!

Part 2 has been added to the event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation"!

This event allows you to challenge "Score Battle" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials, as well as high-difficulty battles "Boss Challenge" and "SP Challenge".

This "Score Battle" can be challenged by using stamina instead of challenge tickets. When you clear Score Battle, you can obtain Battle Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Battle Coins can be exchanged for Grain of Hope and Memory Fragment at the exchange shop.

Clearing Boss Challenge will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will reward you with Star Prayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Kimon [Devoted Loyalty Ninja] and Eternity [Smooth-Talking Familiar User], as well as ★6 awakening-capable characters (regardless of awakening status) will be powered up!

※This event is divided into Part 1 and Part 2.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, please check from Event > Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Information.

※For details about the event mission pass, please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained according to the score rank when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging this challenging battle!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be challenged using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and memoria equipped.

You can freely equip equipment items and combat items created through synthesis to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the specialty attributes and roles of the characters equipped in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※You can obtain clear rewards even when clearing using the Rental Party.

※The characters and memoria equipped in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the equipped characters and memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When you clear quests, if you possess memoria that are bonus targets, the drop amount of synthesis materials will increase!

Target memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonus can be added up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases depending on the unlock status of target memoria.

※Simply possessing target memoria will apply the bonus. You do not need to equip them in your party.

※If you possess multiple copies of the same memoria, the unlock ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 copies of target memoria at unlock rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at unlock rank II.
