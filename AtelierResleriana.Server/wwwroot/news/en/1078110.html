Wish Overview

"Even When Scattered, the Bond of Friends - What the Shrine Maiden's Eyes Gaze Upon LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" now open!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1 to 4, with STEP 3 having double pickup rates, and STEP 4 allowing you to obtain SSR Memoria "What the Shrine Maiden's Eyes Gaze Upon" with guaranteed acquisition for free!

STEP1

10 wishes for Paid Lodestar Gem×1,500

STEP2

10 wishes for Paid Lodestar Gem×1,500

STEP3

Double pickup rates!

10 wishes for Paid Lodestar Gem×1,500

STEP4

1 wish for free

SSR Memoria "What the Shrine Maiden's Eyes Gaze Upon" guaranteed!

※The step-up wish can be executed for a maximum of 5 cycles from STEP 1 to 4.

※In STEP 4, you will not obtain LEGEND Medals when executing "1 wish".

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when you execute wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES exchange shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up wish STEP 1-3: 8 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free wish in STEP 4.
