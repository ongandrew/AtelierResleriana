How to Play

"Aim for High Score! Lamb Roast-kun Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battles.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your earned score will be displayed in ranking format, with higher rankings earning more rewards.

※This event only offers ranking rewards - there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Aim for High Score! Lamb Roast-kun Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can earn various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they will receive the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

You can check reward details from Event > Aim for High Score! Lamb Roast-kun Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
