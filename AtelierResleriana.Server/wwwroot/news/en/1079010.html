Ver2.6.0 Update Complete Notice

The maintenance for Ver2.6.0 update has been completed.

The update contents are as follows.

Thank you very much for your cooperation during the maintenance.

Please continue to enjoy "Atelier Resleriana".

Update Contents

●Implementation of additional data

●App icon change

●Addition of "Neo Board"

○We have newly added the "Neo Board"

○The Neo Board is a new board that allows you to further strengthen your characters. To unlock the Neo Board, the following conditions must be met

■Development Research Lv40 must be completed

■The character you wish to unlock the Neo Board for must meet the following conditions

1.Has reached Level 90 (excluding bonuses from Development Research)

2.Has unlocked all Grow Board panels up to page XVI

3.Has unlocked all EX Board panels up to page III

○Characters with Neo Board unlocked can no longer have their development reset

●Help updates

○We have updated the following help sections

■Home (Adjusted descriptions regarding Guide Missions)

■Character (Added descriptions regarding Neo Board and adjusted descriptions regarding development reset)

●Guide Mission improvements

○We have removed the rule requiring Guide Missions to be cleared one by one in order, and improved the system so rewards can be received as soon as conditions are met

○Following the above change, Story Missions are now unlocked even without clearing all Guide Missions

●Synthesis result screen improvements

○We have improved the ordering of items on the synthesis result screen to display in the following order: Quality > Rarity > Total Trait Level > Highest Trait Level

●Additional difficulty levels for certain development quests

○We have added the following difficulty levels to EXP Quests

■Difficulty: VI

■Difficulty: VII

○We have added the following difficulty levels to Lightstone Quests

■Each color Lightstone Quest Difficulty: VII

■All colors Lightstone Quest Difficulty: II

●Updates to ongoing event content

○We have updated the following content for the event "Main Story Clear Support Missions"

■We have added missions with the condition of clearing Main Story Chapter 28 "Crisis in the Far East" Part 1

○We have updated the following content for the event "Kurogane Island Monster Subjugation Operation"

■We have added unlimited exchangeable Premium Offering Sake to the event exchange shop (In the event "Scattered Yet Still Blooming, Bonds of Friendship", you can give Premium Offering Sake to Kimon and Eien as presents. The dialogue effects when giving presents change based on affinity levels.)

■We have removed the unlock conditions for Score Battle

●[Advance Notice] Changes to reward content for certain content

○We will be changing the reward content for Weekly Missions (※This will take effect from 2025/09/01 12:00.)

■Supreme Orbs and Hope Particles will become obtainable. Additionally, the quantities of some other items will increase

■Only the Orb×1 that was the reward for "Perform synthesis 30 times" will no longer be obtainable in Weekly Missions due to being replaced with Supreme Orb×7

○We will be changing the regular rewards and premium rewards content for Mission Pass (※This will take effect starting from the September Mission Pass that goes on sale from 2025/09/01 12:00.)

■Hope Particles will become obtainable as regular rewards. Additionally, the quantities of some other items will increase

■Grand Holy Orbs and Hope Particles will become obtainable as premium rewards. Additionally, the quantities of some other items will increase

○We have fixed a bug where "Ice Resistance -25%" applied by Saskia [Spring Wishes]'s Skill 2 "Fortune Ice Blessing Dance" was being applied even to enemies with the status effect "Ice Resistance DOWN Immunity"

●Bug with enemy "Vajra Golem" ability

○We have fixed a bug where the ability "Increased Damage While Broken" of the enemy "Vajra Golem" appearing in Col Quests and other content was only being applied to damage when break occurred (※We have fixed it so that damage is increased while broken.)

●Display bug when welcoming characters with special skins under specific conditions

○We have fixed a bug where the welcome animation would not play when welcoming a character with a special skin if you already had the character with the normal skin

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○We have fixed a bug where the app may forcibly close when using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

●Display bug in recipe inspiration screen while progressing through Main Story Chapter 27

○We have fixed a bug where the background and recipe inspiration effects in the recipe inspiration screen were showing Season 1 versions while progressing through Main Story Chapter 27

●Display bug in character list screen with specific sorting settings

○We have fixed a bug in the character list screen where the display positions of the following characters were incorrect when sorted by name

■Kimon [Loyal Ninja of Deep Faith]

■Eien [Charming Onmyouji]
