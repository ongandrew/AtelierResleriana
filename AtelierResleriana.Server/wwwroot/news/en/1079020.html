Thank you for always enjoying 'Atelier Resleriana'.

●Display issue with Story Chapter 28-10 "Fashion Discussion"

Fixed Issues List

●Display issue with Score Battle list in Material Quests

○Fixed an issue where in Home Screen > Quests > Material Quests, the Score Battle for Main Story Chapter 28 was displayed as "Chapter 27"

●Display issue with Story Chapter 28-10 "Fashion Discussion"

○Fixed an issue in Story Chapter 28-10 "Fashion Discussion" where text display during Mieke's character introduction was partially in an unintended state

●Issue with Wish end notification function under specific conditions

○Fixed an issue where after completing the tutorial, if the tutorial Wish had not been used yet and no other Wishes were displayed, the Wish end notification function would still display a banner on the Home screen

●Display issue with Board Ability names when unlocking Neo Boards

○When unlocking Neo Boards, if a Board Ability rank increases, the displayed Board Ability name shows the name of one rank lower

○If you have already recruited the normal skin version of Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin version of Resna [Pretty Summer] sent to mail via login bonus cannot be bulk received (※It can still be received individually using the receive button at the bottom right of each mail.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, Daily Pass does not include a link for deferred payment options

●Movie playback issues on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback:

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering playback

■Audio desynchronization

■App crashes when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with receipt status in Mail

○Despite receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces via mail, the display in mail does not switch to "received" status (※This is a display-only issue; rewards are properly received.)

●Behavioral issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request - 3," the app may crash at specific timings (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve the situation.)

●Issue when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may crash when viewing video ads (※Rest assured that available viewing count does not decrease. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve the situation.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
