List of Fixed Issues

●[09/02 Addition] Issue with receiving rewards in Guide Missions

○Fixed an issue where in the Guide Missions at the bottom left of the Home screen, when there are uncleared missions before missions where rewards are available to receive, attempting to "Receive All" or individually "Receive" completed mission rewards would display an "Invalid Request" error and return to the title screen

●[09/02 Addition] Issue with progress count for certain missions in the event "Neo Board Release Commemoration Missions"

○Fixed an issue where progress for the following missions in the event "Neo Board Release Commemoration Missions" was not being counted correctly (※If conditions were already met at mission start, they are now fixed to count toward mission progress.)

■Raise ○ characters to level 90

■Raise ○ characters' Grow Boards to XVI

●Issue where the revival event "Encounter of Chaos and Order" may not become complete

○Fixed an issue where the revival event "Encounter of Chaos and Order" may not display as complete even after clearing all event stories and battles (※The following event stories were not properly revived, so they have been fixed to display correctly. Clearing all event stories and battles including those below will result in complete display.)

■[Side Story] Music and Life

■[Side Story] Surprisingly Domestic?

■[Side Story] Competitive

■[Side Story] One Step Ahead

■[Side Story] Cleanup

●Issue with "Ice Resistance DOWN" applied by Saskia [Spring Wishes]

○Fixed an issue where "Ice Resistance -25%" applied by Saskia [Spring Wishes] with Skill 2 "Blessing Ice Celebration Dance" was being applied even to enemies with the status condition "Ice Resistance DOWN Immunity"

●Issue with enemy "Diamond Golem" abilities

○Fixed an issue where the ability "Increased Damage Taken During Break" of the enemy "Diamond Golem" appearing in Call Quests and other content was only applying to damage when break occurred (※Fixed so that damage taken is increased while broken.)

●Display issue when welcoming characters with special skins under specific conditions

○Fixed an issue where the welcome animation would not play when welcoming a character with a special skin if you already owned the character with a regular skin

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Fixed an issue where the app may force-close when using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

●Display issue with recipe inspiration screen while progressing Main Story Chapter 27

○Fixed an issue where the background and recipe inspiration animation of the recipe inspiration screen would show Season 1 content while progressing Main Story Chapter 27

●Display issue in character list screen with specific sorting settings

○Fixed an issue where the display position of the following characters was incorrect when sorted by name in the character list screen

■Kimon [Loyal and Faithful Ninja]

■Eternity [Silver-tongued Shikigami User]

●Display issue with title screen on specific devices

○Fixed an issue where version information at the bottom right of the title screen would blend into the background and become invisible on devices with the following aspect ratios

■4:3

■16:9
