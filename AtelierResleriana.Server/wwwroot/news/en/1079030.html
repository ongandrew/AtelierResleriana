New Board "Neo Board" Added!

A new training element, "Neo Board", has been added!

By unlocking the Neo Board, you can strengthen characters even further.

When you unlock Neo Board panels, not only do stats increase further, but you can also learn Board Abilities.

Unlocking the Neo Board requires new training materials such as Great Sacred Orbs and Ultimate Treasure Orbs.

Once you unlock the Neo Board, training reset cannot be used for that character.

Please unlock the Neo Board to strengthen your characters and challenge various battles!

To commemorate the addition of Neo Board, the Event "Neo Board Release Commemoration Mission" is now live!

We will hold missions where you can earn rewards by clearing missions related to character training.

By clearing missions, you can obtain Great Sacred Orbs and Ultimate Treasure Orbs!

※For Neo Board unlock conditions, please check Home Screen ＞ Menu ＞ Help ＞ Characters.

"Friend Invitation Campaign" Now Live!

To commemorate the 2nd anniversary, the "Friend Invitation Campaign" is now live!

By inviting friends using invitation codes, you can get up to Lodestar Gems ×4,000!

Additionally, friends who accept invitations can also get Lodestar Gems ×1,000.

Take this opportunity to invite your friends and enjoy Atelier Resleriana together!

For invitation codes, please select Home Screen ＞ Event ＞ Friend Invitation Campaign, and you can get them from the "Invitation Code" displayed at the bottom of the screen. You can enter invitation codes using the "Enter Invitation Code" button displayed to the right of the code.

※Players who have already participated in the previously held "Friend Invitation Campaign" can "invite" friends again.

※Players who have been invited in past campaigns can also be "invited" again.

※You cannot accept invitations from someone you invited.
