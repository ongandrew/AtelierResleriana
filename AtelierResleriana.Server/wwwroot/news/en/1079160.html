LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you make wishes using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop ＞ Character Memoria ＞ LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop ＞ Packs ＞ Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted on 2025/03/06 or later can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pick-up SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●1x Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Discovery

When you awaken Kaburaya [Brightest Star on Earth] to ★5, you can discover the recipe for an SSR equipment item that only takes effect when equipped by Kaburaya [Brightest Star on Earth].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be discovered, please check Home Screen ＞ Recipe Discovery ＞ Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe discovery.
