Synthesis! SynthGO! Campaign Now On!

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana."

We are now holding the "Synthesis! SynthGO! Campaign" where you can obtain up to 3,000 Lodestar Gems based on your equipment item synthesis results!

During the campaign period, synthesize equipment items, and if the item synthesis is successful, you'll receive Lodestar Gems according to your synthesis results!

Additionally, if your synthesis count exceeds 300 times during the period, you can obtain 3,000 Lodestar Gems as the "SynthGO! Prize."
You can check your current synthesis count from Home > Missions > Event.

Synthesize your desired equipment items and please participate in this campaign!
