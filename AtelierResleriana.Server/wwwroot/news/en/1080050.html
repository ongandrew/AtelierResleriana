Event "2nd Anniversary Boss Battle" Now Open!

This event features "Mission Battles" and high-difficulty battles called "SP Challenge".

By clearing Mission Battle missions, you can obtain rewards such as Lodestar Gems, Grand Sacred Orbs, and ATELIER FES Wish Tickets.

By clearing SP Challenges, you can obtain Prayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Mission Battles and SP Challenges, characters with Kaburaya [The Brightest Star on Earth], Chitose [Shrine Maiden], and Keiun tags will be powered up!

※For details on character power-ups in SP Challenge, please check Event > 2nd Anniversary Boss Battle > SP Challenge or Mission Battle > Battle Info.

Mission Battle

Mission Battles are battles where you can obtain rewards by clearing multiple missions set for each battle.

There are two types of rewards: mission completion rewards and rewards based on the total number of missions completed.

Aim to clear all battle missions and try various team compositions while challenging the battles!

SP Challenge

SP Challenge features high-difficulty battles that exceed Boss Challenge in difficulty.

Rewards are obtained based on the score rank achieved when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these satisfying battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.
