How to Play

"2nd Anniversary Boss Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in a score attack format.

There is only one battle to challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battles.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your achieved scores will be displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "2nd Anniversary Boss Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" by logging in after the event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will receive the same rank.

Example: If there are 3 users with a 10th place score, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

For details on rewards, please check Event > 2nd Anniversary Boss Battle LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
