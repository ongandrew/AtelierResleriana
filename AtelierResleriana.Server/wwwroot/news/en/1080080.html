Wish Overview

"Cherry Blossoms Blooming Chitose LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now open!

★3 Character Chitose [Shrine Maiden] and SSR Memoria "Sky Full of Cherry Blossoms" make their new debut & are featured!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1-5, with STEP 3 having 2x PU rates, and STEP 5 having 3x PU rates!

Additionally, after completing wishes from STEP 1-5, an additional "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has 2x PU rates!

Furthermore, when you make 10 wishes, you can get Chitose [Shrine Maiden] Pieces×50 as a bonus!

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to perform wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for Memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Discovery

When you awaken Chitose [Shrine Maiden] to ★5, you can discover recipes for SSR equipment items that only take effect when equipped by Chitose [Shrine Maiden].

※For details on discoverable SSR equipment item recipes, please check Home > Recipe Discovery > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe discovery.
