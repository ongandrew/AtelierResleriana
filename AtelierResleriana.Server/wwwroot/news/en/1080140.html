72-Hour Limited! Chapter 3 Clear Commemoration Choose Your SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wish Now Open!

Event Period

72 hours after clearing Main Story Chapter 3

Wish Overview

"72-Hour Limited! Chapter 3 Clear Commemoration Choose Your SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wish" is now open!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 5, where STEP 2 and STEP 5 allow you to make a free "single Wish with guaranteed selected Memoria"!

Additionally, this Wish is limited to 72 hours after clearing Main Story Chapter 3.

If you already meet the conditions when "72-Hour Limited! Chapter 3 Clear Commemoration Choose Your SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wish" begins, the 72-hour limit will start from the moment the banner for this Wish appears on the Home screen after the Wish begins, so please be careful.

STEP1

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems x3,000

STEP2

Free single Wish
Selected SSR Memoria guaranteed!

STEP3

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems x2,000

STEP4

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems x1,500

STEP5

Free single Wish
Selected SSR Memoria guaranteed!

※You can select different Memoria for STEP 2 and STEP 5.

※STEP 1-5 can each be executed only once.

※When executing the "single Wish" in STEP 2 and STEP 5, you will not obtain LEGEND Medals.

※Drop rates can be checked at Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

※Selectable Memoria can be checked at Wish > Selectable Memoria.

※Only STEP 2 and STEP 5 allow you to select guaranteed Memoria. Other STEPs do not allow guaranteed Memoria selection.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you make Wishes using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※The SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly released pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●STEP1: 11 medals

●STEP3: 8 medals

●STEP4: 6 medals

※The free single Wishes in STEP 2 and 5 do not grant LEGEND Medals.
