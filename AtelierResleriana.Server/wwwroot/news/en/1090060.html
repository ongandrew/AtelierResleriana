New Feature "Chapter Skip" Added!

Thank you for always enjoying 'Atelier Resleriana'.

We are now adding the new feature "Chapter Skip"!

Chapter Skip

The "Chapter Skip" feature is a function that allows you to skip chapters in the main story by chapter units, up to two chapters before the latest chapter. Additionally, for the first time, it is possible to skip all at once up to Main Story Chapter 24, which marks the beginning of Season 2.

When you skip chapters, the skipped chapters are treated as cleared, fulfilling mission conditions and other requirements. Additionally, for some chapters, limited-time revival Wishes lasting 72 hours will be held.

This feature can be used via the "Skip Chapter" button displayed above the Story button on the Home screen.

After pressing the "Skip Chapter" button, a confirmation screen will appear.

Selecting "Skip" on the confirmation screen will skip the chapter.

Additionally, with the addition of the "Chapter Skip" feature, we have added chapter summaries to Home Screen > Menu > Story Recollection > Review.

※The "Skip Chapter" button will only be displayed when you are progressing through chapters that can be skipped.
