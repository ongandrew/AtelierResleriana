Event "Mysteries and Mechanisms Island" Part 1 Now Open!

Event "Mysteries and Mechanisms Island" Part 1 is now open!

This event is divided into Part 1 and Part 2. Part 2 will be added at a later date.

The event story for this event can be read by clearing event missions and collecting Event PT. Additionally, choices will appear during the event story, and depending on the choice you select, the story may branch into IF routes.

Furthermore, reading Event Story Chapter 1 will allow you to recruit ★1 Jelka [Nine Greats No.3].

★1 Jelka [Nine Greats No.3] has some skills locked in her initial state. Locked skills cannot be used in battle, so let's unlock them using items that can be obtained within the event!

Additionally, you can challenge "Score Battle" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as high-difficulty battles "Boss Challenge" and "SP Challenge".

This "Score Battle" can be challenged using stamina instead of challenge tickets. When you clear Score Battle, you can obtain Battle Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Grand Sacred Orbs, Lodestar Gems, Hope Particles, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will reward you with Star Prayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Jelka [Nifty Rover], Chitose [Shrine Maiden], and characters with the Nine Greats tag are powered up!

Furthermore, in this event, an Event Mission Pass will be sold! By clearing event missions and collecting Event Mission PT, you can obtain additional rewards! If you unlock Premium with paid Lodestar Gems, you can obtain even more luxurious rewards!

※Only some stories have choices.

※Even if you select choices that lead to IF routes, you can read the next story by replaying the story and selecting different choices.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, please check Event > Mysteries and Mechanisms Island > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Information.

※Part 2 event stories and other content will be added at a later date.

※For details about the Event Mission Pass, please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank achieved when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and Memoria pre-configured.

You can freely configure synthesis-created equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the attribute specialties and roles of the characters in the Rental Party to challenge Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria configured in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the configured characters and Memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess bonus target Memoria, the drop amount of synthesis materials increases!

Target Memoria and bonus amounts can be checked at Event > Mysteries and Mechanisms Island > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amounts increase based on the unlock status of target Memoria.

※Bonuses apply just by possessing target Memoria. They do not need to be configured in your party.

※If you possess multiple copies of the same Memoria, bonus amounts increase by combining unlock ranks (maximum V).

Example: If you possess 2 copies of target Memoria at unlock rank I, it is calculated as 1 copy of Memoria at unlock rank II.
