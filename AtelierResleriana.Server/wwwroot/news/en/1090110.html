Wish Overview

The Nine Greats Chronicle: One Who Lives in Style Jelka LEGEND FES [Paid Only] is now open!

★3 Character Jelka [Nifty Rover] and SSR Memoria "Savoring Style" make their debut and are featured!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 5, where STEP 3 has PU rate 2x, and STEP 5 has PU rate 3x!

Additionally, after wishing from STEP 1 to 5, a "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has PU rate 2x!

Furthermore, when you wish 10 times, you can obtain Jelka [Nifty Rover] Pieces x50 as a bonus!

●STEP 1-5 Step-up Wish

STEP3

PU rate 2x!

10 Wishes for Paid Lodestar Gems x1,500

STEP4

STEP5

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Missions > Events > Jelka [Nifty Rover] Release Commemoration Mission.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you execute Wishes using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that newly appeared from 2025/03/06 onwards can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish period. ★3 Characters that appeared before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly released pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Jelka [Nifty Rover] to ★5, you can gain inspiration for SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Jelka [Nifty Rover].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
