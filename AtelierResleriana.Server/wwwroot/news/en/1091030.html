List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug in the rerun event "Sparkling Sekirara Star Festival!" where players could access rental party formation under specific conditions

○Fixed a bug in the rerun event "Sparkling Sekirara Star Festival!" boss challenge where, when a party was defeated, a hint dialog would appear encouraging players to try with a rental party and allow them to access the formation screen, despite rental parties not being available in rerun events

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[09/25 Update]Display bug when Liberation Rank V Memoria are sent via mail

○When Liberation Rank V Memoria are sent via mail, the Liberation Rank is displayed as I on the mail screen (※This is only a display bug on that specific screen; the Memoria can be properly received in Liberation Rank V state.)

●[09/19 Update]Bug where status effect totals are not displayed correctly under specific conditions

○Under the following conditions, status effect totals are not displayed correctly

■When unique status effects that increase in potency based on level are applied, the Lv1 effect amount is reflected in the status effect total regardless of the applied level

■The effect amount of battle item status effects that increase damage and critical damage are incorrectly added to the status effect total as skill damage and critical damage increases

●[09/19 Update]Display bug in enemy information for enemies with multiple break gauges

○For enemies with multiple break gauges, when viewing enemy information and switching break gauge information using the button to the right of the break gauge, the "×2" and "×1" labels on the button are displayed overlapping

●Display bug in Board Ability names when unlocking Neo Board

○When unlocking Neo Board and a Board Ability rank increases, the displayed Board Ability name shows the name from one rank lower

●Bug preventing batch collection of tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] from login bonus under specific conditions

○When already owning the regular skin Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin Resna [Pretty Summer] sent via mail as a login bonus cannot be collected in batches (※It can be collected individually using the receive button at the bottom right of each mail.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for deferred payment options

●Movie playback bugs on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following bugs may occur during movie playback

■Degraded video quality

■Stuttering playback

■Audio desynchronization

■Application crashes when playing movies repeatedly

●Display bug for mail receipt status

○Despite receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces via mail, the display within the mail does not change to show as received (※This is only a display bug; rewards are properly received.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the application may crash at specific times (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Video advertisement viewing bug on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the application may crash when viewing video advertisements (※Please be assured that viewing counts are not reduced. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other bugs as soon as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
