LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When using Lodestar Gems to perform wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Characters/Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR memorias that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-pull Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals with Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Goo [Faithful Fighter] to ★5, you can be inspired with recipes for SSR equipment items that only take effect when equipped by Goo [Faithful Fighter].

※For details on SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
