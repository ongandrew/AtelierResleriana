Wish Overview

The "Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES" is now open!

SSR Memoria "For Those I Believe In" and SSR Memoria "For My Given Duty" make their debut & are featured!

Wish Tickets

Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES Exclusive) can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems x1,500 and LEGEND Medals x25 respectively, available in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make 1 Wish.

※Wishes made using Wish Tickets do not grant LEGEND Medals.

※Wish Tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES Exclusive) cannot be used for Wishes other than "Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets that exceed their usage period will disappear.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange

When using Lodestar Gems to make Wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medals tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memorias available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※Wish Tickets do not grant LEGEND Medals.
