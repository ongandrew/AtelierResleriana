KOEI TECMO LIVE! in TGS2025 Broadcast Announcement!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We are pleased to announce that we will be broadcasting "KOEI TECMO LIVE! in TGS2025"!

In the Atelier Resleriana × DOAXVV collaboration livestream segment, we will reveal the latest information about the collaboration event scheduled to begin at the end of September.

MC Haruka Mori will be joined by Saho Shirasuna, who voices Resna in Atelier Resleriana, and Mai Aizawa, who voices Marie Rose in DOAXVV. Additionally, Naomi Ozora, who voices Heidi and Patricia in Atelier Resleriana and Kanna in DOAXVV, will also appear!

The broadcast will introduce the start date and event content for the collaboration events being held in both Atelier Resleriana and DOAXVV! Please be sure to join us for the live broadcast!

Furthermore, those who post their thoughts on X (formerly Twitter) during the live broadcast using #レスレリぶいぶい and #KTGS2025 hashtags together will have a chance to win prizes!

Please post and help liven up the live broadcast!
