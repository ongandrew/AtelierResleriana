'The Crimson Alchemist and the White Guardian ~Atelier Resleriana~' Released Today!

Thank you for always enjoying 'Atelier Resleriana'.

Today, Friday, September 26th, we have released 'The Crimson Alchemist and the White Guardian ~Atelier Resleriana~' (PlayStation®5/PlayStation®4/Nintendo Switch™/Steam® game software)!

'The Crimson Alchemist and the White Guardian ~Atelier Resleriana~' is the latest entry in the "Atelier" series, sharing the same world as "Atelier Resleriana" but featuring a new protagonist and new story.

The story follows two protagonists, Rias and Slay, who once lost their homeland to disaster, as they meet and seek to rebuild their hometown and uncover the truth behind the disaster.

In addition to the traditional "Atelier" series systems such as highly strategic timeline command battles and simple yet deep "synthesis," this title is packed with unique elements including "shop management" where you can sell items made through "synthesis," and the layered dungeon "Subspace Path" where the map and monsters change each time you challenge it.

We have also released a launch trailer.

Please enjoy this title featuring Rias and Slay, along with many beloved characters from previous "Atelier" series games!
