New Feature "Character Home" Added!

Thank you for always enjoying 『Atelier Resleriana』.

This time, we have added the new "Character Home" feature!

The "Character Home" feature is a home screen customization feature that allows you to display your favorite character from those you have welcomed prominently on the home screen. In addition to characters, you can set 4 types of customizations: background, motion, camera, and BGM.

This feature can be accessed from Home Screen > Home Background Selection > Custom Scene.

Select the character you want to display on the home screen from the character change screen, then press the "Home Edit" button to switch to the editing screen.

Additionally, characters can be favorited by pressing the "Favorite OFF" button to register them as favorites, making them easier to find when sorting. Characters you want to try various customizations with should be registered as favorites.

After pressing the "Home Edit" button, you can customize the background, motion, camera, and BGM while viewing the actual scene.

Pressing the "Confirm" button saves the edited scene as a Custom Scene to the home background selection screen.

You can switch the home screen by selecting the created custom scene on the home background selection screen and pressing the "Confirm" button. Additionally, created custom scenes can be readjusted using the "Home Edit" or "Character Change" buttons.
