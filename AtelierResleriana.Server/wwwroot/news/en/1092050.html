How to Play Roman Grand Tournament Venus Festival Part 1

"Roman Grand Tournament Venus Festival Part 1" is an event where you play in special battles where the amount of enemy HP you reduce becomes your score.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your obtained score will be displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※Rewards for this event are ranking rewards only, with no score rewards or clear rewards.

※The exchange shop for this event is the "Roman Grand Tournament" exchange shop.
