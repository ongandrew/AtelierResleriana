Wish Overview

"Atelier Summer 2024 Wishlist Wish" is starting!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1~4, where STEP 4 offers a free "Single Wish with Selected Character Guaranteed"!

In STEP 4's "Single Wish with Selected Character Guaranteed," you can choose one from among the ★3 characters Resna [Pretty Summer], Ryza [Sunlit Beach], Valeria [Radiant Summer], and Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] and welcome them guaranteed.

STEP 1

10 Wishes for 3,000 Lodestar Gems

STEP 2

10 Wishes for 3,000 Lodestar Gems

STEP 3

10 Wishes for 3,000 Lodestar Gems

STEP 4

Free single Wish
Your selected character from ★3 characters Resna [Pretty Summer], Ryza [Sunlit Beach], Valeria [Radiant Summer], Klaudia [Moonlit Beach] is guaranteed!

※You can execute STEP 1~4 up to 4 cycles maximum.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when executing the "Single Wish with Selected Character Guaranteed" in STEP 4.

※You can select characters only in STEP 4. Characters cannot be selected in other STEPs.

※Selectable characters can be checked from Wish > Selectable Characters.

※In STEPs other than STEP 4, you can welcome characters and Memoria shown in the drop rates by probability.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES exchange shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly introduced after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are newly introduced pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●10 Wishes in STEP 1~3: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free single Wish in STEP 4.
