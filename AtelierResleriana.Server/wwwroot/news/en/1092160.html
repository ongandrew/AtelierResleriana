Marie Rose Profile

A "little" devil servant who serves the leader of the conglomerate "DOATEC".

She accompanied her leader on vacation and visited Venus Islands. However, being treated like a child became the catalyst for her to strive to win first place in the "Venus Festival" to be recognized as an adult.

She has a somewhat aloof personality, but she also has age-appropriate aspects, such as her emotions showing on the surface when she's happy.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you execute Wishes using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that newly appeared after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR memoria that can be exchanged are newly appeared pickup SSR memoria from the currently held LEGEND FES and SSR memoria that are memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1~4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals
