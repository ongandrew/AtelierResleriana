What is Roman Grand Fighting Tournament?

A martial arts tournament hosted by the adventurer Roman.

Adventurers and strong warriors from across the nation take on monster subjugation challenges to compete in their martial prowess.

The tournament attracts tremendous attention, and its champion is widely celebrated.

How to Play Roman Grand Fighting Tournament

Roman Grand Fighting Tournament is a special battle where the amount of enemy HP you reduce becomes your score!

Your earned score is displayed in ranking format, and you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking.

There are 3 difficulty levels, and at the highest difficulty, enemies with infinite HP appear, allowing you to attack many times within the specified turns to increase your score!

Roman Grand Fighting Tournament is updated periodically, and rankings are reset!

Additionally, the effective attributes for battle change with each update.

※Roman Grand Fighting Tournaments with the same effective attributes as previously held tournaments may be held again. In such cases, your previously obtained maximum score will carry over.

※Exchange shop exchange status and Grand Fighting Tournament Coin holdings carry over even after renewals and updates.

About Ranking Rewards

You can obtain various rewards based on your Roman Grand Fighting Tournament ranking position!

Ranking rewards are distributed via "Mail" after logging in when each effective attribute Roman Grand Fighting Tournament event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will all receive the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.
