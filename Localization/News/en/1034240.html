Letter from the Development Team vol.12

Thank you very much for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

This is Hosoi, producer of "Atelier Resleriana".

On September 23, 2024, "Resleri" will proudly celebrate its 1st anniversary. This is all thanks to your continued support. I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude once again.

Looking back on the approximately one year of operation, especially at the beginning of the service, we were unable to fully meet what players were expecting from "Resleri" due to our own shortcomings.

I deeply reflect on and apologize for causing you concern.

In this year's February-March half-anniversary event and subsequent updates, we have worked on various functional improvements based on your feedback and requests. Furthermore, with the update accompanying last month's swimsuit event, we have completely revised our operational policy to improve your "Resleri" experience.

All of these efforts have been undertaken by the development team to express our desire for players to enjoy "Resleri" and our gratitude to those who have been playing.

Now, as we reach this special 1st anniversary milestone for "Resleri," we have prepared update content that responds to numerous requests you've made to enhance your enjoyment. We would like to announce the updates implemented on August 30, 2024, and the content planned for updates in September as follows.

※This includes the same content that was announced during the August 30, 2024 livestream.

First, let's introduce the updates based on the most common player requests.

▼Recap of update content based on numerous requests

〇Addition of Daily Pass

We received many requests for products that make it easier to obtain "Star Guide Stones,"

so we've added a Daily Pass to the shop that allows you to receive Star Guide Stones each day you log in for 30 days after purchase.

〇Adjustments to make synthesis easier, such as obtaining materials from Score Battles and required recipe materials

To make it easier to gather synthesis materials without having to clear dungeons, we've adjusted Score Battles so that all synthesis materials for recipes that can be conceived in the Main Story can be obtained there. For Chapter 12 and beyond, we've adjusted Normal difficulty Score Battles to provide all synthesis materials needed for Main Story recipes. We've also revised exploration so that the addition of destinations corresponds to Main Story progress, and the increase in obtainable synthesis materials corresponds to Score Battle clear status. Additionally, we've reduced the number of synthesis material types required for some recipes to decrease the number of quests that need to be cleared to synthesize a single recipe.

With this adjustment, dungeons in material quests will be discontinued around the end of October and later renewed as a different type of content. Ahead of the dungeon discontinuation, we've changed the mission conditions for player experience that was set as a reward for clearing Danger Level X dungeons to be obtainable by clearing specific Main Story chapters, and we've also changed the conception conditions for some recipes. Similarly, we've made it possible to earn player experience under the same conditions for Chapter 12 and beyond, where Danger Level X dungeons were not yet added.

〇Difficulty setting feature for battles in Main Story

In response to feedback that "enemies are too difficult to progress through the Main Story," it will be possible to set the difficulty for battles in the Main Story. The difficulty can be selected from "Normal," "Easy," or "Very Easy," and when set to "Easy" or "Very Easy," the stats of enemies appearing in battles will be reduced according to each setting.

※This feature is scheduled to be implemented in late September.

〇Home screen setting feature

In response to requests to "freely set certain scenes unlocked through story progression as the home screen," a feature to configure this will be added in an update.

※As before, you can also set the home screen to change automatically with story progression.

※This feature is scheduled to be implemented in late September.

○Interaction feature

In response to requests to "experience more stories with character interactions," an "Interaction" feature will be added where you can enjoy stories with characters you've obtained. Each month, several characters will be added to the "Interaction" feature.

※This feature is scheduled to be implemented in late September.

▼Recap of other update content announced in the livestream

〇Reruns of Wishes added with story chapters up to Chapter 11 available when clearing chapters

Wishes that appeared when Chapters 4-11 were added have been rerun, and you can wish on them for 72 hours after clearing the corresponding chapter.

※Players who have already cleared these chapters at the time of the update can access them for 72 hours from the banner display on the home screen.

〇Guide Missions renewed! Up to 110 free Wishes!

Guide Missions have been renewed to a format where you clear missions one by one. Missions and their corresponding rewards have also been added. They are divided into "Guide Missions" for up to Chapter 3 and "Story Missions" for Chapter 4 and beyond. By completing all Guide Missions, you can earn a total of 30 ATELIER FES Wish Tickets, and by clearing each chapter's Story Missions, you can earn 10 tickets for each chapter's rerun Wish, for a total of 110 Wish Tickets.

※Players who had already cleared Guide Missions before the renewal cannot receive the rewards for those missions again, but can clear and receive rewards for newly added missions.

○Renewed 72-hour limited! ★3 Character Select Guaranteed Wish

The "72-hour limited! ★3 Character Select Guaranteed Wish" has been renewed and split into "Wave 1," which starts when you clear the Prologue, and "Wave 2," which starts when you clear Chapter 8. In STEP 2 and STEP 5 of each Wish, you can obtain your selected ★3 character with a guarantee.

"Wave 1" allows you to select from ★3 characters that appeared from release until the "Arland Alchemists LEGEND FES" held on 2024/02/20, while "Wave 2" allows you to select from ★3 characters that appeared from the "Decisive Battle LEGEND FES Awakened Calamity" held on 2024/02/28 until the "Twilight Development Team LEGEND FES" held on 2024/06/21.

※With this renewal, even players who had already wished on the previous version can access these for 72 hours after meeting the conditions.

○Improvement to set equipment for characters by party

We've improved the system to allow setting equipment for characters by party. With this improvement, you can equip the same item on different characters in different parties.

○Equipment set feature

We've improved the system to save combinations of Memoria, weapons, armor, and accessories as "My Sets" that can be loaded when setting character equipment on the party screen.

○Item conversion screen improvement

We've improved the item conversion screen layout to allow many items to be viewed at once. We've also added a "Maximum Trait Level" condition to the filter settings.

○Addition of Main Story Chapter 16

We've added Main Story Chapter 16. Along with this, we've added mission content and rewards for the Main Story Consecutive Update Clear Support Missions. The Main Story will continue to be updated consecutively until October. The battle between Resna, Valeria, and the Extreme Night Alchemy Party is reaching its climax, so we hope you continue to enjoy it.

○1st Anniversary Event

We're holding events such as the "1st Anniversary Present Battle Wave 1," where you can exchange for an SSR Memoria of your choice by clearing 5 days' worth of quests, and the item challenge "Master's Masterpiece! Lara's Exhibition!" where you can earn up to 1,500 Star Guide Stones.

○KT App Exchange Item Renewal

To commemorate the 1st anniversary of the "KOEI TECMO App," we've renewed the exchange items. We've completely refreshed the lineup and added 6 types of items including "Memory Fragments" and "Enhancement Drops III." Please connect with the KT App and take advantage of these items.

▼Details of updates that couldn't be fully covered in the livestream

○Recipe Conception screen improvement

We've improved the recipe conception screen layout to allow many items to be viewed at once. We've also improved the system to allow receiving rewards for the number of conceived recipes all at once. With this change, we've modified the rewards for the number of conceived recipes in the Attribute Tower from being for the entire Attribute Tower to being for each individual Attribute Tower.

○Emblem screen improvement

We've improved the Emblem screen to show the progress of emblem acquisition condition requirements.

○Shop screen improvement

We've improved the shop screen layout to have 4 tabs: "Recommended," "Limited Time," "Chapter," and "Exchange Shop."

○Adjustment to natural Mana recovery limit

We've adjusted the natural Mana recovery limit from 10 to 20.

○Addition of items that can restore Mana: "Bottled Mana" and "Mana Fragment"

We've added "Bottled Mana" and "Mana Fragment" as items that can restore Mana. Each will be distributed as rewards for events and other activities, and "Mana Fragment" has an expiration date.

In addition to the above, we are continuing to make improvements and considerations based on your feedback to make "Resleri" more enjoyable.

We will continue to do our best to develop a good game together with you, so thank you for your continued support of "Atelier Resleriana".

Producer

Junzo Hosoi (KOEI TECMO GAMES Co., Ltd.)
