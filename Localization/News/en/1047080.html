▼About new features in the event "Sparkly Shiny Star Birthday Festival!"

〇Addition of the concurrent event "Legend Challenge"

We will add a ranking-based concurrent event called "Legend Challenge".

This is an updated version of the traditional "EX Challenge" as a ranking-format event, and is planned to be held simultaneously with events starting from the "Sparkly Shiny Star Birthday Festival!" event. Similar to the "Roman's Grand Martial Arts Tournament," rewards will be available based on your ranking after the event ends, and the higher your rank, the more rewards such as Star Guidance Stones you can obtain, so please give it a try.

Also, with the addition of "Legend Challenge," some future events will feature both Score Battles to collect synthesis materials and high-difficulty Boss Challenges, and Emblems can be obtained by meeting conditions such as clearing Boss Challenges.

○Addition of "Memoria Bonus" to Event Score Battles

We will add a "Memoria Bonus" to Event Score Battles.

Based on the ownership status and release rank of bonus-eligible Memoria, the drop amount of synthesis materials in Event Score Battles will increase. With this bonus, it will be easier than ever to collect synthesis materials in events.

Bonus-eligible SSR Memoria may include those that have appeared in the past, and in such cases, they can be exchanged at the LEGEND Medal Exchange during the period. Until now, SSR Memoria eligible for exchange with LEGEND Medals were limited to those newly introduced in Wishes, but to make this feature more accessible, past SSR Memoria will also be exchangeable with LEGEND Medals during the bonus eligibility period.

○Addition of "Rental Party" to Event Boss Challenges

We will add a new feature called "Rental Party" to Event Boss Challenges.

We have received many comments saying that high-difficulty battles are difficult or that players don't know what kind of party composition to use. Therefore, we are adding a feature that allows you to rent parties composed of characters and Memoria suitable for clearing in future high-difficulty battles in events, except for some ultra-high-difficulty battles like CHARISMA.

The rewards you can obtain will not change even if you use a Rental Party, so please actively use this feature when you find clearing difficult. Also, equipment items and battle items will be composed from the items you own. When you're unsure about which items to synthesize for quest clearing, we recommend focusing on items that match the Rental Party.

▼Other update contents

○"Recipe Pickup" feature

To make synthesis more accessible, we will add a feature to the recipe selection screen that highlights specific recipes for a limited time and applies bonuses during synthesis.

When synthesizing with a pickup target recipe, the probability of creating SSR rarity items and having high-level traits applied will increase. The pickup target recipes will change after a certain period.

This concludes the content we are announcing at this time. We will continue to make improvements and consider ways to make "Resleriana" more enjoyable for our users based on your feedback.

We will continue to strive to develop a better game together with our users, so we ask for your continued support of "Atelier Resleriana".

Producer

Junzo Hosoi (KOEI TECMO GAMES CO., LTD.)
