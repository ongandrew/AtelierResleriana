Letter from the Development Team vol.17

Thank you very much for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

This is Hosoi, the producer of "Atelier Resleriana".

Belatedly, Happy New Year to everyone.

This year, the entire development team will continue to dedicate ourselves to the service operation of "Atelier Resleriana." We will strive to deliver your satisfaction, so we look forward to your continued support this year.

In January, we held the event "Happy New Year! Treasure Dungeon!" We hope it has helped you advance the awakening of your favorite characters.

Additionally, on 2025/01/17, we added Main Story Chapter 22 Part 1. Resna's journey continues even after overcoming a major milestone, so for those who haven't caught up with the latest story yet, please progress through the story.

Now, in this letter, we will introduce the event that started on 2025/01/24, the Main Story Chapter 22 Part 2 scheduled to be added on 2025/01/27, and more.

▼Event "Walking Toward the Future with a Best Friend" Now Live

Since 2025/01/24, the event "Walking Toward the Future with a Best Friend" has been live.

This event features characters from "Atelier Firis: The Alchemist and the Mysterious Journey." In the simultaneously running "Walking Toward the Future with a Best Friend Firis LEGEND FES," the new costume ★3 Firis【Twinning Ribbon】 has made her debut, so please welcome her.

Furthermore, in the Wish scheduled for 2025/01/31, a certain character from "Atelier Firis" will make their new appearance, so please look forward to that as well.

▼Addition of Main Story Chapter 22 Part 2 and Upcoming Main Story

On 2025/01/27 at 12:00, we will add Main Story Chapter 22 Part 2. Please enjoy the latest main story.

Regarding the recent main story, as the narrative is heading toward its climax, some may be concerned that "the story might be coming to an end soon," but rest assured that Resna and friends' story will continue.

It will take some time until the next main story addition, but we are diligently working on Main Story Chapter 23.

We will continue to update the main story regularly, so we hope you look forward to future main story developments.

▼"SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wishlist Wish [New Star]" Now Available

From 2025/01/27 at 12:00, the "SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wishlist Wish [New Star]" will be available.

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 4, and at STEP 4, you can make a "free single Wish that guarantees 1 Memoria from your selection." The Memoria that can be selected at STEP 4 are the 7 SSR Memoria that were newly added to the regular LEGEND FES lineup on 2025/01/17. Please check the in-game announcement scheduled for that day for more details.

▼Addition of Star Prayer Stone Crown (Memoria)

Also on 2025/01/27 at 12:00, we will add "Star Prayer Stone Crown (Memoria)" to the Star Prayer Stone exchange.

This is a Star Prayer Stone exchange that offers only Memoria. The drop rate for SSR Memoria is 60%, and the Memoria featured in the aforementioned "SSR Memoria Guaranteed Wishlist Wish [New Star]" are also included in this lineup.

We plan to hold a sale where you'll receive bonus "Star Prayer Stone Petals (Memoria)" consumed during exchanges, so please check the in-game announcement scheduled for that day for more details.


This concludes the information we wanted to share with you this time.

In March 2025, "Atelier Resleriana" will celebrate its 1.5-year anniversary. This is all thanks to your continued support. The entire development team would like to express our sincere gratitude.

We are planning a major update for the 1.5-year anniversary that we hope you will enjoy. We are working on this release, so please look forward to it.

We will continue to strive to develop a better game together with all of our users, so please continue to support "Atelier Resleriana."
