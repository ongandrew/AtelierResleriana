Changes from this Wish

For characters newly appearing after the start of Season 2, the following changes will be reflected from this Wish.

●The LEGEND Medal exchange period for characters newly appearing after this Wish will be the "Wish period." Even if they become permanently available in future Wishes, they will not be added to the LEGEND Medal exchange.

●The number of LEGEND Medals required to exchange for characters newly appearing after this Wish will be 250.

●When awakening a character that newly appears after this Wish to ★5, you will be able to discover SSR equipment recipes (※there is no time limit for discovering recipes).

●The content and rewards of the new character debut celebration missions will change.

●We will end the sale of awakening packs for newly appearing characters (※awakening packs that have already been released will continue to be sold).

The details of these changes will be explained in the upcoming "Letter from the Development Team vol.19." Thank you for your understanding.

Wish Overview

"Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES" is now live!

★3 character Alvina and Memoria "Leave It to Alvi-chan!" have been added!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES) is now available in the shop!

※During the event period, if you welcome a pickup character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Shards ×50.

Pickup Selection

In this Wish, you can select one of the pickup characters or memorias to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a drop rate of 1%, and the unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

The pickup character and pickup memoria that you want to increase the drop rate for can be selected from Wish > Target Pickup.

Bond Story

We have added a bond story to Alvina [Dream of Hero]. In bond stories, you can enjoy interactions between various characters, and by completing the story, you can unlock a "Memorial Scene" that can be set on the home screen.

Bond stories can be played from Atelier > Bond.

※The "Friendship Key" item required to unlock bond stories can be obtained as rewards from weekly missions and other sources.

Alvina [Dream of Hero] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate the debut of Alvina [Dream of Hero], we are holding the limited-time Alvina [Dream of Hero] Debut Celebration Mission!

Based on the number of times you Wish on "Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES" or "Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can obtain up to 445 Alvina [Dream of Hero] pieces! Other items can also be obtained.

This is your chance to immediately awaken Alvina [Dream of Hero] after welcoming her! Don't miss it!

※Missions will end on 2025/03/13 11:59. Please note that this is shorter than the period for "Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES" and "Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Missions > Events > Alvina [Dream of Hero] Debut Celebration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes made using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive to Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES) cannot be used for Wishes other than "Season 2 Launch! New Guide Alvina LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets that have expired will be lost.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can earn LEGEND Medals when you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the Wish period. Characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added pickup SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can earn is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 Medal

●10x Wish: 11 Medals

※You cannot earn LEGEND Medals from Wishes made using Wish Tickets.

When you awaken Alvina [Dream of Hero] to ★5, you can discover a recipe for an SSR equipment item that is effective only when equipped by Alvina [Dream of Hero].

Take on the limited-time "Alvina [Dream of Hero] Debut Celebration Mission" and Piece Quests to work toward awakening to ★5!
