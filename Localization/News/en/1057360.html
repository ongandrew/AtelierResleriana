▼Recap of updates related to improved playability and new development elements

〇Updates to make synthesis more playable

We have received many requests to make "synthesis," a representative element of the series, more user-friendly. In response, we have prepared two updates: "Rental Synthesis" and "Trait Activators."

【Rental Synthesis】

"Rental Synthesis" is a feature that allows you to rent characters you don't have up to 10 times per day when synthesizing. The characters available for rental are those included in the top three formations displayed in the popular formation ranking. The popular formation feature was originally added for those who felt it was difficult to understand "what combinations to use for synthesis," but as the number of available characters has increased significantly, it has become more common to be unable to recreate formations due to lacking certain characters.

Therefore, by introducing "Rental Synthesis," we've made it possible for those with fewer characters to still perform synthesis up to 10 times daily by borrowing characters. Synthesis materials cannot be borrowed, so please challenge events and material quests to obtain them.

【Trait Activators】

Trait Activators are items that make it easier to add the traits they contain to synthesis items when used during synthesis.

We're introducing these to address the issue many of you have voiced regarding synthesis difficulty, specifically that it's "hard to add desired traits."

Trait Activators are generated with a certain probability when synthesizing any item, containing traits that were candidates for being added to the item. They're designed to naturally accumulate as you synthesize various items. Please try this feature out.


〇Addition of new character development elements

As new character development elements, we're enabling "★6 Awakening" and "Skill Evolution" for some already released characters.

【★6 Awakening】

When awakening to ★6, not only will character stats and Burst Skill levels increase as with conventional awakenings, but the icon will change in character selection screens, and you'll be able to devise UR equipment item recipes.

Additionally, "Skill Evolution" will become possible. Not only will skill effects and power improve, but evolving all three skills—Skill 1, Skill 2, and Burst Skill—will allow you to acquire a new ability.

More characters will be added to the ★6 Awakening roster in the future.

〇【Important】About future characters

With the addition of ★6 Awakening mentioned above, new characters appearing in the future will have status comparable to characters awakened to ★6.

Regarding this, as Season 2 characters will have more powerful stats than previously released characters, we wanted to avoid a situation where Season 1 characters would appear relatively less appealing, creating a gap. After extensive internal discussions, we decided to implement the following changes.

【Changes to LEGEND Medal】

●For new characters appearing in Season 2 and beyond, the LEGEND medal exchange period will be the same as the gacha period.

●For new characters appearing in Season 2 and beyond, 250 LEGEND medals will be required for exchange.

●In the paid-only limited gacha for new characters appearing in Season 2 and beyond, the number of LEGEND medals acquired in STEP1 and STEP2 will increase from 6 to 8.

●There will be no changes for characters that appeared before February 28, 2025.

【Changes to Pieces】

●For new characters appearing in Season 2 and beyond, we will discontinue the sale of awakening packs. As an alternative, we will add a new Daily > Star Stone tab to the shop, where pieces for characters currently featured in pickup gachas will be available.

●We will not remove the awakening packs already on sale, which will remain available for purchase for 72 hours after welcoming a character. Additionally, we plan to sell awakening packs for characters that can be awakened to ★6. Awakening packs for characters that can be awakened to ★6 after the March 6, 2025 maintenance are now available for purchase.

●We have revised the character debut commemoration missions, allowing you to obtain up to 445 pieces based on the number of times you pull the gacha for a new character.

Regarding these changes, we will continue to listen to your feedback and consider adjustments to provide greater satisfaction.

We appreciate your understanding.

▼Details of update content that could not be fully covered during the livestream

○Making Interaction Story Episode 1 viewable without owning the character

We have made it possible to unlock Episode 1 of Interaction Stories using friendship keys even if you don't own the character. This makes it easier to enjoy the beginning of each character's interaction story.


○Research Level Cap Increase

We have increased the level cap for Battle Research and Alchemy Research to level 200.

With this increase, we have added new achievements to Mission > Achievements, so please challenge them.


○Removal of Grow Board Unlock Conditions

We have removed the unlock conditions for the Grow Board.

This allows you to develop characters up to level 90 regardless of your Main Story progress. By collecting light orbs of each color through Light Orb Quests and events, you can challenge Secret Quests, Event Boss Challenges, and more.


○Shop Screen Renovation

We have renovated the shop screen layout to include 5 tabs: "Recommended," "Packs," "Daily," "Exchange," and "Character/Memoria."

This renovation is designed to make it easier to find items or products you're looking for in the shop. We will continue to consider improvements for unclear aspects in future updates.
