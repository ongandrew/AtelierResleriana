How to Play

"Citrine's Handyman Challenge - LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in a score attack format.

There is only one battle available to challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battles.

Challenge repeatedly to aim for higher scores!

Your obtained scores will be displayed in ranking format, and higher ranks will receive more rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards. There are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Citrine's Handyman Challenge - LEGEND CHALLENGE", you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will be placed in the same rank.

Example: If 3 users have the score for 10th place, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be checked from Events > Citrine's Handyman Challenge - LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Rewards List.
