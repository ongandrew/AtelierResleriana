Rental Party

You can challenge the Event Boss Challenge using a "Rental Party" with pre-selected characters and memoria.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the elemental affinities and roles of the characters in the Rental Party, and take on the Boss Challenge!

※You can still obtain clear rewards even when using a Rental Party.

※You cannot change the characters and memoria in the Rental Party. Additionally, the characters and memoria available in the Rental Party will vary depending on the event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you own bonus target memoria, the drop amount of synthesis materials will increase!

You can check the target memoria and bonus amounts from Event > Janne Lindemann's Love and Past > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses can be stacked up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases based on the uncap status of the target memoria.

※Just owning the target memoria provides the bonus. You don't need to include them in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same memoria, the uncap ranks will be combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum rank V).

Example: If you own two copies of a target memoria at uncap rank I, they will be calculated as one memoria at uncap rank II.
