'Atelier Yumia: The Alchemist of Memories & the Envisioned Land' releases today!

The latest entry in the "Atelier" series, "Atelier Yumia: The Alchemist of Memories & the Envisioned Land" releases today!

Story

Walking with the forbidden.
No matter what lies at the end.


The setting is a world where alchemy is considered
"a dangerous art that brings ruin" and has become "forbidden."
The protagonist Yumia loses her mother in an accident,
and learns that her mother was an alchemist.
To discover her mother's true intentions and the truth behind the forbidden alchemy,
Yumia's journey begins as she travels across a ruined continent to trace lost history.
