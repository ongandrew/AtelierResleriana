Wish Details

The "Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES" is now available!

The SSR Memoria "What Awaits Beyond the Sea" makes its debut!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES exclusive) is now available in the Shop!

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES exclusive) can be purchased up to 2 times for 1,500 Lodestar Gems and 25 LEGEND Medals respectively in the Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to perform one Wish.

Additionally, we have added "Season 2 Opening Commemoration Wish Tickets" for a limited time in the Home > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket tab.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES exclusive) cannot be used for Wishes other than the "Season 2 Opening Commemoration LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when using Lodestar Gems to make Wishes.

LEGEND Medals can be used to exchange for ★3 characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, as well as to purchase Wish Ticket Sets in the Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters newly introduced after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange during the limited period of the Wish event. Characters introduced before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memorias available for exchange are the newly featured SSR Memorias from the ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias eligible for Memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
