●Display issue during rental synthesis

○During synthesis, when renting a character from popular formations, three gift colors are displayed in the process of selecting the last synthesis material (This is a display issue only; only synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the character placed in the second position are effective.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request: 3", the app may force close at specific timing (Lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Issue when watching video ads on specific devices
