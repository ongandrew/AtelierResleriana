[Updated 2025/04/23] Regarding Currently Identified Issues

【Updated 2025/04/23】

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

The following issues have been fixed:

●Issue where progress in certain event stories was impossible

Additionally, we have added the following issue:

●Issue with bonus effects in specific revival events

List of Fixed Issues

●Issue where progress in certain event stories was impossible

○Fixed an issue where progress could sometimes be impossible when playing the event story "Must Stop This!" in the event "Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy"

List of Issues Under Investigation

●【Update】Issue with bonus effects in specific revival events

○In revival events where the battle format is Score Battle, when displaying the bonus effects screen from the event screen or the party formation screen before battle deployment, the bonus details are not displayed correctly (This is a display issue only; the bonus effects that are correctly displayed in other events and training quests are also applied in Score Battles in revival events.)

●Display issue with some synthesis materials

○For the following synthesis materials, the display is not appropriate when there are unlocked battles in the obtainable locations

■Forest Moody

■Wind Mill

■Punk Cracka

■Canary's Chirp

■Breeze Stone

■Rock Fish

■Mountain Spring Water

■Whatsthis

■Crystal Sea Urchin

■Lottery Ore

■Cutlass

■Hunter's Claw

●Display issue with certain character details in specific screens

○When transitioning to the character details screen by tapping a character icon in Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates, the status displayed should be with the character at maximum enhancement, but for characters that can be awakened to ★6, only the skills before evolution are displayed, and the details of the third ability cannot be checked

●Display issue during rental synthesis

○When performing synthesis with characters rented from popular formations, three gift colors are displayed during the process of selecting the last synthesis material (This is a display issue only; only the synthesis materials connected to the gift color of the character in the second position will be effective.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Strength"

○Using fast-skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Strength" may cause the app to force close (This issue does not occur if you play the story without using fast-skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the link for post-payment is not displayed in the Daily Pass

●Issue with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices

■Poor image quality

■Stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■Force closing when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with receipt status in mail

○Even after receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail does not change to received (This is a display issue only; you can receive the rewards without any problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3," the app may force close at specific timing (Lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may help improve this issue.)

●Issue when watching video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, watching video ads may cause the app to force close (Please rest assured that the number of viewable times will not decrease. Lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may help improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
