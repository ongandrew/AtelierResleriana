Event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" Now Live!

Event "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" now live!

Enjoy this major event featuring Yumia and her friends from the latest series title "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~"!

In this event, you can develop facilities through "Facility Development," and as you level up facilities, you can obtain rewards such as Lodestar Gems and Lodestar Seed Gems, as well as read short stories that weren't covered in the main event story.

Additionally, this event is split into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Parts 2 and 3 will be added at a later date.

As you progress through the event story, you can recruit ★1 Viktor [Noble of Unwavering Justice].

You can also challenge "Score Battles" to obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Part 1] needed for facility development.

For this "Score Battle," you can challenge using stamina instead of challenge tickets. In addition to synthesis materials, you can also obtain Rare Coins [Yumia Festival Part 1] at a low drop rate. These Rare Coins can be exchanged at the shop for the event-exclusive SSR Memoria "For Justice" and Yumia [Seeker of Memories] Pieces.

※"Score Battle" will also be added when Parts 2 and 3 are released.

※"Boss Challenge" and "Emblems" for Part 1 will be added at a later date. They will also be added when Parts 2 and 3 are released.

※SSR Memoria "For Justice" can also be obtained from the event missions described below.

Facility Development

In "Facility Development," you can construct various structures at the "Research Base," one of the settings for this event. Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Part 1], which are necessary for facility development, can be obtained from event Score Battles. In some cases, you may need to select one facility from multiple options, and your selection will allow you to read event stories or keep "Research Records."

Progress through "Facility Development" to create your own Research Base!

※"Facility Development" will become resettable in a future update, allowing you to restart development and select different options that you didn't choose before.

Research Records

"Research Records" are event-exclusive short stories that are unlocked by meeting certain conditions, such as progressing through facility development.

These records provide glimpses into Yumia and her friends' activities during the event that aren't covered in the main event story. Play through the event and collect as many records as possible!

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you own Memoria that are eligible for bonuses, the drop amount of synthesis materials and coins will increase!

You can check eligible Memoria and bonus amounts from Event > The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※To celebrate the Yumia Festival, Memoria bonuses will stack up to 400% exclusively for this event. The bonus amount increases based on the unlock status of eligible Memoria.

※You only need to own the eligible Memoria to receive the bonus. They don't need to be equipped in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same Memoria, the unlock ranks are summed to increase the bonus amount (up to rank V)

Example: If you own two copies of unlock rank I eligible Memoria, they will be calculated as one copy of unlock rank II Memoria.
