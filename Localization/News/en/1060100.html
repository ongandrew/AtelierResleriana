April 'Mission Pass' Now on Sale!

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

The April Mission Pass will be available for purchase from 2025/04/01 12:00.

The "Mission Pass" can be accessed from Home > Shop > Recommended tab.

※The Premium unlock status from March will not carry over. To receive Premium rewards, you will need to unlock Premium for the new Mission Pass.

About Mission Pass

You can earn various rewards listed in the Mission Pass by acquiring a specified number of Mission Pass points, which are awarded based on the total number of Daily Missions completed.

Rewards include Regular rewards and Premium rewards. Premium rewards can be obtained by unlocking Premium.

The Mission Pass is available for free every month, but unlocking Premium requires 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems per month.

The Mission Pass has a sales period, and when the sales period ends, any Mission Pass points acquired during that period will be reset and cannot be carried over to the following month's Mission Pass.

For rewards from an expired Mission Pass, only unclaimed rewards that were eligible for collection at the time of expiration will be sent to your "Mail".

※Please note that rewards that did not meet the acquisition requirements cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock Premium for the Mission Pass, you become eligible to receive the Premium rewards listed in the Mission Pass that is on sale at the time of unlocking. The contents of the rewards can be viewed from Shop > Recommended > Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation dialog.

Premium rewards that have already met the acquisition requirements at the time of unlocking Premium will become immediately available.

When the Mission Pass sales period ends and switches to the next month's Mission Pass, the Premium unlock status from the previous Mission Pass will not carry over. To receive Premium rewards from the newly available Mission Pass, you will need to unlock Premium for the new Mission Pass again.
