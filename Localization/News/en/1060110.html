Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign Now Live!

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

We are pleased to announce the "Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign" where you can earn up to 3,000 Lodestar Gems based on your equipment item synthesis results!

Synthesize equipment items during the campaign period, and if you successfully synthesize an item, you'll receive Lodestar Gems as a reward based on the synthesis result!

Additionally, if you exceed 300 synthesis attempts during the period, you can earn 3,000 Lodestar Gems as the "Synth-GO! Award".
You can check your current number of syntheses from Home > Mission > Event.

Synthesize the equipment items you need and join this campaign!

In conjunction with the "Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign," limited-time missions will be available in-game!
