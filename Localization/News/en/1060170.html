Wish Details

"Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES" now available!

Lenja from "Atelier Yumia ~Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~" makes her debut!

Also, the new Memoria "Greatest Enemy" is now available!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES) is now available in the Shop!

※During the event period, if you summon a featured character that you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Radiant Stone ×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one featured character or Memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected item will have a 1% drop rate, while the non-selected items will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select your preferred featured character or featured Memoria from Wish > Select Featured.

Lenja [Sincere Devotion] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate Lenja [Sincere Devotion]'s debut, we're holding a limited-time Lenja [Sincere Devotion] Debut Celebration Mission!

Based on the number of times you wish on "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES" or "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can earn up to Lenja [Sincere Devotion] Pieces ×445! Other items are also available.

It's your chance to immediately awaken the Lenja [Sincere Devotion] you summon! Don't miss out!

※The mission ends on 2025/04/11 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES" and "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" period.

※For mission details, please check Home screen > Mission > Event > Lenja [Sincere Devotion] Debut Celebration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, purchasable up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to make one Wish.

Additionally, "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja Wish Ticket" has been temporarily added to Home > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket tab.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES) cannot be used for Wishes other than "Yumia Festival: The Timid Hard Worker! Lenja LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets will expire after the usage period ends.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When using Lodestar Gems to make a Wish, you will obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab. They can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets from Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters introduced after 2025/03/06 can be exchanged using LEGEND Medals only during their featured Wish period. ★3 Characters introduced before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria include newly introduced featured SSR Memoria from the ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for Memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals earned is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 Medal

●10-Wish: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals when using Wish Tickets.
