Middle Chapter Added to "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" Event!

The Middle Chapter has been added to "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" event!

Enjoy this major event featuring Yumia and her companions from the latest title in the series "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasm~"!

The Middle Chapter adds new "Score Battles" and a "Boss Challenge" to the event.

In the Middle Chapter's Score Battles, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Middle] to further upgrade facilities, as well as a chance to receive Rare Coins [Yumia Festival Middle]. Rare Coins can be exchanged for the event-exclusive SSR Memoria "It's Special!" and Isla [Friendly Fashionista] Pieces in the exchange shop. Additionally, clearing specific Score Battles will let you discover event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, clearing the Middle Chapter's Boss Challenge will reward you with an event-exclusive emblem. This emblem is different from the First Chapter's, so take on the challenge to obtain it!

Additionally, the Middle Chapter introduces the "Event Box Wish"!

During the event period, perform synthesis to obtain "Event Box Wish Tickets [Yumia Festival]" and use them in the Event Box Wish to receive rewards including Viktor [Noble Who Upholds Justice] Pieces!

※This event is divided into First, Middle, and Final Chapters. The Final Chapter will be added at a later date.

※Additional "Score Battles" will be added with the Final Chapter.

※The Final Chapter's "Boss Challenge" and "Emblem" will be added at a later date.

※This notice only contains information about the Middle Chapter. For information about the First Chapter of "The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy" event, please check here.

Facility Development

In "Facility Development," you can construct various buildings at the "Research Base," one of this event's settings. Starting with the Middle Chapter, facility levels can be raised to 11 and above, requiring "Facility Coins [Yumia Festival Middle]" which can be obtained from the newly added Middle Chapter Score Battles. When building facilities, you may sometimes need to choose one option from multiple choices, and these choices can unlock event stories and "Research Records."

Progress through "Facility Development" to create your own unique Research Base!

※"Facility Development" will become resettable in a future update, allowing you to make different choices by developing again.

※"Facility Coins [Yumia Festival First]" needed to raise facility levels from 1 to 10 can be obtained from the First Chapter's Score Battles.

Research Records

"Research Records" are event-exclusive short stories that unlock when certain conditions are met, such as progressing through facility development.

These stories offer glimpses of Yumia and the others during the event that aren't shown in the main event story.

New "Research Records" have been added with the Middle Chapter! Play through the event and collect lots of records!

Event Box Wish

The Event Box Wish can be used with "Event Box Wish Tickets [Yumia Festival]" obtained during the event period.

Making a wish in the Event Box Wish will randomly award you items from the box.

The box's contents decrease with each wish, and when all items in a box are obtained, you'll move on to the next box.

You can check the contents of each box from Event > Event Box Wish > Box List.

※This event's Event Box Wish can be accessed from Home > Event > The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy Event Box Wish. Please note that it is not located within the Research Base.

※The method of obtaining "Event Box Wish Tickets" may vary between events.

※Box 6 and subsequent boxes will all contain the same items.

Rental Party

You can challenge the event's Boss Challenge using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and memoria.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party to take on the Boss Challenge!

※You can still obtain clear rewards when clearing with a Rental Party.

※The characters and memoria in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the characters and memoria available vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, owning bonus memoria will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials and coins!

Target memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > The Alchemist of Memories and the Blue Conspiracy > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※The bonus memoria differ between the First and Middle Chapter tabs in Score Battle.

※To celebrate the Yumia Festival, as a special one-time event bonus, the memoria bonus can stack up to 400%. The bonus amount increases based on the awakening status of eligible memoria.

※Simply owning the target memoria will apply the bonus. You don't need to include them in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same memoria, their awakening ranks will be combined to increase the bonus amount (max V)

Example: Owning two Rank I target memoria will count as one Rank II memoria.
