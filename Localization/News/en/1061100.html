Regarding Some Event Missions Not Being Achievable with Rental Parties

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We would like to inform you about some event missions that cannot be achieved with rental parties.

Details

In the event "Alchemist of Memories and Blue Conspiracy," the following event missions cannot be achieved with rental parties:

●Clear the Yumia Festival First Half Boss Challenge with Viktor [Nobleman of Justice] in your party

●Clear the Yumia Festival First Half Boss Challenge with 2 or more characters with the "Aradis Investigation Team" tag in your party

Regarding this issue, as rental parties do not allow for changing the characters in the party freely, this is functioning as intended.

Our Response

As this is functioning as intended, we will not be adjusting the achievement conditions. However, since the mission achievement conditions were written in a way that suggested they could be completed with rental parties, we will be sending 100 Lodestar Gems as compensation for this issue.

Please receive them via the "Mail" on the home screen during the period below.

※Mail (including attached items) will be deleted after 90 days from receipt. Please be sure to open and claim them before the deadline.

Compensation Distribution Period

2025/04/14 18:00 - 2025/04/22 11:59

We apologize for the inconvenience.

Thank you for your continued support of "Atelier Resleriana".
