Wish Overview

'Yumia Festival Mysterious & Beauty Nina LEGEND FES [Paid Only]' is now available!

Nina from "Atelier Yumia ~The Alchemist of Memories and the Land of Phantasms~" makes her debut!

Also, the new Memoria "Proven Talent and..." has been added!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 3, and at STEP 3, you can make a "10x Wish" for free!

STEP1

10x Wish for 1,500 paid Lodestar Gems

STEP2

10x Wish for 1,500 paid Lodestar Gems

STEP3

Free 10x Wish

※You can complete STEP 1-3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when making a "10x Wish" at STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, you will return to STEP 1.

※During the event period, when you receive a featured character that is already in your roster, you will get Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Shards ×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one from the featured characters and Memoria to increase its drop rate. The drop rate for your selected target will be 1%, and the remaining 0.5% will be equally divided among the unselected targets.

You can select the featured character or Memoria you want to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Select Featured.

Nina [Mysterious Expert] Debut Mission

To celebrate Nina [Mysterious Expert]'s debut, we're holding a limited-time "Nina [Mysterious Expert] Debut Mission"!

Depending on the number of times you make a Wish in "Yumia Festival Mysterious & Beauty Nina LEGEND FES" or "Yumia Festival Mysterious & Beauty Nina LEGEND FES [Paid Only]", you can earn up to 445 Nina [Mysterious Expert] Character Pieces! Other items are also available.

This is your chance to immediately awaken Nina [Mysterious Expert] after obtaining her! Don't miss out!

※The mission ends on 2025/04/30 11:59. Please note that this is earlier than the end of "Yumia Festival Mysterious & Beauty Nina LEGEND FES" and "Yumia Festival Mysterious & Beauty Nina LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Nina [Mysterious Expert] Debut Mission.

LEGEND Medals and LEGEND FES Exchange

You will receive LEGEND Medals when you make a Wish using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be exchanged for LEGEND Medals only during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the new featured SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and the SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●10x Wish at STEP 1 and STEP 2: 8 medals

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish at STEP 3.
