Rerun Event 'Resleriana Academy ~Watching Over You From the Shadows~' Added!

Challenge the Event Score Battle and the high-difficulty battle "EX Challenge"!

There are also event-exclusive recipes.

Additionally, by clearing EX Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems!

※For details about the rerun event, please check the announcement here.

※Event-exclusive recipes can be inspired by clearing event stories in 'Resleriana Academy ~Expulsion!? Sudden Big Trouble~'.

Event Period

2025/04/25 12:00〜Unlimited

How to Play

Unlock the event with "Memory Keys" and challenge the Event Score Battle and "EX Challenge"!

There are also event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, by clearing all Event Score Battles, "EX Challenge" will be unlocked!

By clearing specific EX Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

EX Challenges are high-difficulty battles that do not consume stamina.

Please try challenging them with your trained party!

※For rerun events, recipe inspiration status from past events is carried over.

※Event-exclusive recipes can be inspired by clearing event stories in 'Resleriana Academy ~Expulsion!? Sudden Big Trouble~'.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards in the past event, you cannot obtain them again in the rerun event.

※Emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For EX Challenges, only the first-time clear rewards for each score rank can be obtained.

※Note that the "Resleriana Academy ~Watching Over You From the Shadows~ Commemorative LOGIN BONUS," "Event Exchange Shop," "[Expert] Reach for Space! Rocket Contest!" and "Rorona's Bottled Milk Support Campaign!" from the previous event will not be rerun in this event.
