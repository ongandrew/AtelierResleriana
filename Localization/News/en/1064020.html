[2025/05/21 Update] Regarding Currently Known Issues

[2025/05/21 Update]

Thank you for enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We have added information about the following issues.

●Issue with the equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

●Issue where some mails cannot be claimed with "Claim All"

List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Update] Issue with the equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

○The "Reduce damage from AoE attacks by 6%" effect granted by the equipment item "White Waist Pouch" does not disappear even after being attacked multiple times (※In a future update, this will be corrected to disappear after receiving one attack, as stated in the effect description.)

●[Update] Issue where some mails cannot be claimed with "Claim All"

○Mails containing the equipment item "Voting Ticket" cannot be claimed using the "Claim All" function (※It is still possible to claim these mails individually using the claim button at the bottom right of each mail.)

●Issue with bonus effects in specific rerun events

○In rerun events where the battle format is Score Battle, when viewing the bonus effect screen from the event screen or party formation screen before battle deployment, the bonus details are not displayed correctly (This is a display issue only; bonus effects that are displayed correctly in other events and training quests are also being applied in rerun event Score Battles.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the post-payment link for the Daily Pass is not displayed

●Issue with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Video stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■App force closes when playing movies repeatedly

●Display issue with mail claim status

○When claiming characters, memorias, or character pieces from mail, the mail display does not change to "claimed" status (※This is a display issue only; rewards are properly received.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3," the app may force close at specific timing (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Issue with video ad viewing on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, viewing video ads may cause the app to force close (※Please rest assured that your viewable count is not reduced. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
