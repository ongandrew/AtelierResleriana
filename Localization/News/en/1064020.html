[2025/05/07 Update] Regarding Current Known Issues

[2025/05/07 Update]

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

We have added the following issue to the list:

●Display issue with Trait Activator item details

List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Update] Display issue with Trait Activator item details

○In the event exchange shop for "Group Practice ~Material Collection~", the item details for various Trait Activators may not display correctly (this is only a display issue, and exchanged Trait Activators can be used normally.)

●Issue with bonus effects in specific rerun events

○In rerun events where the battle format is Score Battle, when viewing the bonus effect screen from the event screen or the party formation screen before battle, bonus details may not display correctly (this is only a display issue, and the bonus effects that are displaying correctly in other events and training quests are also being applied in Score Battles for rerun events.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using Fast Skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using Fast Skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the link for deferred payment is not displayed in the Daily Pass

●Issue with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur when playing movies on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■Force closing when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with mail receipt status

○When receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the status in the mail does not change to "received" (※This is only a display issue, and rewards can be received without any problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific times (※Lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Issue when watching video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, watching video ads may cause the app to force close (※Please be assured that this does not reduce the number of available views. Lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused.
