[2025/05/16 Update] Regarding Current Known Issues

[2025/05/16 Update]

Thank you for enjoying "Atelier Resleriana".

The following issues have been fixed.

●Issue where synthesis trait effects were not being applied to "Skill Power UP" granted by Alvina [Dream of Hero]

●Issue with the equipment item "Grace Ribbon"

●Issue with status changes granted by certain Memoria abilities

Fixed Issues List

●Issue where synthesis trait effects were not being applied to "Skill Power UP" granted by Alvina [Dream of Hero]

○Fixed an issue where the effect of the synthesis trait "Damage Buff Enhancement [A]" was not being applied to the "Skill Power UP" granted by Alvina [Dream of Hero]'s Burst Skill "Full Power Alvi-chan Special"

●Issue with the equipment item "Grace Ribbon"

○Fixed an issue where the "Grace Ribbon" equipment item's effect "When specialty attribute is Strike and unit is an Attacker, enhancement effect received +◯%" was not activating for enhancement effects granted at the start of battle

●Issue with status changes granted by certain Memoria abilities

○Fixed an issue where effects that trigger based on the "Evasion" status, such as Nina [Mysterious Expert]'s ability "There's Always More to Learn," were not activating when "Evasion" was granted by the following Memoria abilities

■SSR "Dreams of a Doll"

■SSR "It's Prank Time"

Issues Under Investigation

●Display issue with Trait Activator item details

○In the Event Exchange for the "Group Practice ~Material Collection~" event, various Trait Activator item details may not display correctly (this is a display issue only, and exchanged Trait Activators can be used normally.)

●Issue with special bonus effects in certain rerun events

○In rerun events where the event battle format is Score Battle, when viewing the special bonus effects screen from the event screen or the party formation screen before battle, bonus details may not display correctly (this is a display issue only, and the special bonus effects that display correctly in other events and training quests are also applied in rerun event Score Battles.)

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○The app may force close when using Fast Skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using Fast Skip.)

●Issue with Daily Pass on Android devices

○On Android devices, the link for post-payment settlement is not listed in the Daily Pass

●Issues with movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering

■Audio desynchronization

■App may force close when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with mail receipt status

○When receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail may not switch to "claimed" (※This is a display issue only, and rewards can be received without any problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request - 3," the app may force close at specific timings (※There is a possibility of improvement by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Issue with video ad viewing on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, the app may force close when viewing video ads (※Please be assured that the number of viewable ads will not decrease. There is a possibility of improvement by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused.
