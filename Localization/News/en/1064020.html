List of Fixed Issues

●An issue where synthesis trait effects were not being applied to "Fire Resistance DOWN" granted by Isla [Friendly Fashionista]

●An issue where Panel Skills were not included in the maximum damage calculation for score

○Fixed an issue in Score Battles where Panel Skill damage was not being calculated as maximum damage during score calculation

●An issue in specific battles when defeating enemies with Panel Skills

○Fixed an issue in the following battles where defeating the enemy "Malicious Decoy" with a Panel Skill would cause the battle to become unresponsive

■Battle Chapter 23-31 "Imprisoned Vengeful Spirits - 2"

■23-2 Imprisoned Vengeful Spirits - 2 [Blue/Red] (Normal)

■23-2 Imprisoned Vengeful Spirits - 2 [Blue/Red] (Very Hard)

●An issue with Yumia [Seeker of Memories]'s Panel Skill

○Fixed an issue where Yumia [Seeker of Memories]'s Panel Skill animation was playing when obtaining panels other than those that met the activation conditions (This is only a fix for the animation playback conditions; there are no changes to the Panel Skill's performance or activation conditions)

●A display issue in the "Roman Grand Martial Arts Tournament ~Fire Festival~" event exchange shop

○Fixed an issue where the exchange shop logo displayed at the top of the screen when navigating to the "Roman Grand Martial Arts Tournament ~Fire Festival~" event exchange shop was showing the logo for "Yumia Festival Special Cup: Part 2"

●Display issues with some synthesis materials

○Fixed an issue where the display for some synthesis materials was not appropriate when there were unlocked battles in the obtainable locations

●Display issues with some character details on specific screens

○Fixed an issue where, when transitioning to the character details screen by tapping a character icon in Wish > Rates/Wish Details > Rates, the character is displayed with maximum enhancement status, but for characters that can be awakened to ★6, skills would be displayed with pre-evolution performance, and the details of the third ability could not be viewed

●Display issues during rental synthesis

○Fixed an issue where, when performing synthesis with a character rented from popular formations, three gift colors would be displayed during the process of selecting the final synthesis material

●An issue where game mode would not activate under specific conditions when playing on iOS devices

○Fixed an issue on iOS devices where game mode would not activate when launching "Atelier Resleriana" ver2.1.0 or later on OS version 18 or higher
