Total War

"Total War" is an event where you aim to challenge multiple battles arranged as squares one at a time, with the ultimate goal of having all battles in a cleared state simultaneously.

The following rules apply to characters that can be deployed in "Total War":

●Characters who have cleared any battle cannot be deployed in other battles within the event

●By resetting a battle's clear status, you can make the characters who were in that battle's formation available again for battles within the event

Additionally, the following three types of rewards can be obtained in this event:

●First-time Rewards

○These can be obtained when clearing each battle for the first time

●Line Completion Rewards

○These can be obtained by having all battles in a row in a cleared state simultaneously

●Complete Rewards

○These can be obtained by having all battles in a cleared state simultaneously

Once you have received any of these rewards, you cannot receive them again even if you reset the battle clear status and clear the same battles again, or achieve the same lines.
