Event "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" Now Open!

Enjoy an event featuring characters in school uniforms at Resleri Academy!

This event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Parts 2 and 3 will be added at a later date.

You can also challenge "Score Battles" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as high-difficulty "Boss Challenge" battles.

For this "Score Battle," you can participate by using stamina instead of challenge tickets. When clearing Score Battles, you can obtain synthesis materials, Battle Coins, and rarely, Rare Coins. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the Exchange Shop for Lodestar Gems, Hope Fragments, and more. Additionally, Rare Coins can be exchanged for the event-exclusive SSR Memoria "The Start of an Exciting Race!".

Furthermore, clearing the event Boss Challenge will reward you with an event-exclusive Emblem.

※Additional "Score Battles" will be added with Parts 2 and 3.

※"Boss Challenges" and "Emblems" for Parts 2 and 3 will be added at a later date.

Rental Party

You can challenge the event Boss Challenge using a "Rental Party" that includes specific characters and memorias.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※You can still receive clear rewards even when clearing with a Rental Party.

※Characters and memorias in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the characters and memorias included vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you own bonus target memorias, the drop amount of synthesis materials will increase!

Target memorias and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Resleri Academy 2nd Term > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※To celebrate the "Resleri Academy 2nd Term" event, the Memoria Bonus can be specially increased up to 400% for this event only. The bonus amount increases based on the unlock status of target memorias.

※The bonus applies just by owning the target memorias. You don't need to include them in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same memoria, the unlock ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you own two copies of a target memoria with unlock rank I, it's calculated as one memoria with unlock rank II.
