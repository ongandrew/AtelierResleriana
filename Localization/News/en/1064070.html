About This Wish

"Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish" is now available!

The ★3 character Ryza [Blooming Energy] with newly unlocked ★6 awakening is featured!

Additionally, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish) is now available in the Shop!

Increased Piece Acquisition for Ryza [Blooming Energy]!

In this Wish, the amount of pieces you receive for already owned Ryza [Blooming Energy] is increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Crystals ×50.

※The increase only applies to pieces for already owned Ryza [Blooming Energy]. Pieces for other characters and Wish Crystals are not increased.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket ×1 allows you to perform 1 Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes performed using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish) cannot be used for any Wish other than the "Ryza [Blooming Energy] ★6 Awakening Celebration Wish".

※Wish Tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly released after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged permanently.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly featured SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are part of Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes performed using Wish Tickets.

Awakening Pack

After obtaining ★3 Ryza [Blooming Energy], an "Awakening Pack" containing character pieces and Plain Cookies will appear in Shop > Limited Time! This pack will be available for purchase for 72 hours from the moment a banner related to the product appears on the home screen.

You can purchase the "★4.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×4,500, the "★4 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000, and the "★3.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,000.
