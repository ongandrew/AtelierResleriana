About This Wish

"Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students" is now available!

New costume ★3 character Klaudia and Memoria "Are You Excited~?" make their debut!

Additionally, the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students) is now available in the Shop!

※During the event period, if you obtain a featured character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Crystals ×50.

Feature Selection

In this Wish, you can increase the drop rate for one selected featured character or memoria of your choice. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, and the unselected targets will equally share a 0.5% drop rate.

You can select which featured character and featured memoria to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Select Feature.

Klaudia [Blooming Creation] Debut Celebration Mission

To celebrate the debut of Klaudia [Blooming Creation], we're holding a limited-time Klaudia [Blooming Creation] Debut Celebration Mission!

Based on the number of times you've wished on "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students" or "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students [Paid Only]", you can receive up to Klaudia [Blooming Creation] Pieces ×445! You can also receive other items.

This is your chance to awaken Klaudia [Blooming Creation] right away! Don't miss it!

※Missions will end at 2025/05/07 11:59. Please note that this period is shorter than "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students" and "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check from the Home screen > Missions > Event > Klaudia [Blooming Creation] Debut Celebration Mission.

Wish Tickets

The Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students) is now available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket ×1 allows you to perform 1 Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes performed using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from the Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Exclusive for Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students) cannot be used for any Wish other than the "Resleri Academy LEGEND FES Childhood Friend Exchange Students".

※Wish Tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab. You can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly released after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish period. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged permanently.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly featured SSR memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are part of Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes performed using Wish Tickets.
