May 'Mission Pass' Now Available!

Thank you for your continued enjoyment of "Atelier Resleriana".

Starting from 2025/05/01 12:00, the Mission Pass for May is now available for purchase.

The "Mission Pass" can be accessed via Home > Shop > Recommended tab.

※The Premium unlock status from April will not carry over. To receive Premium rewards, you will need to unlock the Premium tier of the new Mission Pass again.

About the Mission Pass

By earning a specified number of Mission Pass Points, which are rewards for completing Daily Missions, you can obtain various rewards presented in the Mission Pass.

Rewards include Regular rewards and Premium rewards. Premium rewards can be obtained by unlocking the Premium tier.

The Mission Pass is available for free every month, but unlocking the Premium tier requires 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems each month.

Additionally, the Mission Pass has a sales period, and when the sales period ends, Mission Pass Points earned during that period will be reset and cannot be carried over to the Mission Pass sold the following month.

For rewards from an expired Mission Pass, only unclaimed rewards that were available at the time of expiration will be sent to your "Mail".

※Please note that rewards for which you did not meet the acquisition conditions cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock the Premium tier of the Mission Pass, you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards presented in the Mission Pass that is on sale at the time of unlocking. The content of the rewards can be confirmed from Shop > Recommended > Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking the Premium tier, Premium rewards for which you have already met the acquisition conditions will be immediately available.

When the Mission Pass sales period ends and switches to the next month's Mission Pass, the Premium unlock status of the previous Mission Pass will not carry over. To obtain Premium rewards for the newly released Mission Pass, you will need to unlock the Premium tier of the new Mission Pass again.
