Synthesize! SynthGO! Campaign Now Live!

Thank you for your continued enjoyment of "Atelier Resleriana".

We are excited to announce the "Synthesize! SynthGO! Campaign" where you can earn up to 3,000 Lodestar Gems based on your equipment synthesis results!

During the campaign period, when you successfully synthesize equipment items, you'll receive Lodestar Gems based on the synthesis results!

Additionally, if you exceed 300 synthesis attempts during the period, you'll receive 3,000 Lodestar Gems as the "SynthGO! Prize".
You can check your current synthesis count from Home > Missions > Event.

Synthesize the equipment items you're aiming for and participate in this campaign!

Synthesize! SynthGO! Campaign Missions

In conjunction with the "Synthesize! SynthGO! Campaign," we are holding limited-time missions in-game!

During the period, when you achieve a specific number of synthesis attempts, you can earn Bottled Mana as mission completion rewards.

Make use of the Bottled Mana obtained from missions and participate in the Synthesize! SynthGO! Campaign!

Details can be checked from Home > Missions > Event.
