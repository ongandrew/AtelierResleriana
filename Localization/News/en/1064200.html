Rerun Event "Incident Occurred! Dash Out of the Office!" Added!

Challenge the Event Score Battles and the high-difficulty "EX Challenge" battles!

Additionally, you can discover event-exclusive recipes by clearing specific Event Score Battles, and earn event-exclusive emblems by clearing EX Challenges!

※For more details about rerun events, please check the notice here.

Event Period

2025/05/09 12:00〜Permanent

How to Play

Unlock the event with "Memory Keys" and challenge the Event Score Battles and "EX Challenges"!

You can discover event-exclusive recipes by clearing specific Event Score Battles.

Additionally, clear all Event Score Battles to unlock the "EX Challenge"!

Clear specific EX Challenges to obtain event-exclusive emblems.

EX Challenges are high-difficulty battles that do not consume stamina.

Please challenge them with your well-developed party!

※For rerun events, your recipe discovery progress from past events will be carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards in past events, you cannot obtain them again in rerun events.

※Your emblem acquisition status from past events will be carried over.

※For EX Challenges, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

※Please note that the previously available "Incident Occurred! Dash Out of the Office! Commemorative LOGIN BONUS" and "Event Exchange Shop" will not be rerun for this event.
