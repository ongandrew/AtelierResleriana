[IMPORTANT] Raising the Minimum iOS Version Requirement

Thank you for playing "Atelier Resleriana".

We would like to inform you about raising the minimum OS version requirement for the iOS app version.

Due to changes in iOS app development tools, we will be raising the minimum OS version requirement for the "Atelier Resleriana" iOS app as follows:

Before change:

iPhone XR, iPhone SE (2nd gen) or later models (iOS 14 or later), iPad with 3GB RAM or more (iPadOS 14 or later)

After change:

iPhone XR, iPhone SE (2nd gen) or later models (iOS 15 or later), iPad with 3GB RAM or more (iPadOS15 or later)

After this change takes effect, you will no longer be able to play "Atelier Resleriana" on devices with OS versions below iOS 15/iPadOS15.

If your device's OS version is below iOS 15, please update to iOS 15 or later, or transfer your account to a compatible device.

Also, we kindly ask that you link your account in advance before this change takes effect. You can link your account through the in-game Menu > Account Link.

Implementation Date

We plan to implement this change with the update scheduled for 2025/05/30.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause to our iOS app users and appreciate your understanding.
