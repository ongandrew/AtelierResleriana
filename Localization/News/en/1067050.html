Second half added to the event "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester"!

Enjoy this event set in Resleriana Academy featuring characters in school uniforms!

In the second half of the event, new "Score Battles" and "Boss Challenges" will be added.

In the second half Score Battles, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Battle Coins. Additionally, by clearing specific Score Battles, you can come up with event-exclusive recipes.

Battle Coins can be exchanged for items such as Lodestar Gems and Particles of Hope at the exchange shop.

Furthermore, by clearing the second half Boss Challenges, you can obtain an event-exclusive emblem. This emblem is different from those in the first and middle parts, so aim to get it by taking on the challenge!

※The Voting Ticket from this event's exclusive recipe will be used in the event "You Choose! Student Council Election". For more information about the "You Choose! Student Council Election" event, please check here.

Rental Party

You can challenge the event Boss Challenges using a "Rental Party" that has specific characters and memorias already arranged.

You can freely equip synthesized equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Let's synthesize items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenges!

※You can still obtain the clear rewards even when clearing with a Rental Party.

※You cannot change the characters and memorias in the Rental Party. Also, the characters and memorias included in the party will vary depending on the event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you own bonus target memorias, the amount of synthesis materials dropped will increase!

Target memorias and bonus amounts can be confirmed from Event > Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※To commemorate the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event, Memoria Bonuses will stack up to a maximum of 400% for this event only. The bonus amount increases based on the awakening status of the target memorias.

※You will receive the bonus just by owning the target memorias. You do not need to include them in your party.

※If you own multiple copies of the same memoria, the awakening ranks will be added together to increase the bonus amount (maximum V)

Example: If you own 2 copies of a target memoria with awakening rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria with awakening rank II.
