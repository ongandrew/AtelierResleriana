Wish Overview

"Sophie [Mighty Boss] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish [Paid Only]" is now open!

The ★3 character Sophie [Mighty Boss] who has had her ★6 awakening unlocked is featured!

This is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 3, and in STEP 3, you can perform a "10x Wish" for free!

STEP 1

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 2

10x Wish for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 3

10x Wish for free

※You can go through STEP 1 to 3 up to 5 times.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when performing the "10x Wish" in STEP 3.

※After reaching STEP 3, it will start again from STEP 1.

Increased Sophie [Mighty Boss] Piece Acquisition!

In this Wish, the amount of pieces you receive for already owned Sophie [Mighty Boss] has been increased! You will receive Character Pieces ×200 and Wish Shards ×50.

※Only the pieces for Sophie [Mighty Boss] that you already own will be increased. Character pieces for other characters and Wish Shards are not increased.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly added after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange only during the limited period when their Wish is available. ★3 characters released before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added featured SSR memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●10x Wish in STEP 1 and STEP 2: 8 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free 10x Wish in STEP 3.

When you obtain ★3 Sophie [Mighty Boss], "Awakening Packs" containing character pieces and Plain Cookies will appear in Shop > Limited Time! You can purchase these packs for 72 hours from the moment the product banner is displayed on the Home screen.
