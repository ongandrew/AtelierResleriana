Wish Overview

"Resleriana Academy Select and Guaranteed Wish Part 2" is now open!

We are holding this special Wish to commemorate the addition of the second half to the "Resleriana Academy 2nd Semester" event.

This is a special single Wish that can be performed with 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems, allowing you to select and guarantee to obtain 1 character from among the following 3: Sophie [Mighty Boss], Rorona [Joyful Leader], and Mimi [Lonely Glory]!

※This Wish can be performed only once.

※If you obtain a character you already own, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Shards ×50.

※You can check the drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when performing Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters & Memorias > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias include newly added featured SSR memorias in the current LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals you can obtain is as follows:

●Single Wish: 8 medals

Notes

●This Wish is exclusive to Paid Lodestar Gems, so you cannot use Free Lodestar Gems or Wish Tickets.

●You can check the drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

●Once you have performed this Wish once, it will no longer be displayed in the Wish list.

●If you obtain a character you already own, it will automatically be converted to character pieces and Wish Shards.
