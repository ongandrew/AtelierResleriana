Wish Overview

The "Select & Guaranteed SSR Memoria Wish [Heidi]" is now available!

This is a step-up wish from STEP 1-5, where you can wish for free at STEP 2, 4, and 5 for a "Guaranteed Selected Memoria single wish"!

Additionally, in the "Guaranteed Selected Memoria single wish" at STEP 2, 4, and 5, you can select one Memoria from the selectable Memoria and obtain it guaranteed.

The selectable Memoria are Memoria that are compatible with Heidi's various costumes.

STEP5

Free single wish

※You can complete STEP 1-5 up to 1 full cycle.

※You can select different Memoria for STEP 2, 4, and 5 respectively.

※You will not receive LEGEND Medals when executing the "Guaranteed Selected Memoria single wish" at STEP 2, 4, and 5.

※You can only select Memoria at STEP 2, 4, and 5. You cannot select Memoria at other STEPs.

※Please check the selectable Memoria from Wish > Selectable Memoria.

※For STEPs other than STEP 2, 4, and 5, you can obtain characters and Memoria based on the drop rates displayed.

※Please check the drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※The SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pick-up SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●STEP1 and STEP3 10-wish: 13 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free single wishes at STEP 2, 4, and 5.
