Wish Overview

"Stab Festival Wish LEGEND FES" is now on!

★3 characters Magda [Pinkish Destroyer] and Brad [Passionate Blood], and SSR memoria Where Knowledge Gathers are featured!

*During the event period, if you obtain a featured character that has already joined your party, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Brilliance ×50.

Featured Selection

In this Wish, you can select one of the featured characters or memoria to increase its drop rate. The selected target will have a 1% drop rate, while unselected targets will have a drop rate of 0.5% divided equally among them.

You can select which featured character or featured memoria you want to increase the drop rate for from Wish > Target Featured.

Wish Tickets

Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Stab Festival Wish LEGEND FES Exclusive) available in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, purchasable twice each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

You can make 1 wish per Wish Ticket.

*LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from wishes using Wish Tickets.

*Wish Tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Stab Festival Wish LEGEND FES Exclusive) cannot be used for wishes other than "Stab Festival Wish LEGEND FES".

*Wish Tickets will disappear after the expiration date.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Characters/Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

*Characters newly introduced after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters introduced before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

*SSR memoria available for exchange are the newly introduced featured SSR memoria from the ongoing LEGEND FES and SSR memoria eligible for memoria bonuses.

*LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

*LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10x Wish: 11 medals

*LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from wishes using Wish Tickets.
