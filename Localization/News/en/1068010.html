Maintenance Completion Notice

The maintenance for the update has been completed.

The update details are as follows.

Thank you very much for your cooperation during the maintenance.

Please continue to enjoy Atelier Resleriana.

※For details regarding Wish drop rate changes/Step-up Wish format changes/New Character Release Commemoration Mission content changes/Sale product content changes, please refer to the individual notices posted here.

Update Details

●Additional data implementation

●App icon change

○The app icon has been changed

●Wish drop rate changes

○The drop rate for SSR Memoria has been changed from 2% to 4%

○Additionally, the pickup selection function has been abolished, and pickup target ★3 characters will now have a 1% drop rate, while pickup target SSR Memoria will have a 3% drop rate

●Format change for paid Step-up Wish when new characters are released

○The paid Step-up Wish held when new characters are released has been changed from 5 cycles to 1 cycle maximum, and the number of steps has been changed from 3 to 5

○Along with the above changes, the number of paid Lodestar Gems required for STEP 3 has been modified

○STEP 3 will have double pickup appearance rate, and STEP 5 will have triple pickup appearance rate

○Furthermore, after completing all steps of the Step-up Wish, a "Bonus Wish" with double pickup appearance rate that can be executed up to 5 times will appear (※Each time you make a wish, you can obtain 50 pieces of the newly released character as a bonus.)

●New Character Release Commemoration Mission content changes

○Mission rewards have been increased, and rewards can now be obtained for up to 400 wishes instead of 300

○All rewards that could be obtained by making 300 wishes in the previous mission format can now be obtained every 200 wishes

●Sale product content changes

○The three types of Lodestar Gem sales that were available when new characters were released have been consolidated into one type with modified purchase limits and content

○Along with the above changes, a monthly purchasable Lodestar Gem sale and a daily purchasable Lodestar Gem sale have been newly added

●"Clear Party" feature added

○A new "Clear Party" feature has been added

○From the party screen just before deploying to a quest, you can now check party information of players who have already cleared that quest (※Clear Party information will not be displayed for certain quests such as Roman's Grand Tournament and Legend Challenge.)

●Improvement to the sorting order of Light Orbs in the owned Light Orbs screen

○In Training Quest > Light Orb Quest > Owned Light Orbs, the display order of Light Orbs shown on screen has been improved to match the tab order in the quest selection screen

●Shop display improvement after purchasing certain products

○For the following products, after purchase they will no longer disappear from the product list, but will continue to be displayed at the end of the product list with a SOLD OUT indication (※This improvement allows visualization of the next reset timing for products that reset monthly after 2025/06/01 12:00. This display improvement will not apply to products sold until 2025/06/01 11:59.)

■Home Screen > Shop > Exchange > Monthly Tab products

■Home Screen > Shop > Pack > Limited Time Tab Star Prayer Stone Seeds/Star Prayer Stone Petals

○The ATELIER FES lineup has been updated to include characters & Memoria up to the "Season 2 Opening! Memory Tracer Valeria LEGEND FES" held on 2025/03/26

●Update to certain Star Prayer Stone exchanges

○The following Star Prayer Stone exchanges have been updated to include characters & Memoria up to the "Season 2 Opening! Memory Tracer Valeria LEGEND FES" held on 2025/03/26

○"Reigning Great Hammer Knight Izana Wish Ticket" has been added to Home Screen > Shop > Exchange > Flocke Ticket Tab for a limited time

●Change to Friendship Key possession limit

○The possession limit for Friendship Keys has been changed from 20 to 99

List of Fixed Bugs

○Regarding the fix for the bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed, which was applied on 2025/03/17, we have once again corrected the unlock conditions to be properly removed, including the bug where the display of unlock conditions viewable on the Grow Board screen was not in a normal state

●Bug with equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

○Fixed a bug where the "AOE attack damage received -6%" effect granted by the equipment item "White Waist Pouch" would not disappear no matter how many times attacks were received

●Bug where event story voices are not downloaded under certain conditions
