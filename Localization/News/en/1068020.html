[06/13/2025 Update] Currently Confirmed Issues

[06/13/2025 Update]

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have fixed the following issue:

●Issue with the event "Explosive!! Trial from Marie"

Additionally, we have added the following issue to our list:

●Issue where Johanna [Phenom's Reborn]'s ability does not activate under specific conditions

List of Fixed Issues

●Issue with the event "Explosive!! Trial from Marie"

○We have fixed the following issues in the event "Explosive!! Trial from Marie" (※The event "Explosive!! Trial from Marie" is planned to be re-released in the near future.)

■The effect amounts displayed for special rules in event battles such as "After Action, Item Gauge Additional Recovery +○%" do not reflect the effect amount increases from equipped items' "Received Enhancement Effect Amount +○%" and similar effects

■The battle start and defeat animations for the enemy "Marie" are not functioning as intended

List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Added] Issue where Johanna [Phenom's Reborn]'s ability does not activate under specific conditions

○When Johanna [Phenom's Reborn] equipped with the "Fugi=Muni" item attacks, the status change "Critical Rate +25%" from the ability "I'll show you how it's done!" may not be applied (※This is scheduled to be fixed in a future update.)

●Issue when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun event, when selecting an unlocked quest, the unlock conditions are not displayed, and instead a dialog indicating that the event period has ended is shown, causing a transition to the home screen (※This issue only occurs when the quest is unlocked, and does not occur with unlocked quests. Additionally, if the unlock conditions for an unlocked quest are met, the quest will unlock normally and can be deployed and cleared.)

●Issue where automatic text scrolling does not function in specific situations

○For skill effect and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, preventing the full text from being read (※Effect descriptions can be viewed normally on screens outside of battle. Additionally, during battle, effect descriptions can be viewed normally on the following screens.)

■The skill details screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands when any skill is selected during battle

■The item details screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands on the item action screen when the item button is selected during battle

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include links for deferred payment options

●Movie playback issues on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering playback

■Audio desynchronization

■App force closes when repeatedly playing movies

●Mail receipt status display issue

○Despite receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display within the mail does not change to "received" (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Video advertisement viewing issue on specific devices

○On specific devices, when certain environment settings are configured, the app may force close when viewing video advertisements (※Please be assured that the viewable count will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
