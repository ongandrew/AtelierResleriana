List of Issues Under Investigation

●[6/25 Update] Issue with SSR Memoria "Perfect Day for Gathering!" Memoria Ability

○The status effect "Magic Damage Taken UP" applied by the SSR Memoria "Perfect Day for Gathering!" increases not only magic attack damage received but also physical attack damage received (※As described in the effect, we plan to fix this in a future update so that only magic attack damage received is increased.)

●[Update] Issue where activating Izana [Knight's Beginning] ability under specific conditions causes the game to become unresponsive

○When Izana [Knight's Beginning] equipped with the equipment item "Mountain Peak Hand Mirror" activates the ability "Shining Knight's Hammer", the battle becomes unresponsive (※We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●[Update] Issue where the app force closes on some Android devices

○On some Android devices, the app force closes when entering specific quests or stories (※This phenomenon may be avoided by setting Shadow Quality and Background Expression to "Low" in Home Screen > Menu > Options > Screen Tab > Custom Settings.)

●Issue when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun event, when selecting an unlocked quest, the unlock conditions are not displayed and a dialog stating that the event period has ended is shown, transitioning to the home screen (※This issue only occurs when the quest is in an unlocked state and does not occur with unlocked quests. When the unlock conditions for an unlocked quest are met, the quest will be unlocked normally and can be deployed and cleared.)

●Issue where automatic text scrolling does not function in specific situations

○Regarding skill effect and combat item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, making it impossible to read the text to the end (※Effect descriptions can be viewed normally on screens other than during battle. During battle, effect descriptions can be viewed normally on the following screens.)

■Skill details screen in the i button displayed at the top right of the command while selecting any skill during battle

■Item details screen in the i button displayed at the top right of the command on the item action screen after selecting the item button during battle

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" may cause the app to force close (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for postpaid payment

●Issue during movie playback on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■Force closes when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue with mail reception status

○Despite receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display in the mail does not switch to received (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timing (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Issue when watching video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, the app may force close when watching video ads under specific environment settings (※Please be assured that the viewing count will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
