List of Bugs Under Investigation

●Bug when selecting specific quests in revival events

○In certain revival events, when selecting unlocked quests, unlock conditions are not displayed and a dialog stating that the event period has ended appears, redirecting to the home screen (※This bug only occurs when quests are unlocked; it does not occur with unlocked quests. Additionally, when unlock conditions for unlocked quests are met, quests are properly unlocked and can be deployed and cleared.)

●Bug where automatic text scrolling doesn't function in specific situations

○For skill effects and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, preventing the full text from being read (※Effect descriptions can be properly viewed on screens outside of battles. Additionally, during battles, effect descriptions can be properly viewed on the following screens.)

■Skill details screen accessible via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands when any skill is selected during battle

■Item details screen accessible via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands on the item action screen when the item button is selected during battle

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to force close (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include links for postpaid payment

●Bug during movie playback on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following bugs may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■Repeatedly playing movies causes force closure

●Mail receipt status display bug

○Despite receiving characters, memorias, or character pieces via mail, the display within the mail does not switch to "received" (※This is a display-only bug; rewards can be properly received.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timing (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices with certain environment settings, viewing video ads may cause the app to force close (※Please be assured that viewing attempts are not deducted. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to our players.
