List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug with SSR Memoria "Perfect Day for Gathering!" Memoria ability

○Fixed a bug where the status effect "Magic Damage Taken UP" granted by SSR Memoria "Perfect Day for Gathering!" was incorrectly increasing not only magic attack damage taken but also physical attack damage taken

●Bug where certain status effect names were inconsistent

○Fixed a bug where the status effects "Critical Rate UP" and "Critical Rate DOWN" had inconsistent names when granted by certain characters or Memoria (※As shown below, they have been unified to "Critical Rate UP".)

Before fix:

"Critical Rate UP/DOWN" and

"Critical Chance UP/DOWN" were mixed

After fix:

Unified to "Critical Rate UP/DOWN"

●Display bug in event story for "Karoston, the Island of Knowledge ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~"

○Fixed the following display bugs in the event story for "Karoston, the Island of Knowledge ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~"

■In the scene where 2D illustrations are displayed in the event story "So Much Unknown!", Johanna's outfit was displayed unintentionally

■In the event story "Reliable Geniuses", Sterk's dialogue speaker was displayed as "???"

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[Added 07/01] Display bug when unlocking SSR Memoria "Blooming Talent"

○When unlocking SSR Memoria "Blooming Talent", the effect amounts enhanced by unlock rank increases do not change to blue text, making it difficult to understand the enhancement details (※We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●[Added 06/30] Bug where certain panels are not affected by panel nullification

○The effects of the following panels activate without being affected by the status effects "Panel Nullification" and "Negative Panel Nullification" (※We plan to fix this in a future update.)

■Poison Panel

■Wisdom Release Panel

■Martelart Panel

●Bug where the game becomes unresponsive when activating Izana [Knight's Beginning]'s ability under specific conditions

○When Izana [Knight's Beginning] equipped with the "Noble's Hand Mirror" activates the ability "Shining Knight's Hammer", the battle becomes unresponsive (※We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●Bug where the app force closes on certain Android devices

○On certain Android devices, the app force closes when entering specific quests or stories (※This can sometimes be avoided by setting Shadow Quality and Background Expression to "Low" in Home Screen > Menu > Options > Display Tab > Custom Settings.)

●Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun event, when selecting an unlocked quest, the unlock conditions are not displayed, and a dialog stating that the event period has ended appears, returning to the home screen (※This bug only occurs when quests are unlocked; it does not occur for already unlocked quests. When unlock conditions for unlocked quests are met, the quests are unlocked normally and can be deployed and cleared.)

●Bug where automatic text scrolling doesn't work in certain situations

○For skill effect and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not work on certain screens, preventing you from reading the full text (※Effect descriptions can be viewed normally on screens outside of battle. During battle, effect descriptions can be viewed normally on the following screens.)

■Skill detail screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands when any skill is selected during battle

■Item detail screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands on the item action screen when the item button is selected during battle

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to force close (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for postpaid billing

●Bug during movie playback on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following bugs may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desync occurs

■Force close occurs when playing movies repeatedly

●Display bug for mail receipt status

○Even after receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces via mail, the display within the mail does not switch to received status (※This is a display-only bug; rewards are received without issue.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, under certain environment settings, the app may force close when viewing video ads (※The number of viewable times will not decrease, so please be assured. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
