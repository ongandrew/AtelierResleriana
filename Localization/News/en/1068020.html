●[Added] Issue where activating Izana [Knight's Beginning]'s ability under specific conditions causes the game to become unresponsive

○When Izana [Knight's Beginning] equipped with the equipment item "High Peak's Hand Mirror" activates the ability "Shining Knight's Hammer," the battle becomes unresponsive (※We plan to fix this in a future update.)

●[Added] Issue where the app crashes on some Android devices

○On some Android devices, the app crashes when entering specific quests or stories (※This phenomenon may be avoided by setting Shadow Quality and Background Expression to "Low" in Home Screen > Menu > Options > Screen Tab > Custom Settings.)

●Issue when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun events, when selecting an unlocked quest, the unlock conditions are not displayed, and a dialog stating that the event period has ended appears, causing transition to the home screen (※This issue only occurs when the quest is unlocked, and does not occur with unlocked quests. Additionally, if the unlock conditions for locked quests are met, the quest will be unlocked normally and can be deployed and cleared.)

●Issue where automatic text scrolling does not function in specific scenes

○For skill effect and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, preventing the full text from being read (※Effect descriptions can be viewed normally on screens other than during battle. Additionally, during battle, effect descriptions can be viewed normally on the following screens.)

■Skill details screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands when any skill is selected during battle

■Item details screen accessed via the i button displayed in the upper right of commands on the item action screen when the item button is selected during battle

●Issue with Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○The app may crash when using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" (※This issue does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for postpaid payment

●Movie playback issue on specific Android devices

○The following issues may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■App crashes when repeatedly playing movies

●Display issue for receipt status in mail

○Despite receiving characters, memoria, or character pieces via mail, the display in the mail does not switch to received status (※This is a display-only issue, and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3," the app may crash at specific timings (※Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

●Issue when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, the app may crash when viewing video ads under certain environment settings (※Please be assured that the number of viewable times will not decrease. Lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution may improve this issue.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused to everyone.
