Thank you for enjoying 'Atelier Resleriana'.

We have fixed the following bugs.

●Bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed

●Bug with equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

●Bug with special effect bonuses in specific rerun events

●Bug causing progression to become impossible during ally turns immediately after Juna [With Ideals]

●Bug where event story voices are not downloaded under specific conditions

Additionally, we have added the following bug content.

●Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

●Bug where automatic text scrolling does not function in specific situations

List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed

○Regarding the fix for the bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed that was applied on 2025/03/17, we have corrected it again to a state where unlock conditions are properly removed, including fixing the bug where the display of unlock conditions visible on the Grow Board screen was not in a normal state

●Bug with equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

○We have fixed the bug where the "AoE damage received -6%" effect granted by the equipment item "White Waist Pouch" would not disappear no matter how many times you were attacked

●Bug with special effect bonuses in specific rerun events

○We have fixed the bug where bonus details would not display properly when viewing the special effect bonus screen from the event screen or pre-battle party formation screen in rerun events where the battle format is Score Battle

●Bug causing progression to become impossible during ally turns immediately after Juna [With Ideals]

○We have fixed the bug where in battle, when Juna [With Ideals] uses Skill 2 "Drei Schnellstoß" when the skill lamp count is 3, commands could not be selected during the ally turn immediately following, potentially causing progression to become impossible

●Bug where event story voices are not downloaded under specific conditions

○We have fixed the bug where voices would not play in event stories in some cases when split download is selected during new user data creation

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[Added]Product display order bug in specific shops

○In Home Screen > Shop > Pack > Wish Ticket Tab, the "Stab Festival Wish Ticket No.2" wish ticket set is displayed at the top

●[Added]Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun event, when selecting an unlocked quest, unlock conditions are not displayed and a dialog stating that the event period has ended appears, causing transition to the home screen (※This bug only occurs when quests are unlocked; it does not occur with unlocked quests. Additionally, when unlock conditions for unlocked quests are met, quests are properly unlocked and can be deployed to and cleared.)

●[Added]Bug where automatic text scrolling does not function in specific situations

○For skill effects and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, making it impossible to read the text to the end (※Effect descriptions can be properly viewed on screens outside of battle. Additionally, during battle, effect descriptions can be properly viewed on the following screens.)

■Skill details screen within the i button displayed at the top right of commands when any skill is selected during battle

■Item details screen within the i button displayed at the top right of commands on the item action screen when the item button is selected during battle

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power"

○The app may force close when using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Difference in Power" (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for deferred payment

●Bug during movie playback on specific Android devices

○The following bugs may occur during movie playback on specific Android devices

■Video quality deterioration

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■Force close occurs when repeatedly playing movies

●Display bug for receipt status in mail

○Despite receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display within the mail does not switch to received status (※This is a display-only bug; rewards can be received without issue.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timing (※This may improve by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, the app may force close when viewing video ads under specific environment settings (※Please be assured that viewable counts will not decrease. This may improve by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.
