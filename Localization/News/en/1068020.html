[2025/05/30 Update] About Currently Confirmed Bugs

[2025/05/30 Update]

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have fixed the following bugs.

●Bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed

●Bug with equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

●Bug with special effect bonus in certain rerun events

●Bug causing progression freeze on ally turns immediately after Juna【With Ideals】's action

●Bug where event story voices are not downloaded under certain conditions

Additionally, we have added the following bug information.

●Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

●Bug where automatic text scrolling doesn't function in certain situations

List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed

○Regarding the fix for the bug where Grow Board unlock conditions were not properly removed, which was applied on 2025/03/17, we have once again corrected the unlock conditions to be properly removed, including the bug where the display of unlock conditions viewable on the Grow Board screen was not in a normal state

●Bug with equipment item "White Waist Pouch"

○Fixed a bug where the "AOE attack damage received -6%" effect granted by the equipment item "White Waist Pouch" would not disappear no matter how many times attacks were received

●Bug with special effect bonus in certain rerun events

○Fixed a bug where bonus details would not display properly when viewing the special effect bonus screen from the event screen or party formation screen before battle deployment in rerun events where the battle format is Score Battle

●Bug causing progression freeze on ally turns immediately after Juna【With Ideals】's action

○Fixed a bug where in battle, when Juna【With Ideals】uses Skill 2 "Dry Schnellstoss" with 3 skill lamps lit, commands could not be selected on the ally's turn immediately after, causing progression to freeze

●Bug where event story voices are not downloaded under certain conditions

○Fixed a bug where voices might not play in event stories when split download is selected during new user data creation

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[Update] Bug when selecting specific quests in rerun events

○In any rerun event, when selecting an unlocked quest, the unlock conditions are not displayed and a dialog indicating that the event period has ended appears, causing a transition to the home screen (※This bug only occurs when the quest is in an unlocked state and does not occur with already unlocked quests. Additionally, when the unlock conditions for unlocked quests are met, the quest will be properly unlocked and can be deployed to and cleared.)

●[Update] Bug where automatic text scrolling doesn't function in certain situations

○For skill effect and battle item effect descriptions, scrolling may not function on some screens, making it impossible to read the text to the end (※Effect descriptions can be viewed normally on screens outside of battle. Additionally, during battle, effect descriptions can be viewed normally on the following screens.)

■Skill detail screen within the i button displayed in the upper right of the command while selecting any skill during battle

■Item detail screen within the i button displayed in the upper right of the command on the item action screen after selecting the item button during battle

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○Using high-speed skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to force close (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using high-speed skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for postpaid payment

●Bug during movie playback on certain Android devices

○The following bugs may occur during movie playback on certain Android devices

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desynchronization occurs

■Force close occurs when repeatedly playing movies

●Display bug for receipt status in mail

○Despite receiving characters, Memoria, or character pieces from mail, the display within the mail does not switch to received status (※This is a display-only bug, and rewards can be received without any issues.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at specific timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on certain devices

○On certain devices, when viewing video ads with specific environment settings, the app may force close (※Please be assured that the number of viewable times will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.

We deeply apologize for the inconvenience caused to everyone.
