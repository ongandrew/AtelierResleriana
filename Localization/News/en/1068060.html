About the Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass

By obtaining the specified number of Mission Pass Points [Star Prayer Stone] that serve as clear rewards for the event mission "Star Prayer Stone Mission", you can acquire various rewards listed within the Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass.

There are Regular rewards and Premium rewards, with Premium rewards becoming available by unlocking Premium. The Star Prayer Stone Seeds obtainable through Regular rewards can be exchanged for Star Prayer Stone Buds at Home > Shop > Character Memoria > Star Prayer Stone Tab. Additionally, the Star Prayer Stone Petals (Memoria) obtainable through Premium rewards can be exchanged for Star Prayer Stone Crowns (Memoria) at Home > Shop > Character Memoria > Star Prayer Stone Tab.

The Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass can be used free of charge, but unlocking Premium requires 250 Paid Lodestar Gems.

Additionally, the Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass has a sales period, and for rewards from Star Prayer Stone Mission Passes that have ended sales, only unclaimed rewards that were available for acquisition at the time of termination will be sent to "Mail".

※Star Prayer Stone Exchange allows you to obtain characters and Memoria in exchange for items. For details, please see here.

※Please check the drop rates for Star Prayer Stone Buds and Star Prayer Stone Crowns (Memoria) from Home > Shop > Character Memoria > Star Prayer Stone > Drop rates within each product's exchange confirmation screen.

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Star Prayer Stone Mission.

※Please note that rewards for conditions that were not met cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock Premium for the Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass, you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards listed within the Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass that is currently on sale at the time of unlock. The reward content can be confirmed from Shop > Recommended > Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, any Premium rewards whose acquisition conditions have already been met will become immediately available for acquisition.

※Before unlocking Premium, please be sure to confirm the Premium reward content and verify that the Mission Pass you are purchasing is indeed the "Star Prayer Stone Mission Pass".
