"Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]" Now on Sale!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

To commemorate the event "Hammer of Tested Resolve", we are now selling the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve].

The "Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]" can be accessed from Home > Shop > Recommended Tab.

About Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]

By obtaining the specified number of Event Mission Pass Points that serve as clear rewards for the event mission "Hammer of Tested Resolve Mission", you can acquire various rewards listed within the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve].

There are Regular rewards and Premium rewards, with Premium rewards becoming available by unlocking Premium.

The Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] can be used free of charge, but unlocking Premium requires 500 Paid Lodestar Gems.

Additionally, the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] has a sales period, and for rewards from the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] that has ended sales, only unclaimed rewards that were available for acquisition at the time of termination will be sent to "Mail".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Hammer of Tested Resolve.

※Please note that rewards for conditions that were not met cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock Premium for the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve], you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards listed within the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] that is currently on sale at the time of unlock. The reward content can be confirmed from Shop > Recommended > Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, any Premium rewards whose acquisition conditions have already been met will become immediately available for acquisition.

※Before unlocking Premium, please be sure to confirm the Premium reward content and verify that the Mission Pass you are purchasing is indeed the "Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]".
