The "Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]" can be accessed from Home > Shop > Recommended tab.

About Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]

By obtaining the specified number of Event Mission Pass Points, which are the clear rewards for the event mission "Hammer of Tested Resolve Mission," you can obtain various rewards presented in the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve].

There are Regular rewards and Premium rewards, and Premium rewards can be obtained by unlocking Premium.

The Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] can be used free of charge, but unlocking Premium requires 500 Paid Lodestar Gems.

Additionally, the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] has a sales period, and for rewards from Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] whose sales have ended, only unclaimed rewards that were available for acquisition at the time of termination will be sent to "Mail."

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Hammer of Tested Resolve.

※Please note that rewards for which acquisition conditions were not met cannot be obtained.

About Unlocking Premium

When you unlock Premium for Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve], you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards presented in the Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] that is on sale at the time of unlocking. You can check the reward contents from Shop > Recommended > Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve] > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, Premium rewards whose acquisition conditions have already been met become immediately available for acquisition.

※Before unlocking Premium, please make sure to check the Premium reward contents and confirm that the Mission Pass you are purchasing is the "Event Mission Pass [Hammer of Tested Resolve]" without any mistakes.
