Wish Overview

Up to 500 Free Wishes! The "FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish" is now live!

To commemorate the general release of the "FumoColle" × "Atelier Resleriana" collaboration goods that were pre-ordered at the end of last year, we are holding a special Wish event.

This Wish can only be accessed using the FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish Tickets obtainable during the event period, making it a special Wish event. The obtainable SSR Memoria are only the 10 new SSR Memoria making their debut in this Wish! All of them feature original illustrations used for the collaboration goods!

※This Wish provides Memoria only.

※Please check the drop rates via Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

※Since this Wish can only be accessed using FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish Tickets, no LEGEND Medals will be obtained when making Wishes.

※Wish Tickets that exceed their expiration date will be lost.

How to Obtain FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish Tickets

The FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish Tickets needed to access this Wish can be obtained through Event Missions.

Through Mission rewards, you can obtain up to 500 Wish Tickets.

Take on the Event Missions and make your "FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wishes"!

Furthermore, missions that can be completed by making the "FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish" a certain number of times will reward FumoColle Medals!

FumoColle Medals can be exchanged in Shop > Character Memoria > FumoColle for any 1 Memoria from the 10 new SSR Memoria making their debut in this Wish.

By completing all missions, you can obtain 10 FumoColle Medals! You can even obtain all 10 new Memoria using only medals, so please aim for a complete collection!

※Some missions will unlock the next mission after receiving rewards.

※There are no exchange limits for SSR Memoria obtainable with FumoColle Medals. You can consume multiple medals to exchange for multiple copies of the same Memoria.

Important Notes

●This Wish can only be accessed using FumoColle Goods Launch Commemorative Wish Tickets. It cannot be accessed using Lodestar Gems.

●Please check drop rates via Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

●The new Memoria making their debut in this Wish may appear in future Wish events.
