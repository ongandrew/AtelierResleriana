LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to make Wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets in Shop > Pack > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 03/06/2025 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange during their Wish event period only. ★3 Characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●1x Wish: 1 Medal

●10x Wish: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When Izana [Knight's Beginning] is awakened to ★5, you can gain inspiration for SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Izana [Knight's Beginning].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
