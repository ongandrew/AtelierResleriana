Wish Overview

"Great Hammer Knight Reigns Supreme: Izana LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" event is now on!

★3 character Izana [Knight's Beginning] and Memoria "As a Knight" make their debut & are featured as pickups!

This Wish is a step-up event with STEP 1~5, where STEP 3 features 2x Pickup Rate, and STEP 5 features 3x Pickup Rate!

Additionally, after completing STEP 1~5, an additional "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish features 2x Pickup Rate!

Furthermore, when you wish 10 times, you can obtain Izana [Knight's Beginning] Pieces x50 as a bonus!

●STEP 1~5 Step-up Wish

Izana [Knight's Beginning] Debut Commemoration Mission

To commemorate the debut of Izana [Knight's Beginning], we are holding the "Izana [Knight's Beginning] Debut Commemoration Mission" for a limited time!

※Please note that this has a shorter duration than the "Great Hammer Knight Reigns Supreme: Izana LEGEND FES" and "Great Hammer Knight Reigns Supreme: Izana LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" events.

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Izana [Knight's Beginning] Debut Commemoration Mission.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you perform a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained via LEGEND Medal exchange during the Wish event period only on a limited-time basis. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1~4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Izana [Knight's Beginning] to ★5, you can gain inspiration for SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Izana [Knight's Beginning].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
