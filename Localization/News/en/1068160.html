Wish Overview

The "Season 2 Resna or Valeria Guaranteed Wish" event is now on!

This Wish is a step-up event with STEP 1~4, and at STEP 4 you can guarantee either Resna [Rise Above Fate] or Valeria [Get Over Limit] for free!

Additionally, at STEP 4, if you obtain Resna [Rise Above Fate] or Valeria [Get Over Limit] when you already have them, the amount of pieces you receive is increased! You can obtain Character Pieces x100 and Gems of Aspiration x50.

STEP1

10x Wish for Lodestar Gems x3,000

STEP2

10x Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems x3,000

STEP3

10x Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems x2,000

STEP4

1x Wish for free

Resna [Rise Above Fate] or Valeria [Get Over Limit] guaranteed!

※You can complete 1 cycle from STEP 1~4.

※At STEP 4, no LEGEND Medals will be obtained when performing the "1x Wish".

※At STEP 4's 1x Wish, you cannot select which guaranteed character you receive.

※Please check the drop rates at Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

LEGEND Medals & LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you perform a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained via LEGEND Medal exchange during the Wish event period only on a limited-time basis. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※During this Wish event period, Resna [Rise Above Fate] and Valeria [Get Over Limit] cannot be exchanged for LEGEND Medals.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not expire.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●STEP1, STEP2: 13 medals

●STEP3: 10 medals

※No LEGEND Medals can be obtained from STEP4's free Wish.

Important Notes

●This Wish cannot be performed using Wish Tickets. Additionally, STEP2 and STEP3 are limited to Paid Lodestar Gems only, so Free Lodestar Gems cannot be used.

●Please check the drop rates at Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

●Once you complete 1 cycle of this Wish, it will be hidden from the Wish list.

●If you obtain a character you already have, it will automatically be converted to Character Pieces and Gems of Aspiration.

●The event period and content may be changed without prior notice. Please understand this in advance.
