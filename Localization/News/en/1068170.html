●Review of Sale Items and Wishes


▼Review of Sale Items and Wishes

Approximately a year and a half has passed since the service launch, and the frequency and content of Wishes have changed significantly from the beginning.

Therefore, after deliberation within the development team, we have determined that it is necessary to adjust the content to consider future operational balance and the current environment, so we will be reviewing sales and Wishes.


○Changes to Sale Item Content

Previously, Step-Up Wishes from STEP1 to STEP3 could be executed up to 5 rounds, but after the change, it will be possible to execute Step-Up Wishes from STEP1 to STEP5 for 1 round. STEP3 after the change will have 2x featured appearance rate, and STEP5 will have 3x, so the probability of featured targets appearing is increased compared to previous Step-Up Wishes.

Furthermore, when you finish wishing through all of the Step-Up Wishes for 1 round, "Bonus Wishes" that can be executed up to 5 times will appear where the Step-Up Wishes were displayed. These are 10-Wishes with 2x featured appearance rate for all 5 times, and each time you execute a Wish, you can obtain 50 pieces of the newly appearing character as a bonus on the Wish results screen.

With the current Wish drop rates, there was a problem where "obtaining Memoria through Wishes is difficult and you can't raise unlock ranks." Therefore, we will change the overall drop rate for SSR Memoria to 4%, which is double compared to before the change, with 3% of that being the drop rate for featured Memoria.

In implementing the above changes, since the featured Memoria drop rate will be higher than when using featured selection, we will abolish featured selection for Wishes held after maintenance ends on 2025/05/30. With this abolishment, we will set the featured character drop rate to 1%, the same probability as when using the previous featured selection function.


With this adjustment making it easier to obtain SSR Memoria through Wishes, we will present everyone with Memory Crystal×1 which can be used to unlock the Memoria you obtain.

The distribution period will be announced in a future in-game notice, so please check that.

The format where missions are completed every 10 Wishes remains the same as before, but we will adjust so that rewards that could previously be obtained by wishing 300 times can now be obtained by wishing 200 times.

Furthermore, we will increase mission rewards so that rewards can be obtained for up to 400 Wishes. The amount of rewards obtainable from wishing 210-400 times will be the same as the rewards obtainable from wishing 10-200 times.

With the change from Step-Up Wishes being available for 5 rounds to 1 round, the number of Wishes you can make with Step-Up Wishes alone will decrease compared to before, so we will change the mission completion conditions so that the same amount of rewards as before can be obtained with appropriate Wish counts. We will also expand the overall missions so that rewards can be obtained even when wishing more than 200 times, making rewards obtainable up to 400 times.
