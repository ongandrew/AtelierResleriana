June Mission Pass Now Available!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

The Mission Pass for June is now available for purchase.

The Mission Pass can be accessed from Home > Shop > Recommended tab.

※Premium unlock status from the previous month's sales period will not carry over. To obtain premium rewards, you will need to unlock premium for the new Mission Pass again.

About Mission Pass

By earning the specified amount of Mission Pass Points, which are awarded as cumulative completion rewards for Daily Missions, you can obtain various rewards presented in the Mission Pass.

There are Regular rewards and Premium rewards, with Premium rewards becoming available when you unlock Premium.

The Mission Pass can be used free of charge every month, but unlocking Premium requires Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500 each month.

Additionally, the Mission Pass has a sales period, and when the sales period ends, Mission Pass Points earned during that period will be reset and cannot be carried over to the next month's Mission Pass.

For rewards from Mission Passes that have ended sales, only unclaimed rewards that were available for acquisition at the time of expiration will be sent to "Mail."

※Please note that rewards for which acquisition conditions were not met cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock Mission Pass Premium, you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards presented in the currently available Mission Pass. You can check the reward contents from Shop > Recommended > Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, any Premium rewards for which you have already met the acquisition conditions will immediately become available.

When the Mission Pass sales period ends and switches to the next month's Mission Pass, the Premium unlock status of the previous Mission Pass will not carry over. To obtain Premium rewards from a newly released Mission Pass, you will need to unlock Premium for the new Mission Pass again.
