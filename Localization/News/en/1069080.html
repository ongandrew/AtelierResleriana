LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when you perform Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop and Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, as well as purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Pack > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted from 2025/03/06 onwards can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before this date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-Wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Johanna [Phenom's Reborn] to ★5, you can inspire recipes for SSR equipment items that only take effect when equipped by Johanna [Phenom's Reborn].

※For details on SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
