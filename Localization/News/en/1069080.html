Wish Tickets

Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Crow Crossing the Great Sea Johanna LEGEND FES Exclusive) that can be purchased for Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25 respectively, with a limit of 2 purchases each, appears in Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab!

Each Wish Ticket allows you to perform one wish.

※Wishes using Wish Tickets do not grant LEGEND Medals.

※Wish Tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Crow Crossing the Great Sea Johanna LEGEND FES Exclusive) cannot be used in wishes other than "Crow Crossing the Great Sea Johanna LEGEND FES".

※Wish Tickets that exceed their usage period will be lost.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to perform wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR memorias available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR memorias from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-Wish: 11 medals

※Wish Tickets do not grant LEGEND Medals.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When you awaken Johanna [Phenom's Reborn] to ★5, you can be inspired with SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Johanna [Phenom's Reborn].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipes that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※Recipe inspiration has no time limit.
