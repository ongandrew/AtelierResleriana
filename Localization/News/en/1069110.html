Wish Overview

"Alvina or Bepp or Janne Guaranteed Wish" begins!

This wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1~4, and at STEP 4 you can obtain Alvina [Dream of Hero] or Bepp [Mourning Blades] or Janne [Mother's Affection] guaranteed for free!

Furthermore, at STEP 4, when you obtain Alvina [Dream of Hero] or Bepp [Mourning Blades] or Janne [Mother's Affection] that you already have, the amount of pieces obtained is increased! You can obtain Character Pieces ×100 and Wishing Gems ×50.

STEP 1

10-Wish for Lodestar Gems ×3,000

STEP 2

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×3,000

STEP 3

10-Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000

STEP 4

Single Wish for Free

Alvina [Dream of Hero] or Bepp [Mourning Blades] or Janne [Mother's Affection] Guaranteed!

※You can execute 1 complete cycle through STEP 1~4.

※At STEP 4, you will not obtain LEGEND Medals when executing a "Single Wish".

※In STEP 4's Single Wish, you cannot select which guaranteed target you receive.

※Please check provision rates at Wish > Provision Rates/Wish Details > Provision Rates.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to perform wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※During this wish event period, Alvina [Dream of Hero], Bepp [Mourning Blades], and Janne [Mother's Affection] cannot be exchanged for LEGEND Medals.

※SSR memorias available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR memorias from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●STEP 1, STEP 2: 13 medals

●STEP 3: 10 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from STEP 4's free wish.

Important Notes

●This wish cannot be used with Wish Tickets. Additionally, STEP 2 and STEP 3 are limited to paid Lodestar Gems only, so they cannot be used with free Lodestar Gems.

●Please check provision rates at Wish > Provision Rates/Wish Details > Provision Rates.

●After completing one full cycle of this wish, it will be hidden from the wish list.

●When you obtain a character you already have, they are automatically converted to character pieces and wishing gems.

●The event period and content may be changed without notice. Please understand this in advance.
