Event "Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1" Now Underway!

Event "Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1" is now underway!

Enjoy an event featuring Rorona, Totori, Mimi and others!

This event consists of Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Part 2 and Part 3 are scheduled to be added later.

Additionally, you can take on "Score Battles" that provide event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as high-difficulty battles called "Boss Challenges".

The Score Battles in this event can be challenged by using stamina. Clearing Score Battles will reward you with synthesis materials as well as Battle Coins. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the Exchange Shop for Lodestar Gems, Grains of Hope, ATELIER FES Wish Tickets, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenges will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Furthermore, this event introduces "SP Challenges", high-difficulty battles that exceed even Boss Challenges!

Clearing them will reward you with Starwhisper Stone Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenges and SP Challenges, Johanna [Phenom's Reborn], Mimi [Proud Voyager], and characters with the Arland tag receive a power-up!

※"Score Battles" are also scheduled to be added when Part 3 is released.

※"SP Challenges" are also scheduled to be added when Part 2 and Part 3 are released.

※"Boss Challenges" and "Emblems" for Part 2 and Part 3 are scheduled to be added later.

※For details on character power-ups, please check Event＞Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1＞Boss Challenge or SP Challenge＞Battle Info.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenges.

Rewards can be obtained according to the score rank achieved when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Parties" cannot be used in SP Challenges.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenges can be attempted using "Rental Parties" that have specific characters and memoria pre-arranged.

Equipment items and battle items created through synthesis can be freely arranged in Rental Parties.

Try synthesizing items that match the attribute affinities and roles of the characters arranged in the Rental Party, then challenge the Boss Challenges!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using Rental Parties.

※The characters and memoria arranged in Rental Parties cannot be changed. Additionally, the arranged characters and memoria differ depending on the event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, possessing eligible memoria will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials!

Eligible memoria and bonus amounts can be confirmed from Event＞Scholarly Island Kalloston ~The Cursed Book and the Alchemists of Arland~ Part 1＞Score Battle＞Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses can be added up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amounts increase depending on the unlock status of eligible memoria.

※Bonuses apply simply by possessing eligible memoria. They do not need to be arranged in your party.

※When possessing multiple copies of the same memoria, unlock ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V).

Example: If you possess 2 copies of eligible memoria at unlock rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at unlock rank II.
