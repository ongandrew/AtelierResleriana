Wish Overview

The "Meruru [Damsel in Distress] ★6 Awakening Commemoration Wish" is now live!

The ★3 character Meruru [Damsel in Distress], whose ★6 awakening has been unlocked, is featured!

In this wish, you can obtain Meruru [Damsel in Distress] already awakened to ★5!

Furthermore, Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Meruru [Damsel in Distress] ★6 Awakening Commemoration Wish Exclusive) appears in the shop!

Awakening Stage and Character Piece Amount When Obtaining Featured Character

If Meruru [Damsel in Distress] has not yet joined and you obtain Meruru [Damsel in Distress] from this wish for the first time, you can obtain her already awakened to ★5!

Additionally, if Meruru [Damsel in Distress] has already joined and you obtain Meruru [Damsel in Distress] from this wish for the first time, the piece acquisition amount is increased! You can obtain Character Pieces ×1130 and Wish Crystals ×50.

※The piece acquisition amount increase is only once, and from the second time onwards when obtaining the featured character, you can obtain Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Crystals ×50.

Wish Tickets

Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Meruru [Damsel in Distress] ★6 Awakening Commemoration Wish Exclusive) that can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25 respectively appears in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab!

You can execute the wish once per wish ticket ×1.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from wishes using wish tickets.

※Wish tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medals/Meruru [Damsel in Distress] ★6 Awakening Commemoration Wish Exclusive) cannot be used in wishes other than the "Meruru [Damsel in Distress] ★6 Awakening Commemoration Wish".

※Wish tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When using Lodestar Gems to execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop and Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and you can also purchase wish ticket sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish period. ★3 characters that appeared before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted featured SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are targets for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-wish: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals with wish tickets.

Awakening Packs

When you obtain ★3 Meruru [Damsel in Distress], "Awakening Packs" containing character pieces and Plain Cookies appear in Shop > Packs > Limited Time! You can purchase them for 72 hours from the moment the product banner appears on the home screen.

You can purchase the "★4.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×4,500, the "★4 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000, and the "★3.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,000.
