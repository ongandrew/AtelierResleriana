Middle Part Added to Event "Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~"!

The middle part has been added to the event "Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~"!

Enjoy the event featuring Rorona, Totori, Mimi, and others in action!

The event middle part adds new "Dungeons", middle part "Boss Challenge", and "SP Challenge".

In dungeons, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and Dungeon Coins. Additionally, by collecting event-exclusive synthesis materials, you can inspire event-exclusive recipes.

Dungeon Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Lodestar Gems, Hope Fragments, ATELIER FES Wish Tickets, SSR equipment items with trait level V/V, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge will reward you with event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will reward you with Star Prayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Mimi [Proud Voyager], Totori [Endless Voyager], and characters with the Arland tag receive a power-up!

※This event is divided into beginning, middle, and final parts. The final part will be added at a later date.

※"Score Battle" and "SP Challenge" are also scheduled to be added when the final part is released.

※The final part's "Boss Challenge" and "Emblem" will be added at a later date.

※For details on character power-ups, please check Event > Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Middle Part > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Information.

※Clearing high-danger dungeons will automatically mark lower-danger uncleared dungeons as cleared.

※This notice only covers the middle part content. For information about the beginning part of the event "Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~", please see here.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank achieved when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging this tough battle!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" that has specific characters and Memoria pre-configured.

You can freely equip the Rental Party with equipment items and battle items created through synthesis.

Try synthesizing items that match the attribute affinities and roles of the characters in the Rental Party to challenge the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can still be obtained even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the configured characters and Memoria vary by event.
