How to Play

"Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Middle Part LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle available to challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your acquired scores are displayed in ranking format, and higher rankings earn more rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Middle Part LEGEND CHALLENGE", various rewards can be obtained based on ranking position!

Ranking rewards are distributed via "Mail" by logging in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they receive the same ranking.

Example: If 3 users have 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be checked from Event > Isle of Learning Caroston ~The Cursed Book and the Arland Alchemists~ Middle Part LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
