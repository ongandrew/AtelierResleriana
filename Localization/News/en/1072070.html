LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to perform Wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medals tab, as well as used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●1× Wish: 1 medal

●10× Wish: 11 medals

※LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Development

When you awaken Rorona [Wonderful Princess] to ★5, you can develop the recipe for an SSR equipment item that only takes effect when equipped by Rorona [Wonderful Princess].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be developed, please check Home Screen > Recipe Development > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe development.
