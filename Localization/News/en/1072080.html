Wish Overview

"Arland Festival Wonderland Princess Rorona LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" now open!

★3 character Rorona [Wonderful Princess] and Memoria "Princess and Knight" make their debut & are featured in pickup!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1 to 5, with 2× Pickup Rate at STEP 3, and 3× Pickup Rate! at STEP 5!

Additionally, after wishing through STEP 1 to 5, an additional "Bonus Wish" will appear!

The Bonus Wish has 2× Pickup Rate!

Furthermore, after performing a 10× Wish, you can obtain Rorona [Wonderful Princess] Pieces ×50 as a bonus!

●STEP 1-5 Step-Up Wish

STEP 1

STEP 2

10× Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 3

2× Pickup Rate!

10× Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 4

10× Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,500

STEP 5

3× Pickup Rate!

10× Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000

●Bonus Wish

2× Pickup Rate!

10× Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems ×3,000

※The Step-Up Wish can be performed up to 1 full cycle from STEP 1 to 5.

※The Bonus Wish can be performed up to 5 times.

※During the event period, if you obtain a pickup character you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and Wishing Crystals ×50.

Rorona [Wonderful Princess] Debut Commemorative Mission

To commemorate the debut of Rorona [Wonderful Princess], we will hold the limited-time "Rorona [Wonderful Princess] Debut Commemorative Mission"!

※Please note that this has a shorter period than "Arland Festival Wonderland Princess Rorona LEGEND FES" and "Arland Festival Wonderland Princess Rorona LEGEND FES [Paid Only]".

※For mission details, please check Home Screen > Mission > Event > Rorona [Wonderful Princess] Debut Commemorative Mission.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems to perform Wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medals tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their Wish event period. ★3 characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-Up Wish STEP 1-4: 8 medals

●Step-Up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Development

When you awaken Rorona [Wonderful Princess] to ★5, you can develop the recipe for an SSR equipment item that only takes effect when equipped by Rorona [Wonderful Princess].

※For details on the SSR equipment item recipe that can be developed, please check Home Screen > Recipe Development > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe development.
