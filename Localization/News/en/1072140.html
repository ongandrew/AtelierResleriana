How to Play

"Whirlwind!! Judie's Challenge LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation items are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for a higher score!

The scores you obtain are displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards, and there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Whirlwind!! Judie's Challenge LEGEND CHALLENGE", you can obtain various rewards according to your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they will have the same rank.

Example: If there are 3 users with the 10th place score, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Details of the rewards can be checked from Event > Whirlwind!! Judie's Challenge LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
