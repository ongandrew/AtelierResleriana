Rerun Event "Atelier Summer 2024 Swimsuit Resort!" Added!

Challenge the Event Score Battle and the high-difficulty battle "EX Challenge"!

Additionally, by clearing specific event stories, you can discover event-exclusive recipes, and by clearing EX Challenge, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems!

Furthermore, by clearing event stories, you can unlock the "Memory Album"!

※The "Beach House" will not be rerun in this event.

※Unlike the previous event, synthesis materials that could be obtained from "Beach Flag" can now be obtained from Event Score Battle. Also, some unlock conditions for the "Memory Album" have been changed to clearing event stories.

※For details about rerun events, please check the announcement here.

Event Period

After 2025/07/11 Maintenance Ends - Indefinite

How to Play

Use "Memory Keys" to unlock events and challenge Event Score Battles and "EX Challenge"!

Additionally, by clearing specific event stories, you can discover event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, by clearing specific event stories and Event Score Battles, "EX Challenge" will be unlocked!

By clearing EX Challenge, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

EX Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that does not consume stamina.

Please try challenging it with your trained party!

※In rerun events, recipe discovery progress from past events is also carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards from past events, you cannot obtain them again in rerun events.

※Emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For EX Challenge, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

Memory Album

The "Memory Album" consists of event-exclusive short stories that are unlocked by clearing event stories.

The content provides glimpses into the activities of Resna and others during this event that were not covered in the event stories. Play the event and leave many memories in the Memory Album!
