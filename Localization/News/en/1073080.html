Event "Atelier Summer 2025" Part 1 Now Live!

Enjoy the event featuring characters in swimsuit outfits!

This event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Part 2 and Part 3 will be added at a later date.

Additionally, you can challenge the "Dungeon" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials, the high-difficulty battle "Boss Challenge," and the "SP Challenge" battle that exceeds the difficulty of Boss Challenge. Furthermore, by collecting event-exclusive synthesis materials, you can conceive event-exclusive recipes.

When you clear dungeons, you can obtain Dungeon Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Dungeon Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Lodestar Gems, Particles of Hope, Stellar Garb Lanterns, and more.

When you clear Boss Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

Furthermore, this event introduces "Panel Missions"!

When cleared, you can obtain Emerald Crystals and Emerald Fragments, among other rewards.

Also, in this event, Antje [Marine Puppeteer] and Valeria [Radiant Summer], as well as characters with the Seasonal Attire tag are powered up when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge!

※"SP Challenge" will also be added when Part 2 and Part 3 are released.

※Part 2 and Part 3 "Boss Challenge" and "Emblems" will be added at a later date.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Boss Challenge > Battle Information.

※For details on character power-ups in SP Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 BATTLE > SP Challenge > Battle Information.

※When you clear a high-danger dungeon, lower-danger uncompleted dungeons will automatically be set to cleared status.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank achieved when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenges can be attempted using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and memorias already assembled.

Equipment items and battle items created through synthesis can be freely organized into the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the preferred attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and memorias in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the characters and memorias included vary by event.
