Event "Atelier Summer 2025" Part 1 Now Open!

Event "Atelier Summer 2025" Part 1 now open!

Enjoy an event featuring characters in swimsuit outfits!

This event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3. Parts 2 and 3 are scheduled to be added later.

Additionally, you can challenge "Dungeons" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials, high-difficulty battles called "Boss Challenge", and "SP Challenge" which are even higher difficulty battles than Boss Challenge. Furthermore, by collecting event-exclusive synthesis materials, you can discover event-exclusive recipes.

By clearing dungeons, you can obtain Dungeon Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Dungeon Coins can be exchanged at the exchange for Lodestar Gems, Hope Grains, Starcloth Flames, and more.

By clearing Boss Challenge, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

Furthermore, this event introduces "Panel Missions"!

By clearing them, you can obtain Jade Crystals and Jade Fragments.

Also, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Antje [Marine Puppeteer], Valeria [Radiant Summer], and characters with the Seasonal Attire tag are powered up!

※"SP Challenge" is also scheduled to be added when Parts 2 and 3 are added.

※"Boss Challenge" and "Emblems" for Parts 2 and 3 are scheduled to be added later.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Boss Challenge > Battle Info.

※For details on character power-ups in SP Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 BATTLE > SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※When you clear a high-danger dungeon, lower-danger uncleared dungeons will automatically become cleared.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank when clearing quests.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be challenged using a "Rental Party" composed of specific characters and memoria.

You can freely assign synthesis-created equipment items and battle items to the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the favored attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※You can obtain clear rewards even when clearing using a Rental Party.

※Characters and memoria assigned to the Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the assigned characters and memoria differ by event.
