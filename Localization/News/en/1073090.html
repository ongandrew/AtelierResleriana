How to Play

"Atelier Summer 2025 Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as score battles.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Obtained scores are displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards, with no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Atelier Summer 2025 Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE", various rewards can be obtained based on ranking position!

Ranking rewards are distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※When multiple users have the same score, they receive the same rank.

Example: If 3 users have the 10th place score, all 3 users will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Reward details can be checked from Event > Atelier Summer 2025 Part 1 LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Reward List.
