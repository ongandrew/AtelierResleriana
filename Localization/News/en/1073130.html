LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when you use Lodestar Gems to make wishes.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their respective wish event periods. ★3 characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria available for exchange includes newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from active LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●1 Wish: 1 Medal

●10 Wishes: 11 Medals

※Wish Tickets do not grant LEGEND Medals.

When you awaken Antje [Marine Puppeteer] to ★5, you can gain inspiration for SSR equipment item recipes that only take effect when equipped by Antje [Marine Puppeteer].
