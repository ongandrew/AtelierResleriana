▼Antje [Marine Puppeteer] with tanned skin

Skins are a new feature added with this update, allowing you to switch the appearance of certain characters. Using Antje [Marine Puppeteer] as an example, let me explain a bit.


Skin acquisition is determined individually for each skin. For example, if you obtain Antje [Marine Puppeteer] with normal skin, the normal appearance model will be displayed on the character screen and during battles. At this time, if you have not obtained Antje [Marine Puppeteer] with tanned skin, you cannot change to the tanned skin model. If you obtain Antje [Marine Puppeteer] with both skins, you can change skins from the character screen. If you obtain Antje [Marine Puppeteer] with either skin, you can deploy them in your party and take them into battle.

Also, since characters with different skins are identical characters except for their appearance, all stats and skills are shared. Additionally, even if you have obtained characters with both skins, you cannot deploy them in your party simultaneously.

▼Update content introduced in the 2025/07/11 livestream

○"Exploration" renewal

We have renewed the "Exploration" function. The main changes are as follows.

■Main Changes

●Changed exploration reward accumulation time limit to 24 hours

●Changed destinations to 7 attribute-based destinations, abolishing all existing destinations

●Changed to be able to obtain pieces of characters with specialty attributes matching the destination's attribute

●Standardized and adjusted all reward items except pieces across all destinations, changing so synthesis materials do not drop

○You can obtain the following items commonly across all exploration destinations

■Bottled Mana

■Enhancement Drop Ⅱ

■Light Orbs Ⅰ～Ⅲ of each attribute

■Col

●Added ability to obtain new "Treasures" at low probability (these are collection elements recorded in the "Compendium" described later and cannot be used as items)

●Changed so recommended characters update at regular intervals


Regarding exploration, we felt there were issues with having too many destinations making it unclear where to go, and the obtainable rewards no longer feeling attractive.

Therefore, we renewed it as an element that aids the play cycle by setting items that can restore stamina and mana as rewards. Character pieces can now also be narrowed down by attribute, allowing you to choose destinations focused on the attribute you want to strengthen.


We also added a new category of items called "Treasures". Rather than just receiving familiar items, we wanted to add collection elements to add enjoyment when receiving rewards.

These treasures cannot be used as items, but are recorded in the "Compendium" function described later, and you can obtain additional rewards based on the number collected. You can only hold one of each item, but if you obtain duplicates, they are converted to Lodestar Gems ×300.


○New "Compendium" function added

We have added a new "Compendium" function.

The compendium records memoria and treasures you have obtained, and you can receive compendium rewards based on the total number of each recorded.

We felt we wanted to add value to memoria beyond just equipping them in battles, so we added this function to provide enjoyment as a collection element. The treasures that newly appeared with the aforementioned exploration renewal are also included as recorded targets so you can visually enjoy seeing how many you've collected.


Compendium rewards allow you to obtain the following items based on what you've recorded. Please collect memoria and treasures and aim for completion.
