[2025/07/17 Update] About Currently Confirmed Bugs

[2025/07/17 Update]

Thank you for always enjoying "Atelier Resleriana."

We have fixed the following bug.

●Bug that causes the game to become unresponsive at battle start under specific conditions

We have also added the following bug information.

●Bug where Tanned Skin Resna [Pretty Summer] from login bonus cannot be claimed in bulk under specific conditions

List of Fixed Bugs

●Bug that causes the game to become unresponsive at battle start under specific conditions

○Fixed a bug where the game could become unresponsive during the battle start animation when Tanned Skin Resna [Pretty Summer] is included in the party and deployed to battle

List of Bugs Under Investigation

●[07/17 Update] Bug where Tanned Skin Resna [Pretty Summer] from login bonus cannot be claimed in bulk under specific conditions

○When you already have the normal skin Resna [Pretty Summer], the Tanned Skin Resna [Pretty Summer] sent to mail as a login bonus cannot be claimed in bulk (※It is possible to claim individually using the claim button at the bottom right of each mail.)

●Display bug when obtaining a character with a different skin

○When obtaining a character with a different skin from one already owned, an unnecessary white icon appears in the acquisition dialog

●Bug where Grand Recipes display incorrectly on the recipe inspiration reward screen

○Grand Recipes added after the 2025/07/11 maintenance are incorrectly displayed as "Extra" instead of "Grand" on the Home screen > Recipe Inspiration > Recipe Inspiration Count screen

●Bug where the app crashes on some Android devices

○On some Android devices, the app crashes when entering specific quests or stories (※This phenomenon may be avoided by setting Shadow Quality and Background Expression to "Low" in Home screen > Menu > Options > Display tab > Custom settings.)

●Bug in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap"

○Using fast skip in Story Chapter 21-58 "Power Gap" may cause the app to crash (※This bug does not occur when playing the story without using fast skip.)

●Daily Pass bug on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for deferred payment

●Movie playback bug on specific Android devices

○On specific Android devices, the following bugs may occur during movie playback

■Poor video quality

■Stuttering occurs

■Audio desync occurs

■App crashes when playing movies repeatedly

●Display bug for mail receipt status

○After receiving characters, memorias, or character pieces via mail, the display within the mail does not switch to "received" (※This is a display-only bug, and rewards can be received without any issues.)

●Behavior bug in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request 3," the app may crash at specific timing (※This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Bug when viewing video ads on specific devices

○On specific devices, the app may crash when viewing video ads under certain environment settings (※The number of viewable times will not decrease, so please rest assured. This may be improved by lowering the screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other bugs as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
