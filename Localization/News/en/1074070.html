Total War

"Total War" is an event where you challenge one battle at a time from multiple battles arranged as tiles, with the goal of ultimately having all battles in a cleared state simultaneously.

In "Total War," the following rules apply to characters that can be deployed:

●Characters who have cleared any battle cannot be deployed in other battles within the event

●By resetting the clear status of battles, you can make characters that were deployed in those battles available for deployment in other battles within the event again

Additionally, the following three types of rewards are available in this event:

●First Clear Rewards

○Can be obtained when clearing each battle for the first time

●Line Achievement Rewards

○Can be obtained by having all battles in each row in a cleared state simultaneously

●Completion Rewards

○Can be obtained by having all battles in a cleared state simultaneously

For all rewards, once you have received a reward, you cannot receive the reward again even if you reset the battle clear status and clear the same battle again or achieve the same line.
