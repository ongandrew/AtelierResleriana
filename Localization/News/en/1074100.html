Wish Overview

"Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish" is now live!

The ★3 character Shallotte [Girls Ambitious], whose ★6 Awakening has been unlocked, is featured!

In this Wish, you can obtain Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] already awakened to ★5!

Additionally, Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish Exclusive) appears in the Shop!

Awakening Stage When Obtaining Featured Character and Amount of Character Pieces Obtained

If you obtain Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] for the first time from this Wish when Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] is not yet joined, you can obtain her already awakened to ★5!

Also, if you obtain Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] for the first time from this Wish when Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] is already joined, the amount of pieces obtained is increased! You can obtain Character Pieces ×1130 and Wish Crystals ×50.

※The increased amount of pieces is only for the first time, and from the second time onward when obtaining the featured character, you will obtain Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Crystals ×50.

Wish Ticket

Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish Exclusive) appears in Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab, which can be purchased up to 2 times each with Lodestar Gems ×1,500 and LEGEND Medals ×25!

Each Wish Ticket ×1 allows you to execute one Wish.

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wishes using Wish Tickets.

※Wish Tickets obtained from Wish Ticket Set (No Medal/Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish Exclusive) cannot be used in Wishes other than "Shallotte [Girls Ambitious] ★6 Release Commemoration Wish."

※Wish Tickets that have passed their expiration date will disappear.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you execute a Wish using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters or SSR memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted from 2025/03/06 onward can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR memorias are the newly appeared featured SSR memorias from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR memorias that are eligible for memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●1 Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from Wish Tickets.
