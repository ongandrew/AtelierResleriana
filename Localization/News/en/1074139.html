About Characters with Special Skins

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We have received numerous inquiries about characters with special "skins," so we would like to explain this again.


Available Skins and Battle Deployment Based on Character Acquisition Status

The available skins and battle deployment based on character acquisition status are as shown above.

Characters with special skins can be taken into battle even without acquiring the regular skin version of the character.

Furthermore, if you acquire both the regular skin character and the special skin character, you can choose and set your preferred appearance.

Additionally, if you acquire a special skin character after acquiring the regular skin character, you can obtain Character Pieces ×50 and Wish Crystals ×50. The same applies if you acquire them in the reverse order.


Acquire characters with special skins and enjoy battles and Photo Mode!
