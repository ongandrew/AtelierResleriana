LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you use Lodestar Gems to make wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before then can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria eligible for Memoria bonuses.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-Up Wish STEP 1~4: 8 medals

●Step-Up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

SSR Equipment Recipe Conception

※For details about conceivable SSR equipment item recipes, please check Home Screen > Recipe Conception > Awakening.
