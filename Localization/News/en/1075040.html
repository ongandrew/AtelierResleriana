Town Added to "Atelier Summer 2025" Event!

Town added to the "Atelier Summer 2025" event!

Enjoy the event featuring characters in swimsuit outfits!

In addition to "Town," "Score Battle," "Boss Challenge," and "SP Challenge" will also be added.

In Score Battle, you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials, Battle Coins, and Home Medals.

By collecting event-exclusive synthesis materials, you can inspire event-exclusive recipes.

Home Medals can be exchanged at the exchange shop for event scenes from the "Atelier Summer 2024" event. The exchanged event scenes can be set as your home screen background via Home Screen > Home Background Settings.

Additionally, Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Lodestar Gems, Hope Particles, ATELIER FES Wish Tickets, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge will allow you to obtain event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will allow you to obtain Lodestar Gem Seeds.

Additionally, in this event, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Criselda [Seaside Butterfly], Lara [Seaside Queen], and characters with the Seasonal Attire tag will be powered up!

※This event is divided into Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3.

※For details about character power-ups in Boss Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Boss Challenge > Battle Info.

※For details about character power-ups in SP Challenge, please check Event > Atelier Summer 2025 BATTLE > SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※This notice only covers the Town content. For Part 1 of the "Atelier Summer 2025" event, please see here.

※For Part 2 of the "Atelier Summer 2025" event, please see here.

Town

In "Town," you can control specific characters and talk to other characters in the town to enjoy stories and progress town quests.

Various characters appear in the town! You might see character interactions that are rarely seen in other stories...?

Please enjoy freely moving around town, looking at the scenery, and talking to characters!

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be attempted using a "Rental Party" composed of specific characters and Memoria.

You can freely organize equipment items and battle items created through synthesis into the Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the specialty attributes and roles of the characters in the Rental Party and challenge the Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can still be obtained when clearing using a Rental Party.

※The characters and Memoria in the Rental Party cannot be changed. Additionally, the characters and Memoria vary by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, having eligible Memoria will increase the drop amount of synthesis materials!

Eligible Memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > Atelier Summer 2025 > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses stack up to a maximum of 200%. The bonus amount increases based on the unlock status of eligible Memoria.

※Simply owning eligible Memoria applies the bonus. They do not need to be in your party.

※When owning multiple copies of the same Memoria, the unlock ranks are combined to increase the bonus amount (maximum V).

Example: If you own 2 copies of eligible Memoria at unlock rank I, it will be calculated as 1 Memoria at unlock rank II.
