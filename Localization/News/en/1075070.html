LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

LEGEND Medals can be obtained when performing wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medals tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Tickets tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their respective wish event periods. ★3 characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※Exchangeable SSR Memoria are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from currently running LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10-pull Wish: 11 medals

※LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from Wish Tickets.

SSR Equipment Recipe Inspiration

When Criselda [Seaside Butterfly] is awakened to ★5, you can inspire a recipe for an SSR equipment item that only takes effect when equipped by Criselda [Seaside Butterfly].

※For details about the SSR equipment item recipe that can be inspired, please check Home Screen > Recipe Inspiration > Awakening.

※There is no time limit for recipe inspiration.
