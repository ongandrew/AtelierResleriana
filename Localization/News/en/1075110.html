Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign Now Live!

Thank you for always enjoying Atelier Resleriana.

We are now holding the "Synthesis! Synth-GO! Campaign" where you can earn up to 3,000 Lodestar Gems based on your equipment item synthesis results!

Synthesize equipment items during the campaign period, and if your item synthesis is successful, you'll receive Lodestar Gems based on your synthesis results!

Additionally, if you exceed 300 synthesis attempts during the period, you can earn 3,000 Lodestar Gems as the "Synth-GO! Prize".
You can check your current synthesis count from Home > Missions > Events.

Synthesize your desired equipment items and be sure to participate in this campaign!
