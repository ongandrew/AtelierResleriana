Part 2 Added to "Atelier Summer 2025" Event!

Part 2 has been added to the "Atelier Summer 2025" event!

Enjoy the event featuring characters in swimsuit outfits!

In Part 2 of the event, the new feature "Mission Battles" and Part 2's event story, along with "SP Challenge" have been added.

Mission Battles are battles where you can earn rewards by clearing multiple missions set for each battle.

You can earn rewards such as Lodestar Gems, Hope Fragments, and Starprayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, based on the total number of missions achieved, you can earn rewards and event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge will earn you Starprayer Stone Seeds.

Furthermore, when challenging SP Challenge, Johanna [Brilliant Diver], Criselda [Seaside Butterfly], and characters with the Seasonal Attire tag will be powered up!

Mission Battles

Mission Battles are battles where you can earn rewards by clearing multiple missions set for each battle.

There are two types of rewards: mission completion rewards and rewards earned based on the total number of missions achieved.

Aim to clear all battle missions by trying various team compositions and challenging the battles!

SP Challenge

SP Challenge features high-difficulty battles that exceed Boss Challenges.

Rewards can be earned based on the score rank achieved when clearing quests.

Please try challenging these engaging battles!

※You cannot use "Rental Party" in SP Challenge.
