Rerun Event "Encounter of Chaos and Order" Added!

Enjoy this special format story event focused on "Brawler" Magda and "Alchemist Noble" Brad!

Also, take on the Boss Challenge and high-difficulty battle "EX Challenge"!

Additionally, by clearing specific Boss Challenges, you can conceive event-exclusive recipes, and by clearing the EX Challenge, you can obtain an event-exclusive emblem!

※Unlike the previous run, we have removed the event point acquisition requirement from the event story unlock conditions. We have also made adjustments to reduce some enemy attribute resistances.

※For details about the rerun event, please check this announcement.

Event Period

2025/08/13 12:00〜Indefinite

How to Play

Use "Memory Keys" to unlock the event and challenge the Boss Challenges and "EX Challenge"!

In this event, choices will be displayed during the event story, and the subsequent story will change depending on what you choose. Depending on your choices, you may even branch into IF routes, so enjoy reading through the event story while considering which choices to make!

Also, by clearing specific Boss Challenges, you can conceive event-exclusive recipes.

Furthermore, when you clear all Boss Challenges, the "EX Challenge" will be unlocked!

By clearing the EX Challenge, you can obtain an event-exclusive emblem.

The EX Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that does not consume stamina.

Please try challenging it with your trained party!

※Only some stories have choices.

※Even if you choose a choice that leads to an IF route, you can replay the story and choose different choices to read the next story.

※In rerun events, past event recipe conception status is also carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards in past events, you cannot obtain them again in the rerun event.

※Emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For EX Challenge, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

※Please note that the "Encounter of Chaos and Order Commemoration LOGIN BONUS" and "Event Exchange Shop" that were held in the past will not be brought back in this event.

Important Notes
