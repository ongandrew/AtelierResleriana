How to Play

"Encounter of Chaos and Order LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as in Score Battles.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

Your obtained scores are displayed in ranking format, and the higher your rank, the more rewards you can obtain.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Encounter of Chaos and Order LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can obtain various rewards according to your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※If there are multiple users with the same score, they will receive the same rank.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 will be ranked 10th, and the next user will be ranked 13th.

Detailed rewards can be checked from Event > Encounter of Chaos and Order LEGEND CHALLENGE > Ranking > Ranking Rewards List.
