Gifts

In "Gifts", you can give items to specific characters as presents. The characters you can give gifts to are determined for each event, and in this event, you can give gifts to Kimon [Devoted Loyal Ninja].

When you give an item as a gift, you accumulate Friendship Points, and when you obtain a certain amount of Friendship Points, your Affection Level increases. By raising the Affection Level, you can progress through the event story.

The only item you can give to Kimon as a gift in this event is Supreme Offering Sake, which can be obtained by clearing event missions. When the Affection Level reaches a certain point, you can read Kimon's special stories.

※Items given to Kimon as "Gifts" will be consumed.

※Supreme Offering Sake can only be obtained through event missions in the "Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation" event. Please note that it cannot be synthesized.

※If you convert Supreme Offering Sake, you will not be able to raise Kimon's Affection Level for a certain period, and you will not be able to meet the unlock conditions for event stories, so please be careful (it will become obtainable through means other than event missions at a later date).

※Event missions can be checked from Home Screen > Missions > Event > Kurogane Island Monster Extermination Operation.
