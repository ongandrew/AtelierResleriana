LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange

When you use Lodestar Gems and execute wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and you can also purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters that debuted on or after 03/06/2025 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are targets for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 Medal

●10× Wish: 11 Medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals with Wish Tickets.
