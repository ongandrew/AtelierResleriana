About the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass

By obtaining the specified number of Event Mission PT that serve as clear rewards for the event mission "2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission", you can obtain various rewards presented within the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass.

Rewards include Regular rewards and Premium rewards, and Premium rewards can be obtained by unlocking Premium.

The 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass can be used free of charge, but unlocking Premium requires Paid Lodestar Gem×500.

Additionally, the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass has a sales period, and for rewards from the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass whose sales have ended, only unreceived rewards that were available for acquisition at the time of termination will be sent to "Mail".

※For mission details, please check from Home Screen > Mission > Event > 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission.

※Please note that rewards for which acquisition conditions were not met cannot be obtained.

About Unlocking Premium

When you unlock Premium for the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass, you will be able to obtain the Premium rewards presented within the 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass that is currently on sale at the time of unlocking. You can check the reward contents from Shop > Recommended > 2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, Premium rewards for which acquisition conditions have already been met will become immediately available for acquisition.

※Before unlocking Premium, please be sure to confirm the Premium reward contents and that the mission pass you are purchasing is the "2nd Anniversary Countdown Mission Pass" without any mistakes.
