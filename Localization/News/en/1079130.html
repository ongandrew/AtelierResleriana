Wish Overview

"3 Characters Simultaneous ★6 Release Commemoration Select Guaranteed Wish" now live!

This Wish is a step-up wish from STEP 1 to 4, where at STEP 4 you can select one character from Elie [Child of Cheesecake], Judie [Hero of Tempest], or Izana [Return of the Knight] for free and obtain them guaranteed!

STEP1

10x Wish for Lodestar Gems ×3,000

STEP2

10x Wish for Lodestar Gems ×3,000

STEP3

10x Wish for Lodestar Gems ×3,000

STEP4

Free 1x Wish

Selected character is guaranteed!

※You can complete STEPs 1 through 4 3 times.

※At STEP 4, you will not obtain LEGEND Medals when executing "1x Wish".

※Selectable characters can be confirmed at Wish ＞ Selectable Characters.

※You can only select characters at STEP 4. Characters cannot be selected at other STEPs.

※At STEPs other than STEP 4, you can obtain characters and memoria displayed in the drop rates by chance.

※Drop rates can be confirmed at Wish ＞ Drop Rates/Wish Details ＞ Drop Rates.

Awakening Stage and Character Pieces Obtained When Obtaining Selectable Characters at STEP 4

When you obtain Elie [Child of Cheesecake], Judie [Hero of Tempest], or Izana [Return of the Knight] for the first time from this Wish while they are not yet in your party, you can obtain them already awakened to ★5!

Additionally, when you obtain Elie [Child of Cheesecake], Judie [Hero of Tempest], or Izana [Return of the Knight] for the first time from this Wish while they are already in your party, the piece acquisition amount is increased! You can obtain Character Pieces ×1130 and Wish Brightstone ×50.

※The increased piece acquisition amount is only once, and when obtaining selectable characters at STEP 4 for the second time and beyond, you can obtain Character Pieces ×100 and Wish Brightstone ×50.

LEGEND Medal・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

When you make wishes using Lodestar Gems, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES Exchange Shop or Shop ＞ Character Memoria ＞ LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters that debuted on 2025/03/06 or later can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during their wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are the newly debuted pick-up SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●STEPs 1-3: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals from the free wish at STEP 4.

Awakening Packs

When you obtain Elie [Child of Cheesecake], Judie [Hero of Tempest], or Izana [Return of the Knight], "Awakening Packs" containing Character Pieces and Plain Cookies appear in Shop ＞ Packs ＞ Limited Time! You can purchase them for 72 hours from the moment the product banner appears on the Home screen.

You can purchase "★4.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×4,500, "★4 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×2,000, and "★3.5 Awakening Pack" for Paid Lodestar Gems ×1,000.
