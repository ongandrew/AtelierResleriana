○"Neo Board" introduction

As a new character enhancement element, we have added the "Neo Board".

On the Neo Board, similar to Growth Boards and EX Boards, you can receive benefits such as character status increases and synthesis success rate increases, and as a unique element of Neo Boards, you can acquire Board Abilities. Board Abilities are granted with performance that aligns with the character's characteristics. The image shown is just one example.

Breakers and Supporters, who don't rely heavily on dealing damage or HP and receive fewer benefits from status increases, will become more user-friendly through acquiring Board Abilities.


Neo Boards can be unlocked when specific conditions are met. Please check the in-game help for details.

Additionally, when unlocking Neo Boards, you will use new materials: Great Sacred Orbs, Supreme Treasure Orbs, and Light Orbs V of each color.


To commemorate the addition of Neo Boards, we will hold "Neo Board Addition Commemorative Missions" where you can obtain Great Sacred Orbs and other materials, sell "2nd Anniversary Neo Board Unlock Packs" where you can obtain Supreme Treasure Orbs and Light Orbs V, and present the Great Sacred Orbs, Supreme Treasure Orbs, and Light Orbs V of each color needed to enhance one character.

Please try unlocking the Neo Board for your favorite character!

【2025/09/12 Scheduled Update Content】

○"Chapter Skip" function added

We will add a function to skip Main Story chapters.


The Main Story of Atelier Resleriana has become quite voluminous. Because of this, there were concerns that it would take time for new players to catch up to the latest story, making it difficult for them to enjoy event stories and other content.

Therefore, so that you can enjoy new characters and stories, we decided it was necessary to allow users the option to skip stories at their discretion, and we will add this function from 2025/09/12.


Even if you skip, you can still enjoy the stories through Story Recollection. The "synopsis" displayed when skipping can also be confirmed in Story Recollection.

Since we would like you to enjoy the Main Story as much as possible, the latest chapter and the chapter immediately before the latest chapter cannot be skipped, so we hope you will read the new stories.

【2025/09/19 Scheduled Update Content】

○Battle screen improvements for better understanding

To improve battle comprehensibility, we will update some of the screen displays during battle.

■Battle Screen Update Content

●Addition of "CHANCE!" display

○When selecting skills, if there are enemies that can be defeated or broken, a "CHANCE!" display will appear on the enemy icons displayed at the top of the screen

●Wait display on timeline

○Wait values will now be displayed on the timeline. By displaying numbers, it becomes easier to understand why the current timeline is displayed in that order

●Addition of enemy and ally status display

○The total values of status changes due to status effects and equipment effects applied to enemies and allies will be displayed. This makes it easier to imagine the damage dealt by skills through numerical calculation

●Expansion of skill selection detail dialog display and additional information

○The skill selection detail dialog will be expanded, increasing the amount of information that can be viewed at once. Additionally, predicted values for skill damage and break damage will be displayed

While there are many requests to make battles more understandable, this time we focused on display improvements related to action order and damage during battle.


○Addition of skip settings for some effects to options

To enable comfortable gameplay focused on smooth operation of Atelier Resleriana, we will add skip settings for some effects to the options.

By utilizing this, you can play with reduced loading times and camera movement times.

Additionally, the time it takes for screen transitions will also be shortened, so please try this setting if you want to play casually with reduced device load.
