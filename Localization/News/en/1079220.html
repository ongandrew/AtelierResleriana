About Mission Pass

By obtaining a specified number of Mission PT, which is the total achievement reward for Daily Missions, you can acquire various rewards presented in the Mission Pass.

There are Regular Rewards and Premium Rewards, and Premium Rewards can be obtained by unlocking Premium.

Mission Pass can be used free of charge every month, but unlocking Premium requires 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems per month.

Also, Mission Pass has a sales period, and when the sales period ends, Mission PT acquired during that period will be reset and cannot be carried over to the Mission Pass sold the following month.

For rewards from Mission Pass whose sales have ended, only unreceived rewards that were obtainable at the time of termination will be sent to "Mail."

※Please note that rewards for which acquisition conditions were not met cannot be obtained.

About Premium Unlock

When you unlock Mission Pass Premium, you will be able to obtain the Premium Rewards presented in the Mission Pass currently on sale at the time of unlock. The content of rewards can be confirmed from Shop > Recommended > Mission Pass > Premium Exchange Confirmation Dialog.

When unlocking Premium, Premium Rewards whose acquisition conditions have already been met will become immediately obtainable.

When the Mission Pass sales period ends and switches to the following month's Mission Pass, the previous Mission Pass Premium unlock status will not be carried over. To obtain Premium Rewards from the newly sold Mission Pass, you need to unlock the Premium for the new Mission Pass again.
