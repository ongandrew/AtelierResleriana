How to Play

"Sparkling Stellar Star Festival! LEGEND CHALLENGE" is an event where you play high-difficulty battles in score attack format.

There is only one battle you can challenge, and the score evaluation criteria are the same as Score Battle.

Challenge repeatedly and aim for higher scores!

The scores you obtain will be displayed in ranking format, with higher rankings earning more rewards.

※This event only has ranking rewards; there are no score rewards or clear rewards.

Ranking Rewards

In "Sparkling Stellar Star Festival! LEGEND CHALLENGE," you can obtain various rewards based on your ranking position!

Ranking rewards will be distributed via "Mail" when you log in after the event period ends.

※If multiple users have the same score, they will have the same ranking.

Example: If there are 3 users with 10th place scores, all 3 users will be 10th place, and the next user will be 13th place.

For detailed rewards, check Event > Sparkling Stellar Star Festival! LEGEND CHALLENGE > Rankings > Ranking Reward List.
