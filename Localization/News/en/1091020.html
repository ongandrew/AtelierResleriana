Ver2.6.7 Update Completion Notice

The maintenance for the Ver2.6.7 update has been completed.

The update contents are as follows.

Thank you very much for your cooperation during maintenance.

Please continue to enjoy "Atelier Resleriana".

Update Contents

●Application of additional data

●Battle screen improvements

○To improve battle clarity, we have updated the battle screen as follows

■Addition of "CHANCE!" display

When selecting skills, if there are enemies that can be defeated or broken, "CHANCE!" will be displayed on the enemy icons at the top of the screen

■Wait display on timeline

Wait values will now be displayed on the timeline. By showing numerical values, it becomes easier to understand why the current timeline is displayed in its order

■Addition of enemy and ally status display

The total value of status changes from status effects and equipment effects applied to enemies and allies will now be displayed. This makes it easier to visualize skill damage through numerical calculation

■Expansion and additional information for skill selection detail dialog

The detail dialog when selecting skills has been expanded, increasing the amount of information that can be viewed at a glance. Additionally, predicted values for skill damage and break damage will now be displayed

●Addition of home banners

○We have added home banners to the bottom left of the home screen

○You can navigate to the corresponding content by tapping the banners

○With the addition of home banners, guide missions will now be displayed on the leftmost home banner

●Addition of options

○We have added the following options to Home Screen > Menu > Options > Game tab

■Wait visualization

■Main story transition effect skip

■Recipe inspiration effect skip

■Atelier interior effect skip

■Café interior effect skip

○With these additions, we have organized the Game tab by category and partially adjusted the option arrangement

●Help updates

○We have updated the following help sections

■Home

●Event mission text adjustment

○For the event missions of the event "Strange and Mysterious Kikai Island", we have added "Part 1" to some mission condition descriptions in preparation for the addition of Part 2

List of Fixed Issues
