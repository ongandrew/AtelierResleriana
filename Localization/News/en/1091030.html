List of Issues Under Investigation

●[Added 09/19] Issue where status change total values are not displayed correctly under certain conditions

○Under the following conditions, status change total values are not displayed correctly

■When unique status changes that increase in effect based on level are applied, the Lv1 effect amount is reflected in the status change total regardless of the actual applied level

■The effect amounts of status changes that increase combat item damage and critical damage are incorrectly added to the status change total as skill damage and critical damage increases

●[Added 09/19] Enemy information display issue for enemies with multiple break gauges

○For enemies with multiple break gauges, when displaying enemy information and switching break gauge information using the button to the right of the break gauge, the "×2" and "×1" labels on the button are displayed overlapping

●Neo Board ability name display issue when unlocking

○When unlocking Neo Board, if a board ability rank increases, the displayed board ability name shows the name of one rank lower than it should

●Issue where Resna [Pretty Summer] tanned skin from login bonus cannot be batch collected under certain conditions

○If you already own the regular skin version of Resna [Pretty Summer], the tanned skin version sent via login bonus mail cannot be batch collected (※You can still receive it individually using the receive button in the bottom right of the mail.)

●Daily Pass issue on Android devices

○On Android devices, the Daily Pass does not include a link for post-payment billing

●Movie playback issues on certain Android devices

○On certain Android devices, the following issues may occur during movie playback

■Degraded video quality

■Stuttering playback

■Audio desynchronization

■Forced termination when repeatedly playing movies

●Mail receipt status display issue

○Despite receiving characters, memorias, or character pieces from mail, the display within the mail does not change to show received status (※This is a display-only issue and rewards can be received without problems.)

●Behavior issue in specific battles

○In Battle Chapter 1-35 "Emergency Request・3", the app may force close at certain timings (※This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

●Video advertisement viewing issue on certain devices

○On certain devices with specific environment settings, the app may force close when viewing video advertisements (※Please be assured that viewable attempts will not decrease. This may be improved by lowering screen resolution or 3D rendering resolution.)

We will continue to address other issues as they are discovered.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.
