Total War

"Total War" is an event where you challenge battles one at a time from multiple battles arranged as squares, with the goal of ultimately clearing all battles simultaneously.

In "Total War," the following rules apply to characters that can be deployed:

●Characters who have cleared any battle cannot be deployed in other battles within the event

●By resetting the clear status of battles, you can make characters who were deployed in those battles available for deployment in event battles again

Additionally, this event offers the following three types of rewards:

●First Clear Rewards

○Obtained when clearing each battle for the first time

●Line Achievement Rewards

○Obtained by simultaneously clearing all battles in each row

●Completion Rewards

○Obtained by simultaneously clearing all battles

For all rewards, once you have received a reward, you cannot receive it again even if you reset the battle clear status and clear the same battles again or achieve lines again.
