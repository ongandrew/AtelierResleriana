Part 2 Added to Event "Strange and Mysterious Kikai Island"!

Part 2 has been added to the event "Strange and Mysterious Kikai Island"!

In Part 2 of the event, new "Mission Battles", Part 2 event story, "Score Battle", "Boss Challenge", and "SP Challenge" have been added.

By clearing Mission Battle missions, you can obtain rewards such as Sacred Orb, Memory Fragment, and ATELIER FES Wish Ticket.

By clearing Score Battles, you can obtain Battle Coins in addition to synthesis materials. Battle Coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for event scenes from events like "Sparkling Sekirara Star Festival!" and Sacred Orbs. The event scenes you exchange can be set as your home screen through Home Screen > Home Background Settings.

By clearing Boss Challenge, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

By clearing SP Challenge, you can obtain Stellar Prayer Stone Seed.

Additionally, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Goo [Faithful Fighter], Jelka [Nifty Rover], and characters with Nine Sages tags are powered up!

※This event is divided into Part 1 and Part 2.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, please check Event > Strange and Mysterious Kikai Island > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Info.

Mission Battle

Mission Battle is a battle where you can obtain rewards by clearing multiple missions set for each battle.

There are two types of rewards: mission achievement rewards and rewards obtainable based on the total number of missions achieved.

Aim to clear all battle missions while trying various team compositions!

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that exceeds Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank when clearing the quest.

Please try challenging this challenging battle!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be challenged using a "Rental Party" with specific characters and memorias already deployed.

You can freely equip the Rental Party with equipment items and combat items created through synthesis.

Try synthesizing items that match the specialty attributes and roles of characters deployed in the Rental Party to challenge Boss Challenge!

※You can still obtain clear rewards even when clearing using Rental Party.

※The characters and memorias deployed in Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, the deployed characters and memorias differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess target memorias for the bonus, synthesis material drop amounts increase!

You can check target memorias and bonus amounts from Event > Strange and Mysterious Kikai Island > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amounts increase based on the unlock status of target memorias.

※Bonuses apply simply by possessing target memorias. They do not need to be deployed in your party.

※If you possess multiple of the same memoria, unlock ranks are combined to increase bonus amounts (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 copies of a target memoria at unlock rank I, it will be calculated as 1 memoria at unlock rank II.
