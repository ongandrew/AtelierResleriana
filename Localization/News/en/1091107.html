Bug Details

In the mission battles for the event "Weird and Wondrous Kikai Island," some missions do not clear even when the conditions described in the achievement requirements are met.

Response

We have fixed the issue through a data update at approximately 20:43 on September 19, 2025, so that missions can now be cleared by meeting the described conditions.

※Missions that could not be completed during the bug occurrence can be cleared by meeting the conditions again.
