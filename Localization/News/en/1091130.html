Wish Overview

The "Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES [Paid Only]" is now open!

This Wish is a Step-Up Wish with STEP 1~4, where STEP 3 features 2x Pickup Rate, and STEP 4 allows you to obtain SSR Memoria "For Those I Believe In" or SSR Memoria "For My Given Duty" guaranteed for free!

STEP1

10x Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems x1,500

STEP2

10x Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems x1,500

STEP3

2x Pickup Rate!

10x Wish for Paid Lodestar Gems x1,500

STEP4

Single Wish for free

SSR Memoria "For Those I Believe In" or

SSR Memoria "For My Given Duty" guaranteed!

※The Step-Up Wish can be completed up to 5 cycles maximum from STEP 1~4.

※LEGEND Medals are not obtained when performing a "Single Wish" in STEP 4.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange

When using Lodestar Gems to make Wishes, you can obtain LEGEND Medals.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 Characters and SSR Memorias at the LEGEND FES Exchange or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medals tab.

※Characters that debuted after 2025/03/06 can be obtained through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 Characters that debuted before that date can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memorias available for exchange are the newly debuted pickup SSR Memorias from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memorias eligible for Memoria Bonus.

※LEGEND Medals do not have an expiration date.

※LEGEND Medal acquisition amounts are as follows:

●Step-Up Wish STEP 1~3: 8 medals each

※LEGEND Medals cannot be obtained from the free Wish in STEP 4.
