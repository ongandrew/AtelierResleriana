Letter from the Development Team vol.26

Thank you for always playing Atelier Resleriana.

This is Hosoi, producer of Atelier Resleriana.


Today, September 23rd, 2025, Atelier Resleriana has reached its 2nd anniversary!

Among the many titles being created, we have been able to continue operating for 2 years since service began thanks to the support of many of you. We would like to express our heartfelt gratitude once again. We hope you will continue to look forward to the future journeys of Resna, Valeria, and Alvina.

In this letter, we would like to inform you about the event content being held from today, September 23rd, 2025, and the collaboration event scheduled for the end of September.


▼Event content being held from September 23rd, 2025

○"Congratulations! 2nd Anniversary on September 23rd! LOGIN BONUS"

We are holding the "Congratulations! 2nd Anniversary on September 23rd! LOGIN BONUS" where you can obtain Free Lodestar Gems x3,000. You can obtain these by logging in once during the event period, so please be sure to log in and enjoy!

Event Period

2025/09/23 12:00~2025/09/30 11:59


◯"Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES" Currently Running

We are currently holding the "Nine Great Legends: Faith and Duty LEGEND FES".

In this Wish, SSR Memoria "For Those I Believe In" and SSR Memoria "For My Given Duty", which use illustrations from the key visual commemorating the 2nd anniversary, make their debut & are featured as pickups.

Additionally, the paid-only version is a Step-Up Wish with STEP 1~4, where STEP 3 features 2x Pickup Rate, and STEP 4 allows you to obtain SSR Memoria "For Those I Believe In" or SSR Memoria "For My Given Duty" guaranteed for free.


▼DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation Collaboration Event Confirmed!

The collaboration event between Atelier Resleriana and DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation has been confirmed!


Atelier Resleriana's first collaboration event is scheduled to begin at the end of September 2025. As our first collaboration, we have included content that is different from the events we have held so far, so that everyone can enjoy it. We hope you will look forward to it.

Details about the collaboration, including the event start date, will be announced during the Tokyo Game Show 2025 live broadcast on September 26th, 2025. Please come join us for the live broadcast on that day.


That concludes the content we wanted to share with you this time.


We will continue to work hard to grow this into a good game together with all of our users, so please continue to support Atelier Resleriana.
