Rerun Event "The Matchmaker: A Star-Lit Path" Added!

Challenge the "Score Battle" and high-difficulty battles in "Boss Challenge"!

Event Period

2025/09/24 12:00～Indefinite

How to Play

Use "Memory Keys" to unlock the event and challenge Score Battles and Boss Challenges!

Also, by clearing specific Score Battles, you can conceive event-exclusive recipes.

By clearing specific Boss Challenges, you can obtain event-exclusive emblems.

Boss Challenges are high-difficulty battles that do not consume stamina.

Please try challenging them with your trained party!

※In rerun events, your recipe conception progress from past events is carried over.

※If you have already obtained story clear rewards or first-time rewards from past events, you cannot obtain them again in the rerun event.

※Your emblem acquisition status from past events is carried over.

※For Boss Challenges, you can only obtain first-time clear rewards for each score rank.

※Please note that the previously held "HAPPY NEW YEAR 2025 LOGIN BONUS" and "Event Exchange Shop" will not be rerun in this event.
