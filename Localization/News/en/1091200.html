▼Recap of collaboration event content introduced in the live broadcast

○Event "DOAXVV Collaboration" begins!

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin the event "DOAXVV Collaboration".

The stage for this collaboration event is the tropical paradise "Venus Islands". The story begins when Resna and the others get lost and end up here.

On Venus Islands, a festival of goddesses called "Venus Fest" is being held to compete in strength and beauty, and Resna and Alvina decide to participate in the festival at the suggestion of Marie Rose and Honoka.

Please play the event to find out the continuation of the story.


This event is divided into four parts: opening, middle, latter, and conclusion, and starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin with the opening part "DOAXVV Collaboration Opening - To the Tropical Venus Islands!".

Also, this event has an element called "Activities".

You can enjoy the familiar activity performances from DOAXVV. By selecting a character and activity, and presenting the specified synthesis items, you can enjoy various performances depending on the selected activity. Furthermore, the character's event-exclusive parameters will increase, which will allow you to read the continuation of the event story and unlock gravure panels

Note that two types of activities will be available when the event starts after the September 29 maintenance, and two additional activities will become available later.


〇"Tropical Venuses Marie Rose LEGEND FES" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin "Tropical Venuses Marie Rose LEGEND FES".

In this Wish, ★3 CharacterMarie Rose [Twinkle Rose] and SSR Memoria "Marie Rose's Secret" will appear limited to the collaboration period.


Regarding the characters and Memorias appearing from "DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation" in this collaboration, they will not be added to the permanent pool of newly held Wishes after the collaboration ends, and future reappearance plans are undecided, so please be aware.


Also, in the "Marie Rose [Twinkle Rose] Debut Commemoration Mission", you can obtain Tanned Skin Marie Rose [Twinkle Rose].

All characters appearing in this collaboration are planned to have Tanned Skin implementations.

▼Tanned Skin Marie Rose [Twinkle Rose]

Like the tanned skin that appeared in "Atelier Summer 2025", if you obtain both the normal skin and tanned skin characters, you can switch between skins in the character screen.

For details, please check the in-game announcements posted on the day.


〇"Atelier Summer 2024 Pickup Wish" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin "Atelier Summer 2024 Pickup Wish".

This Wish features pickup for the four characters that appeared in last year's "Atelier Summer 2024". The ★3 characters you can obtain in this Wish are only the four characters from Atelier Summer 2024.

For details, please check the in-game announcements posted on the day.


〇Collaboration commemoration "Gravure Panel" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin "Gravure Panel" to commemorate the collaboration.

By clearing missions for each panel, you can obtain rewards, and when you complete all panel missions, you can receive additional rewards. Furthermore, character bromides are hidden behind the panels this time, and by completing missions and opening panels, you will be able to see the bromides. The missions for each panel can be completed by progressing through the aforementioned "Activities".

For details, please check the in-game announcements posted on the day.


○"Character Home" feature added

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will add the "Character Home feature" that allows you to have obtained characters appear on the home screen.

You can choose not only character movements and backgrounds, but also BGM, so you can customize your home screen to your liking. Note that homes matching the main story and events can still be set as before.

For details, please check the in-game announcements posted on the day.


From here, we will introduce update content that couldn't be fully conveyed in the live broadcast.


▼Details of update content that couldn't be fully conveyed in the live broadcast

〇"Atelier Resleriana" × "DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation" Mutual Collaboration Mission begins

We will hold mutual missions linking the two titles "Atelier Resleriana" and "DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation". By playing both titles and achieving the conditions set in each title, we will distribute rewards later.

In "Resleriana", among the event missions for event "DOAXVV Collaboration", "Clear 'To the Tropical Venus Islands! 1' 40 times" is the condition for achieving the mutual mission.

For details, please check the in-game announcements posted on the day.


〇"Resna [Awakened Future] & Resna [Rise Above Fate] Revival Wish" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin "Resna [Awakened Future] & Resna [Rise Above Fate] Revival Wish" featuring two costume versions of Resna.

The Wish format is the same as LEGEND FES during regular event periods. Since new costumes for Resna will also appear in the collaboration, we are reviving past Resnas on this occasion.


○"Atelier Summer 2024 Wishlist Wish" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin "Atelier Summer 2024 Wishlist Wish".

This is slightly different from the aforementioned "Atelier Summer 2024 Pickup Wish", and is a step-up Wish where you can execute STEPs 1-4 up to 4 cycles.

STEPs 1-3 are the same as regular Wishes, where you can obtain characters and Memorias shown in the rates by probability. For the characters and Memorias that appear, please check the rates.

In STEP 4, you can select and guaranteed obtain one character of your choice from the four characters that appeared in last year's "Atelier Summer 2024".


○Event "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament Venus Festival Opening" begins

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will begin event "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament Venus Festival Opening".

Compared to each attribute's "Roman Grand Fighting Tournament", the Lodestar Gems obtainable through event missions have been increased by 600, so please aim to clear the missions.


○"DOAXVV" Collaboration Commemoration Pack on sale

To commemorate the collaboration, we will sell the great value "DOAXVV" Collaboration Commemoration Pack that includes Wish Tickets×20 along with Motion that can be used in the aforementioned "Character Home feature".

Starting after the September 29 maintenance, we will sell "Marie Rose's Motion-included DOAXVV Collaboration Commemoration Pack", so please check it out.

We plan to sell three additional packs during the event period to match the newly appearing Wishes, so please look forward to those as well.


This concludes the content we will be introducing this time.


For the first collaboration event in "Resleriana", we have prepared abundant content so that not only those who play "Resleriana" but also "DOAXVV" owners who start playing "Resleriana" through this collaboration will be satisfied. Please enjoy it.

We ask for your continued support of "Atelier Resleriana".
