Marie Rose Profile

A "little" devil servant who serves the leader of the conglomerate "DOATEC".

She accompanied her leader on vacation and visited the Venus Islands. However, being treated like a child became the catalyst for her to strive to become number one at the "Venus Festival" to get recognition as an adult.

She has a slightly aloof personality, but when she's happy, her emotions show on the surface, revealing age-appropriate sides of her character.
