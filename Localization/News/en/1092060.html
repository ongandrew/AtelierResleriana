Activities

In "Activities," you can select specific characters, choose activities for parameters you want to increase, and present items to raise event-exclusive parameters. The characters you can give presents to are determined per event, and in this event, you can give presents to Marie Rose, Honoka, Resna, and Alvina.

When you present items, EXP accumulates, and when you gain a certain amount of EXP, parameters increase. When parameters increase to a certain level, you can receive rewards and also watch characters challenging activities.

In this event, synthesis items that can be presented are determined for each activity, common to all characters. Recipes for synthesis items you can present can be inspired in the score battles of the "DOAXVV Collaboration" Part 1 event.

※Synthesis items presented to characters through "Present" will disappear.

※For Part 1 of the "DOAXVV Collaboration" event, please see here.

Gravure Panels

In "Gravure Panels," by clearing panel-format missions, the cleared panels disappear and you can view hidden bromides. Each mission in gravure panels can be achieved by raising parameters in activities.

In this event, there are gravure panels for Marie Rose, Honoka, Resna, and Alvina, and by clearing all missions, you can view each character's bromide.

Also, clearing missions can earn you Mana Fragments and Chocolate Cookies! Additionally, clearing all missions earns you Plain Cookies.

※For gravure panel details, please check Event > DOAXVV Collaboration > Gravure Panels.
