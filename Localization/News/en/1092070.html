Event "DOAXVV Collaboration" Part 1 Starting!

Collaboration event "DOAXVV Collaboration" Part 1 with 『DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation』 is starting!

Enjoy the event where Marie Rose, Honoka, Resna, and Alvina play active roles!

This event is divided into Part 1, Mid Part, Part 2, and Final Part. Mid Part, Part 2, and Final Part are planned to be added at a later date.

Also, this event introduces the new feature "Gravure Panels"!

By clearing missions, you can view bromides of Marie Rose and others.

Furthermore, you can challenge "Score Battles" where you can obtain event-exclusive synthesis materials and recipes, as well as high-difficulty battles "Boss Challenge" and "SP Challenge".

"Score Battle" can be challenged by using stamina. When you clear score battles, you can obtain battle coins in addition to synthesis materials. Battle coins can be exchanged at the exchange shop for Grand Sacred Orbs, Lodestar Gems, Hope Grains, and more.

Clearing Boss Challenge earns you event-exclusive emblems.

Clearing SP Challenge earns you Stellar Prayer Stone Seeds.

Additionally, when challenging Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, Marie Rose [Twinkle Rose] and Gou [Faithful Fighter], as well as Atelier Summer 2024 and characters newly introduced in Atelier Summer 2025 Wishes will be powered up!

※Some missions in Gravure Panels will become clearable when activities are added at a later date.

※For details on character power-ups in Boss Challenge and SP Challenge, please check Event > DOAXVV Collaboration > Boss Challenge or SP Challenge > Battle Info.

※Event stories for Mid Part, Part 2, and Final Part are planned to be added at a later date.

※For "DOAXVV Collaboration" event activities, please see here.

Gravure Panels

In "Gravure Panels," by clearing panel-format missions, the cleared panels disappear and you can view hidden bromides.

In this event, there are gravure panels for Marie Rose, Honoka, Resna, and Alvina, and by clearing all missions, you can view each character's bromide.

Also, clearing missions can earn you Mana Fragments and Chocolate Cookies! Additionally, clearing all missions earns you Plain Cookies.

※For gravure panel details, please check Event > DOAXVV Collaboration > Gravure Panels.

SP Challenge

SP Challenge is a high-difficulty battle that surpasses Boss Challenge.

Rewards can be obtained based on the score rank when clearing quests.

Please try challenging these challenging battles!

※"Rental Party" cannot be used in SP Challenge.

Rental Party

Event Boss Challenge can be challenged using "Rental Party" with specific characters and Memoria pre-equipped.

You can freely equip synthesis-created equipment items and battle items to Rental Party.

Try synthesizing items that match the attribute specialties and roles of characters in Rental Party to challenge Boss Challenge!

※Clear rewards can be obtained even when clearing using Rental Party.

※Characters and Memoria equipped in Rental Party cannot be changed. Also, equipped characters and Memoria differ by event.

Memoria Bonus

When clearing quests, if you possess bonus target Memoria, synthesis material drop amounts increase!

Target Memoria and bonus amounts can be checked from Event > DOAXVV Collaboration > Score Battle > Memoria Bonus.

※Bonuses are added up to a maximum of 200%. Bonus amounts increase based on the unlock status of target Memoria.

※Bonuses apply just by possessing target Memoria. They don't need to be equipped in your party.

※When possessing multiple copies of the same Memoria, unlock ranks are combined to increase bonus amounts (maximum V)

Example: If you possess 2 copies of target Memoria at unlock rank I, it's calculated as 1 copy of unlock rank II Memoria.
