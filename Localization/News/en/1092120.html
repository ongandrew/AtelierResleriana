LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES exchange shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab, and can also be used to purchase Wish Ticket Sets at Shop > Packs > Wish Ticket tab.

※Characters newly introduced after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are newly introduced pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Single Wish: 1 medal

●10 Wishes: 11 medals

※You cannot obtain LEGEND Medals with Wish Tickets.
