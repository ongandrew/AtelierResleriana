Wish Overview

"Resna [Awakened Future] & Resna [Rise Above Fate] Revival Wish [Paid Only]" is starting!

★3 characters Resna [Awakened Future] and Resna [Rise Above Fate] are featured!

This Wish is a step-up Wish from STEP 1~5, with 2x pickup rate at STEP 3, and furthermore 3x pickup rate at STEP 5!

Additionally, when you make Wishes through STEP 1~5, an additional "Bonus Wish" appears!

The Bonus Wish has 2x pickup rate!

Furthermore, make 10 Wishes and get Resna [Awakened Future] Pieces ×50 and Resna [Rise Above Fate] Pieces ×50 as a bonus!

●STEP 1~5 Step-up Wish

STEP 1

10 Wishes for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 2

10 Wishes for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 3

2x pickup rate!

10 Wishes for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 4

10 Wishes for 1,500 Paid Lodestar Gems

STEP 5

3x pickup rate!

10 Wishes for 2,000 Paid Lodestar Gems

●Bonus Wish

2x pickup rate!

10 Wishes for 3,000 Paid Lodestar Gems

Bonus: Resna [Awakened Future] Pieces ×50 and Resna [Rise Above Fate] Pieces ×50!

※The step-up Wish can be executed up to 1 cycle maximum from STEP 1~5.

※The Bonus Wish can be executed up to 5 times maximum.

※During the event period, if you welcome pickup characters you already have, you will receive Character Pieces ×100 and 50 Wish Radiant Stones.

LEGEND Medals・LEGEND FES Exchange Shop

You can obtain LEGEND Medals when making Wishes using Lodestar Gems.

LEGEND Medals can be exchanged for ★3 characters and SSR Memoria at the LEGEND FES exchange shop or Shop > Character Memoria > LEGEND Medal tab.

※Characters newly introduced after 2025/03/06 can be welcomed through LEGEND Medal exchange for a limited time during the Wish event period. ★3 characters that appeared before that can be exchanged indefinitely.

※SSR Memoria that can be exchanged are newly introduced pickup SSR Memoria from the current LEGEND FES and SSR Memoria that are Memoria Bonus targets.

※LEGEND Medals have no expiration date.

※The number of LEGEND Medals obtained is as follows:

●Step-up Wish STEP 1~4: 8 medals

●Step-up Wish STEP 5: 10 medals

●Bonus Wish: 16 medals

Important Notes

●This Wish is limited to Paid Lodestar Gems only, so it cannot be made using free Lodestar Gems or Wish Tickets.

●Please check drop rates from Wish > Drop Rates/Wish Details > Drop Rates.

●Once you complete 1 full cycle of the step-up Wish from STEP 1~5, it will be hidden from the Wish list, and the Bonus Wish will appear where the step-up Wish was displayed.

●Once you complete all 5 Bonus Wishes, it will be hidden from the Wish list.

●If you welcome a character you already have, they will automatically be converted to character pieces and Wish Radiant Stones.
